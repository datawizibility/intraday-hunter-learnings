- Breakouts failed → BN sold → **booked** into expiry (premium crush risk on green candles).

### Process lessons
- Morning trades need pre-defined loss/profit handling — overtraders should reduce size or skip open.
- “One breakout then fail” trap: after first breakout of round number, second breakout less required for trap thesis.
- Expiry → book sooner; green candles can crush put premium fast.

### Keep permanently
1. Positive companion indices + BN holding round number → soft open often = **sell the buyers**.
2. Pre-define loss if breakout continues against you before entering open.
3. On expiry, prioritize booking over hoping for second leg.
