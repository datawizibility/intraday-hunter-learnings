### Process lessons
- After sellers flushed by support-bounce momentum: **flat / GD → sell with**; **gap-up → buy with / target sellers**. Flat can’t sustainably cross RN (only buyers after) → down more likely.
- Flat open: allow small **60–70 pt** breakout then entry, or sell if falls first; prefer **no breakout**; ~160-style overshoot can make entry wrong.
- CP–RN chop (green/red flicker) is OK while net sell thesis holds.
- Upper-area put entry → room for **expanded target**; straight dump from open → often only coverage/average target.
- Nifty sell + retrace close → flat/GD follow sell; direct up = only buyers; good gap = different plan.
- Temporary SN/Nifty + against puts is tolerable while BN holds map and breakout hasn’t printed; expect multi-index fall together.
- Support pause activates buyers → next sell leg = **book** zone — don’t fear the pause; direct drop without pause is even better.
- Book **before round number** while strong 3-index momentum continues — don’t get greedy into RN with everyone else.
- Small-momentum / sideways pre-context raises chop risk — still follow open-type branch; size carefully.

### Keep permanently

2. Flat open: small 60–70 pt breakout can be allowed then sell; prefer **no breakout**; overshoot (e.g. ~160) can invalidate the timed entry.
