### What happened / what he did
- Opened with a **solid gap-up**, then light rejection / chop.
- Read: multi-day buying + gap-up → only **light** Friday-buyer profit booking, not deep selling from longer-term longs (big selling would be faster/larger).
- Matched pre plan: **gap-up → don’t target buyers; buy with market**. Added **calls** (BN ~1170 qty, Nifty ~1365 qty).
- Early small mark-to-market loss while thesis was “pull out weak Friday buyers, then upside.”
- Wanted **continuous** upside without pause — if market stalls, late buyers get filled and edge dies.
- BN broke out; **Sensex/Nifty lagged**. Took a normal BN target (~720–740 pts) and **booked** once path beyond known setup was unclear.
- Exit rule: wait only as far as the known path; after that, book and wait for a **new opportunity** (continuation vs turn/sideways vs Nifty–Sensex psychology).

### Process lessons
- Gap-up after multi-day buying: distinguish **shallow Friday booking** vs **real rejection** from deep longs.
- Don’t hunt buyers on a good gap-up; ride with the open.
- Continuous move without pause > stalled move that invites fresh buyers against you.
- When only one index leads and others lag, take the known target — don’t invent a path you don’t have.

### Keep permanently
1. Gap-up + multi-day buyers: light open rejection often = Friday booking, not thesis kill.
2. Edge needs **continuous** momentum; stall = late buyers get in and your trade can reverse.
3. Book when you leave the **mapped** path (especially single-index lead); wait for a fresh setup.
