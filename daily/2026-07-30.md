### What happened / what he did
- Almost **flat to slight gap-down**; Sensex/Nifty mildly positive, BN selling.
- Sold puts across indices (~1170 BN, ~900 Sensex, ~1365 Nifty) to target **multi-day buyers**.
- Trade went to profit quickly — easier to manage than a losing open.
- Noted traps form via **breakout OR fast momentum** (greed). Expected sell continuation without needing a fresh breakout.
- **Booked** after solid move: risk of sideways (like prior day), BN already had a decent fall, Sensex expiry = one-way continuation not guaranteed.

### Process lessons
- After a multi-day bid, soft open = harvest buyers; don’t wait for perfect confirmation forever.
- Profit-running trades are psychologically easier — still pre-define exit.
- After a quiet-profit day, take the smooth day when it comes rather than forcing extension.

### Keep permanently
1. Multi-day buyers + soft open → sell-the-buyers is the default map.
2. Avoid runaway gap-up chases even when you wanted to sell elsewhere.
3. Expiry + already-captured momentum → book; don’t gift back P&L to a sideways afternoon.
