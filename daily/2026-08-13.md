**Entry**

- Puts: BN **57,600** + **57,500**; Sensex qty **900** (**expiry day**); Nifty qty **1430**.

**Why put (thesis)**
- Prior: good selling then **exact ~500 support** bounce flushed sellers on retrace → sellers gone → direct upside odds low (unless big GU builds strength / different traps).
- Flat (or GD even better) → sell plan. Follow the already-visible sell direction.
- **BN:** prior positive + support take into close → not sellers sitting; **buyers** → target them with puts on soft open. Buyer danger drops if market runs **above 58k** or gaps up hard — then don’t hunt; treat as trap map.
- **Nifty:** good sell then recovery flushed sellers (esp. watching BN); few sellers left; some buyers possible → follow the sell momentum on flat/GD.
- Open dump gave the put entry; light hold after first sell = comfortable entry (vs needing retrace into a huge dump).

**Invalidation / mid**
- Market should not go up — direct fall helps; upside = problem.
- Upside odds low; after breakdown maybe some retrace later. Expect continuous selling. Sensex expiry → keep targets sensible if momentum good.

**Psychology (near target)**
- Profit rising toward target → fear “should I book / will it reverse?” is normal.
- Waiting to cut at the **decided place** builds confidence; cutting early in profit becomes habit → small profits booked, big losses waited → traders stay unprofitable.
- Fear OK — **don’t convert fear into early exit action**. In **loss**, cut at limit; in **profit**, wait the decided target (unless over-greed).

**Near target → reverse → wait → partial book**
- Almost at target then market **reversed** — simple trade spoiled time-wise; still expect lower eventually but time damaged; loss flick then recover toward profit again.
- Breakdown of ~500 would deliver fuller target; momentum weak / half of earlier profit → wait.
- Booked **some profit**: don’t want good profit → **zero-zero**; momentum not continuing; chop up/down; Sensex expiry can mute momentum; time already long.
- If momentum had continued → expand target; second flush of remaining holders can reverse hard later → zero-zero worse than partial profit.

**Recap**
- Puts because BN buyers after positive+support; SN/Nifty sellers flushed by RN-support bounce → can’t go direct up → flat/GD sell-with. Opportunity matched plan but momentum weak + expiry + time → **partial profit book**. **PROFIT (partial / not full target)**.

### Process lessons
- Exact RN support bounce after sell → **sellers flushed** → flat/GD sell-with; GU buy-with (buyer SLs below RN far).

- Near decided target: **fear is OK**; don’t convert to early exit; cutting early in profit becomes a losing habit.
- In loss → cut at **limit**; in profit → wait decided target (unless over-greed).
- After near-target reverse + weak momentum / expiry / long time: prefer **partial book** over holding to **zero-zero**.
