### Keep permanently

2. Prior-session positioning matters. A rejected gap-up with no likely overnight buyers can leave sellers as the more useful group to target in a recovery.
3. Cross-index divergence can provide information: Bank Nifty holding while Sensex and Nifty were still selling strengthened the possibility of recovery.
4. A thesis can remain valid through an initial mark-to-market loss, but only when observable structure supports it—not merely because one hopes the loss will reverse.
5. Time remaining is part of risk management. Once a good profit exists late in the trade, do not expose it to a retracement merely to reach a round-number profit target.
6. Distinguish an aspirational P&L figure from realised P&L; record only what the trader explicitly confirms as booked.
