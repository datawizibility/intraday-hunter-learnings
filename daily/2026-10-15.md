### Keep permanently
1. **The higher-point cross is a gate, not a detail.** On a two-sided/range day, the pre gives *two* plans and the **only** thing that decides between them is whether the market **crosses its previous higher point**. IH literally waited at the bell — *"we will not buy or sell right now… let the market take a direction in net"* — and only bought after the crossing printed. Write the gate down before the open, then obey it.
2. **A "two-sided / no inventory" chart is traded on the side that is moving, not on a forecast.** *"Neither the seller's SL is available here, nor the buyer's SL"* — so the decision falls to the **live direction**: it tried positive ⇒ walk with it. This is the same SL-inventory test as 14 and 16 Oct, applied when **both** pools are empty.
3. **A trap for the buyers is bullish, once direction resumes.** *"It made a trap for the buyers and then started rising again."* The flush removes the crowded side and leaves nobody to sell into the move — the entry window is the resumption, not the flush.
4. **Weak rejection = sellers not activated.** *"The rejection came but the rejection is not big, so the sellers would not have got activated."* A rejection that does not grow does not seat a tradable pool — which is why the pre's sell branch was only conditional and was abandoned when the gate failed.
5. **A range-bound laggard is acceptable inside a basket.** Stated *before* entry: *"Bank Nifty may not give much momentum… it will stay stuck inside a range — but in net there is benefit in the call here, because it will definitely test the higher point once."* The requirement is not that all three run, only that the **laggard does not break** and the leaders pay.
6. **In an established range, "the higher point will be tested" is a legitimate intraday target.** The target came out of the **range geometry** (the prior session's high / the higher point), not a round number.
7. **Exit ahead of expiry mechanics, not at the number.** He left the profit because Sensex expiry could produce *"breakout then retracement"*. Expiry-day exits are timing decisions about **flow**, not target decisions.
8. **A small loss immediately after a rule-based entry is budgeted, not a warning.** *"Some loss will show — Sensex and Nifty can give retracement… but it will not happen that our loss limit gets cut."* The limit is the only line.
