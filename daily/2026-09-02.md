### Keep permanently
1. Before entry, define not only the trade you can take but also the trade you cannot take; this prevents impulsive direction changes.
2. Do not change bias merely because price temporarily moves against the entry. Wait within a predefined loss limit—but exit when the invalidation, time condition, or loss ceiling is reached.
3. A trade thesis has a time expectation. If momentum expected within 5–10 minutes still has not appeared after the deadline, the delay itself is evidence against the setup.
4. Never cut a planned put trade and immediately buy calls only because the market moved up; repeated reactive reversals destroy net expectancy.
5. **Do not fight when wrong; make good profit when right.** Discipline across many trades matters more than rescuing one trade.
