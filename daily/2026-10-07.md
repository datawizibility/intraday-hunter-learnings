### Keep permanently
1. **A one-minute reversal of a multi-hour move = sideways day, not a trend day.** When yesterday's 3–4 hours of momentum is converted into rejection in ~1 minute at the open, the market is *too volatile to trend* — it will neither sell much nor buy much. The trade becomes a **follow-the-direction-you-were-handed** trade, not a directional bet.
2. **"Buyer/Seller game" vs "direction game."** When **neither side has an SL available** (buyers already booked on yesterday's momentum; sellers were never there because the market gave positive momentum directly), there is **no inventory to hunt** ⇒ no direction ⇒ sideways revolve. In that state, **follow the tiny edge** (here: we were handed the market lower ⇒ follow positive momentum ⇒ buy). IH's own framing: *"with a retracement I would have sold; because I got it lower, I bought."*
3. **Volatility ≠ opportunity.** Extreme volatility is read as *range*, not as a big move. Size/expectation must be set to a **normal target**, because a sideways market caps the outcome.
4. **The "small breakdown" test.** On a supposed sideways day, if selling runs *more than a genuine range would show* and the market makes a **definite small breakdown**, the sideways thesis is wrong — pause, define the point at which you take the loss, and **book the loss as per limit** rather than sit and hope.
5. **Discipline over daily P&L.** *"If you follow discipline and don't make money today, you make it tomorrow — or in the next trade. If you break discipline you may make money today, but not the next day."* Stay inside the loss limit and **stay in the trade while the limit holds** — no storytelling either way.
6. **A trader is made by accuracy + mindset + discipline, not by size of P&L.** Don't compare with other traders' numbers or "levels"; measure your own capital and your own risk. (Reinforces Playbook §"Size & risk adjustments".)
7. **Exit attribution: book when the profit is single-index.** The trade only worked because **Bank Nifty** moved; with **Sensex and Nifty sideways**, a bigger target could not form ⇒ book the **normal** target. Per Bible §6: *"only BN works, companions lag ⇒ take the known target, don't invent a path."*
8. **Don't be greedy after a good momentum capture.** *"We got a chance to capture a good momentum, then we should not be too greedy; cut the trade and leave."* The residual move he *might* have missed was likely a **seller-SL flush above the closing price** — real, but not yours to bank twice.
