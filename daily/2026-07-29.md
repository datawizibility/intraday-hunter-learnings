## Post-market (live)
**Video:** Live Bank Nifty Option Trading  
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e9qDdFfOVyk  
**Duration:** ~5:53

### What happened / what he did
- **Gap-up** (Sensex/Nifty even larger) + mild rejection.
- Because inventory unclear, **followed ongoing cover** → **bought** (calls) rather than shorting the gap.
- Risk: oversized gap-up can suck in FOMO buyers then flip fully negative — watched for **fast rejection**.
- BN lagged initially then joined upside; average target reached → **booked** as time passed and momentum stayed slow/sideways.
- Explicit: no clear buyer/seller SL pools → follow market, don’t invent a counter-trade.

### Process lessons
- Confusion days → trade **with** the existing recovery, not against it.
- Large gap-up increases trap risk even on longs — don’t overstay.
- Average target + slow tape + lots of time = book.

### Keep permanently
1. If you can’t identify inventory, default to **following the active regime**.
2. Gap size changes risk even when direction bias is buy.
3. Sideways after decent profit → exit; don’t wait for a “big” day that isn’t coming.
