## Post-market (live)
**Video:** Live Bank Nifty Option Trading  
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpK2h9xsrXk  
**Duration:** ~9:01

### What happened / what he did
- Pre-open looked **flat**; planned **positive / buy** if open momentum confirms.
- Risk called: sudden **large selling** at open implies market wants to stay in range → bad for longs.
- Opened with positive momentum → bought calls (Sensex ~840, Nifty ~1365; BN size implied in flow).
- Thesis: prior breakout attracted buyers who got rejected/flushed; then slow cover → **don’t target those buyers**; flat/small gap-up → **go with upside**.
- Also discussed: market doesn’t hunt *your* SL personally — it hunts **clustered** retail SLs.
- Trade went to loss as rejection matched the prior cover; held while thesis alive, then **cut** when continuous selling + Sensex/Nifty joined downside and **loss limit** hit.
- Post-mortem: breakout-then-flush buyers thesis failed when market sold straight through — no point “thinking” a wrong trade.

### Process lessons
- Wait for first open momentum when range is messy; don’t force before tape speaks.
- Pre-calculate risk; exit at limit without storytelling.
- SL-cluster psychology ≠ “market is out to get me.”
- Wrong trade + accelerating sell = cut; don’t average hope.

### Keep permanently
1. Range days: large open sell = range warning; small pullback + breakout attempt can still be buy.
2. Flush-then-cover maps need the market to **hold**; if it sells through your limit, exit.
3. Personalize risk math, not personalize “SL hunting.”
