### Keep permanently
1. **After a very large one-way move, the upper side is no longer worth targeting** — those sellers have booked and hold little quantity; the trade is **continuation**, not a counter-hunt.
2. **Wait out the expiry volatility — but don't wait out a trending structure.** Fast momentum is not tradeable; once the direction is clear and continuing, enter, but size/booking must respect the expiry.
3. **Random sellers → flush → big retracement.** When late/random sellers pile in and the market takes them out, expect an outsized retracement; book the target **while continue-momentum is intact** rather than sitting through the flush.
4. **Don't regret an early-as-possible booked target.** When you sit through a turn, two candles can cut the profit in half — a booked target at the mapped level is the completed trade.
