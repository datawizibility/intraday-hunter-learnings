## Setups / process shown
1. **Choose your game first:** investing vs intraday vs positional. Clarity before charts.
2. **Root value (रूट वैल्यू):** infer what the crowd that already traded is likely holding (buyer vs seller inventory), then plan for gap open.
3. **Gap playbook from this week:**
   - After sharp selling + sudden recovery → sellers may be trapped/sitting. Flat / small gap-down helps them; **large gap-up** often *tempts* put sellers to average → he looks for **intraday sell** on that temptation (not blindly shorting buyers).
   - When sellers keep getting stopped and Nifty/Sensex stay strong → buyers may be sitting → **flat / gap-down** day: look to **target buyers** (sell setups).
   - **Large gap-down** after buyers sitting: usually **don't** target buyers; follow the market with **buy** setups instead.
   - When direction unclear / mixed inventory → **follow what's already happening** (recovery/positive = buy bias) rather than inventing a counter-trade.
4. **Multi-index rule:** if only one index gaps hard while others don't align, temptation/trap setups appear; if all three go together, one-sided chase is harder.
5. One loss called out: early **range breakout buy** on Bank Nifty hit SL (he frames it as “SL hunting his trade”), but **direction still correct** — Nifty/Sensex stayed positive.

## Risk / sizing / RR
- Prefers **1:1 risk-reward** in **option buying** because time decay makes wide targets + tiny SLs unrealistic.
- Tradeoff he teaches: raise **accuracy** via root reading so 1:1 works; OR raise RR (1:2, 1:3) if accuracy is weak — but RR expansion is hard in options.
- Rejects fantasy: “10-point SL, 100-point target” in option buying.
- Acceptable example: ~50 SL / 100 target if process supports it — not extreme RR fantasies.
- Scale: smaller size on first entry; add confidence with 2nd/3rd entry if direction thesis holds.

## Psychology / mindset
- Sudden buying after selling = retail joy → buyers often exit; don't assume leftover buyers will hold.
- Market **tempts** losers to average (especially after small gap against them) then reverses — he trades that temptation.
- Fast candles near round numbers pull in reluctant buyers; later sideways can flush them — mindset shift matters more than indicators.
- Process over P&L day: if situation is understood, missing a day/week/month of “edge feeling” is fine; when it clicks, consistency compounds.

## Keep permanently (playbook candidates)
1. **Segment clarity first** (intraday vs positional vs investing) before applying any setup.
2. **Root = buyer/seller inventory + open-type plan** (flat / gap-up / gap-down branches), not single-indicator signals.
3. **Options: prioritize accuracy + sensible RR (often ~1:1)** over heroic RR with tiny stops.
4. **Direction > perfect first entry** — allow re-entries with controlled size.
5. **Cross-check Nifty / Sensex / Bank Nifty** before acting on one index’s story.
