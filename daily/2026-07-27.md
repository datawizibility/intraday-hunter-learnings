### What happened / what he did
- **Gap-up** open (Sensex especially large; Nifty also up).
- Thesis: after continuous selling + recovery into holiday, holders likely **booked** → few seller SLs left → gap-up often brings **buyer temptation** then **rejection**.
- **Sold** Bank Nifty / Sensex / Nifty (sizes mentioned ~1170 BN, ~900 Sensex, ~1430 Nifty).
- Expected: up → down → retest → direction; captured the sell momentum.
- Bank Nifty sold well; Sensex/Nifty lagged → he wanted them to break down for a bigger target.
- **Booked** when BN printed small candles near round number and Sensex/Nifty failed to confirm breakdown — risk rising that trapped sellers get squeezed.

### Process lessons
- Holiday + recovery → don’t assume short inventory still sitting.
- Gap-up without instant runaway momentum → trap risk favors sell of late buyers.
- If one index works alone, don’t overhold waiting for confirmation forever.
- Exit when structure turns choppy even if direction thesis was right.

### Keep permanently
1. Inventory after holiday/recovery often clears — gap-up can be a **temptation trap**, not a free long.
2. Multi-index confirmation matters for extending targets.
3. Book when candles shrink and round-number trap risk rises.
