### Process lessons
- Gap-down after sell day: bounce ≠ automatic short-cover cascade if inventory is confident or thin.
- Prefer intraday target when structure already printed a lower point (not yesterday’s continuous dump shape).
- Expiry index may lag breakdown — take the index that confirms.

### Keep permanently
1. After strong sell + failed late momentum, overnight short inventory is often **thin or confident** — don’t invent seller-hunt; follow gap-down / sell the bounce.
2. Planned bounce-then-sell is process, not FOMO reverse.
3. Book known intraday path; don’t demand continuous-dump extension when chart already made a lower pivot.
