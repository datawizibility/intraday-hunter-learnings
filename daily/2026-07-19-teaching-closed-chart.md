### Core idea
- Learn **closed charts** first: analysis and next-day plans are built on closed structure; live is execution of that logic.
- Same-looking candles can demand **opposite** open plans depending on whether **buyers actually accumulated** (psychology), not candle color alone.

### Psychology / inventory examples (week review style)
- Positive day + **gap-up**: crowd wants to buy → often hunt that FOMO with **sell** setups; flat/gap-down after weak/no follow-through breakout can be **buy** (crowd confused).
- If breakout had **real momentum** and buyers held → flat **and** gap-up can both become buyer-target days.
- **Huge** gap-down resets structure vs small gap-down — don’t copy the prior recovery plan blindly.
- Day-2 of constructive upside after a prior flush is when buyers **do** accumulate → soft opens can target those buyers; sudden bounce from deep negative often has **few** buyers to hunt.
- Conceptual link (also user refinement): index options are zero-sum-ish → market needs **clustered SLs**; after upside there may be **fewer stops above**, so soft sessions often pressure **yesterday’s buyers** (stops below) — educational framing only, not advice.

### Keep permanently
1. Closed-chart inventory + psychology decide the matrix; identical shapes can invert the plan.
2. Crowd FOMO on gap-up after a green day ≠ automatic long; often the sell side of the temptation.
3. Buyer-hunt after upside applies when buyers **actually sat**; weak/no-momentum breakouts leave little inventory to hunt.
