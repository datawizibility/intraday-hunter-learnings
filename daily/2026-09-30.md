### Keep permanently
1. **Flat is the trade; a gap-up of the same thesis is a no-trade.** When buyers have already been flushed by a retracement, a flat open lets you follow positive momentum precisely *because others won't buy it*; a gap-up would let everyone in and get sold into — so the same thesis flips to SELL-side on a gap-up.
2. **"Where few traders work, the market takes direction."** The cleanest entries are the opens that the crowd is *not* playing; a crowded, obvious entry (gap-up) is where you get trapped.
3. **An intraday trader must not label the market positive or negative.** Read what the chart is offering *at this time*; "positive/negative market" is a positional-trader lens that smuggles in the wrong timeframe.
4. **A single rejection can return a large slice of an unrealised target — take the average target.** High premiums make the give-back bigger; "an option buyer should book as soon as momentum comes."
5. **Handle the profit and the loss symmetrically.** IH's stated rule: had it been an equally large loss instead of a profit, he would have taken the *same* exit action — the management is the same on both sides.
