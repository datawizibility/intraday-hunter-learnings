### Keep permanently
1. Entry at the opening bell carries premium-adjustment risk — a directionally correct trade can still show an abrupt loss in the first minutes; size for that.
2. P&L symmetry: exiting profits early while sitting in losses is what damages the account. Wait for the target up to a defined limit and for the loss up to a defined limit — don't hold either extreme.
3. Flat-to-gap-down openings trap sellers, gap-up openings trap buyers → trade the opposite side's setup rather than the direction of the gap.
4. When two of three indices break out and the third cannot cross its round number, treat the breakout as at risk of converting into failure; don't stretch the target.
5. Small-momentum regimes require small quantity, and small quantity means a normal — not ambitious — target.
6. Discipline beats knowledge: with discipline you can still be net profitable while getting a thing or two wrong; without discipline, knowledge alone does not produce profit.
