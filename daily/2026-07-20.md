### What happened / what he did
- **Huge gap-down** across indices (news-driven) — pre chart plan discarded; wait for **first momentum**.
- Open chop → brief recovery → second candle selling. Waited; then thesis: gap-down + news → **sellers tempt** into puts with nearby SLs → need a **retracement/recovery**, not continuous 1000-pt dump.
- Bought **calls** (Sensex ~900, Nifty ~1430) to capture seller-flush recovery; held through early MTM loss.
- Booked once a solid recovery / average target hit. Explicit: trade was **not** from prior-day buyers sitting — inventory had cleared; live **seller SL** psychology drove the call.

### Process lessons
- Large news gap-down **resets** structure; don’t force yesterday’s inventory map.
- Continuous dump with no pause = possible runaway → take loss; choppy sell after big gap = sellers to flush → recovery trade.
- Entry after SLs form nearby, not into the first panic candle.

### Keep permanently
1. Huge gap-down + news → structure reset; wait first tape, then decide recovery vs runaway.
2. After big gap-down, clustered **seller** SLs often need a flush → recovery can be the trade (not “buy yesterday’s buyers”).
3. If momentum is continuous one-way with no retrace, treat as runaway and respect the loss path.
