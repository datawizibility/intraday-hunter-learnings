### What happened / what he did
- **Call side** trade (500 call, 600 call same qty) + Sensex 900 (expiry), Nifty 1430.
- Gap-up open after long selling = temptation for sellers → keeps buyers out → then positive momentum should come.
- He noted: if you trade at open you get better benefit than later; but risk is bigger if momentum opposes (5-10 min can kill the trade). Discipline required.
- Option writers active: market flushed put writers yesterday; now may run call writers → buy side.

### Process lessons
- After put writers are flushed, market often targets call writers → buy.
- Trading at open gives better benefit but demands discipline; slower momentum around 10-11 AM.
- If you can't follow discipline, don't take the risk.

### Keep permanently
1. Option-writer flushing sequence: who gets flushed first determines next target (put writers flushed → call side works).
2. Open trades pay better but punish undisciplined traders fast (5-10 min).
