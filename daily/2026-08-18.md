### Process lessons
- Recovery after breakdown → most retail sells into strength → market traps them → buy side works.
- Don't target good buyers (in profit); target the trapped crowd.
- Huge gap above first resistance = no clean plan.

### Keep permanently
1. After a breakdown-then-recovery, the breakout crowd is trapped → follow market (buy).
2. Don't fight good/profitable traders; trade against trapped inventory.
3. No plan when gap opens far beyond first resistance.
