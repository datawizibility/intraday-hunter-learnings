### Process lessons
- Day-2 sellers after a prior sell → flat/gap-up **buy** them; gap-down can flip to follow sell if shorts already profitable.
- Prefer dip/retracement entry on light gap-up buys; confirm dip isn’t sudden large selling.
- When hunting sellers with tight stops: expect **fast** upside, not a long grind — don’t demand huge targets.
- Book decent profit without greed once the mapped fast move pays.
- Options psychology: don’t bet 100% on one trade; cut mistakes in loss; small process slips are more tolerable while in profit.

### Keep permanently
1. **Day-1** sell after positive/gap-up → thin sellers → don’t hunt; **day-2** sell → sellers sit → flat/gap-up **buy** them.
2. Hunting sellers with tight SLs → expect **sharp/fast** upside; **don’t** expect huge momentum.
3. Light gap-up buy: prefer **slight dip / retracement** entry; skip if the dip is sudden large selling.
4. When mapped fast move pays → **book** decent profit; don’t over-greed.
