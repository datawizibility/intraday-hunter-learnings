### Teaching (key concepts)
1. **RR vs Accuracy** — the two levers:
   - PATTERN setups → depend on HIGH RR (1:2 / 1:3 reward). Accuracy less critical — accept more losing trades, win big on the right ones. Manage RR high, as much as you can capture.
   - PRICE ACTION setups → focus on ACCURACY first. RR can be managed gradually (targets can be widened later).
2. **Always set Target + SL** on every trade — entry may be right or wrong, but risk is controlled.
3. Common mistake: traders with a WORKING setup chase accuracy in pattern setups (where it doesn't apply) — this breaks the edge.
