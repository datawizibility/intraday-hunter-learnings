### Keep permanently
1. **A flat open on a positive-momentum chart is a buyer's session — but the entry is the RECOVERY, not the fall.** *"The rejection was a bit sharp, so we waited — but now a recovery has started… so here we start building the call trade."* Do not buy into the opening rejection; wait for strength to appear.
2. **The trap leg has no measurable depth — so it is not tradable.** *"When it was falling we could not understand how much the market would fall, because the SLs were not available there. The market was directly making a trap."* If there is no counter-side SL cluster to measure against, the size of the flush is unknowable ⇒ **no trade while it falls; trade only the recovery.**
3. **Small breakdowns are a psychology tool, not a trend change.** *"The market does small breakdowns so that the psychology can change a bit — so that it cannot directly take a buy trade."* ⇒ Expect the flush; let it happen; don't be flipped by it.
4. **Sell only when buyer stops are available; otherwise follow the momentum (the side rule, stated from the sell side).** *"There is no benefit in selling here. Selling would pay when buyers' SLs are available. Otherwise you must try to follow the momentum that is happening inside the market."* Combined with 16 Oct's one-breakdown rule this is the corpus's durable side-selector: **trade the side where the counter-side stops are — and if no one is trapped, follow the running momentum.**
5. **A round-number breakdown that immediately recovers is trap confirmation, not a trend change.** *"The round number broke down here [BN], and in Nifty too the round number broke down — the moment the breakdown happened the market immediately gave recovery. Sensex exactly took its own support."* Read this as: the level was defended, the psychology got cleaned out, the direction resumes.
6. **Don't let a big profit shrink — book into continuing momentum.** *"You do not have to let a big profit shrink… after seeing a good profit, if you go away having reduced a big profit, what was the benefit to you?"* Once *"we have seen a tremendous profit"*, a **normal target is a waste** — take the good target and exit when profit starts reducing.
7. **The holiday vacuum pays one day early.** *"Tomorrow is a holiday — it is possible we get everything today; let's cover tomorrow's profit today."* With a closed session next, momentum moves appear (and are paid) in the *pre-holiday* session — the operator has the day to itself because most traders will not hold. Also: *"as long as others do not buy, we do not need to book our target."*
8. **A laggard/compressed index (Bank Nifty here) is still traded — the basket is judged, not the laggard.** BN's chart was the *"confusion"* chart at the pre and still got the 55,400 CE leg.
