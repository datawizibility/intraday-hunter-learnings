### Keep permanently
1. **A seated positional side is not a trap side — it is the side you join, until their SL level breaks.** *"जब तक 500 का लेवल मार्केट क्रॉस नहीं करता इन बायर को हम टारगेट अपना नहीं बना सकते"* … *"जब तक इस प्रकार के सपोर्ट वगैरह मार्केट के अंदर ना टूटे"*. This is the same "inventory" law the corpus already has, applied in the bullish direction: **target the trapped side only when their level is under attack; otherwise ride with them.** Recorded as the bull-side twin of the existing bear-side rule.
2. **A flat open with an immediate rejection is a weaker buy signal than a gap-up — and IH says exactly why.** *"गैप अप को देखकर दूसरे लोग भी बाय करना शुरू कर देते हैं … लेकिन फ्लैट वगैरह ओपन हुआ है, ओपन होते ही थोड़ा बहुत रिजेक्शन भी मार्केट ने दे दिया है। तो ऐसे में डायरेक्टली दूसरे लोग तो यहां पे बाय वगैरह नहीं करने वाले।"* ⇒ **flat+rejection ⇒ expect a narrower, achievable-only-if-cheap move; gap-up ⇒ expect chasers but a sharper failure risk.** This is the bull-side counterpart of the corpus's gap-down nuance and is the day's most transferable insight.
3. **In a sideways tape the target is not a destination, so a "decent profit" IS the exit rule.** *"अगर हमें ठीक-ठाक प्रॉफिट नजर आ रहा है तो अभी निकल जाते हैं"* … *"बार-बार टारगेट से कम होना … वो चीज देखने से अच्छा है कि अपना प्रॉफिट वगैरह बुक करके चले जाते हैं क्योंकि डायरेक्शनल मोमेंटम यहां पे नहीं होने वाला।"* Compare 28 Oct, where he said the opposite in words (*"अवरेज टारगेट … फिक्स है"*) and gave back a seen profit; today the manager learned the lesson and booked. **The rule: fix the target in advance, then re-price the target when the tape type changes (trend → sideways).**
4. **Time decay changes psychology — so the loss window is a time window, not just a points window.** *"ऐसा लॉस अगर आपको दो-ती घंटे के बाद किसी ट्रेड में नजर आ रहा है तो फिर उस ट्रेड में बैठकर देखने का कोई फायदा नहीं"* … *"अगर ये बहुत ज्यादा टाइम हो जाए, धीरे-धीरे प्रीमियम कम हो जाए, फिर साइकोलॉजी भी चेंज हो जाता है।"* A **fresh** trade inside its loss limit can be held (premium hasn't bled, decision is intact); an **old** trade sitting in loss cannot, because time has already spent the option's money. This is the sentence that justifies 28 Oct's "cut at the limit" vs today's "hold through the dip" — same rule, different ages of trade.
5. **Entry precision is the whole edge in a sideways market.** *"साइडवेज मार्केट में या तो हमें बहुत सही जगह पे एंट्री मिल जाए या फिर मार्केट के अंदर हमें नीचे से नीचे मिल जाए"* — and he names his own error plainly (*"थोड़ा सा इसमें अपना एंट्री पॉइंट गलत हो गया"*) instead of blaming the market. **In trend, a bad entry is survivable; in range, it is the trade.**
6. **The lagging leg in a multi-index basket decides the outcome, so it must be monitored live as a first-class signal, not a footnote.** *"मोमेंटम वगैरह मार्केट के अंदर ससेक्स और निफ्टी में तो अच्छे हो रहे हैं लेकिन बैंक निफ्टी थोड़ा पीछे है"* — he flagged it while still in profit and exited into it. This is the third session in a row where the **divergence between legs** (26–28 Oct BN weak, 30 Oct BN weak on an up day) was the day's decisive fact.
7. **Symmetric corollary to 28 Oct's point 5, now recorded for the bull side:** on 28 Oct the *weak* leg (BN) snapped back and killed a sell basket; today the *strong* leg of the previous day (BN, +1.18%) failed to continue and capped a buy basket. **Both failures share one cause — the basket's largest leg was the one that did not do what the plan needed.** Working rule candidate: *when the plan is a multi-index ride, the basket is only as good as its lagging leg; if the largest leg is the laggard, the trade is a small-profit trade by construction.* (Recorded, not promoted to the ledger.)
