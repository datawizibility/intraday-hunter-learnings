- Contrast with prior day: yesterday’s “continuous up” flipped and stopped him out; today continuous down held → booked average/strong target when stall/retrace risk rose (late sellers start averaging).

### Process lessons
- Big momentum days: **no retrace** = stay with flow; large retrace invites crowd and kills extension.
- Don’t hunt sellers already deep in profit after a big dump.
- Correct trades you can sit; wrong continuous flips you cut (yesterday’s lesson applied).

### Keep permanently
1. Continuous one-way tape without retrace → follow; wait for stall only to **book**, not to invent reverse.
2. After a large move, resting inventory in profit is usually **not** the hunt target — follow the open.
3. Same “continuous momentum” read can be long or short; the kill is a **deep flip**, not a small pause.
