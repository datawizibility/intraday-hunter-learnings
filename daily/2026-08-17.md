- Big target kept because momentum strong; held while small green candles appeared (fear of reversal keeps others out).

### Process lessons
- Long selling + sudden momentum = follow it; retracement chances low.
- Reduce size when expiry/slow-momentum risk is high.
- Double bottom after a fast move = trap to keep sellers out — read it as continuation, not reversal.
- When others are afraid of reversal, your profit builds.

### Keep permanently
1. Strong momentum after long trend = follow, don't wait for retracement.
2. Cut size when weekly-expiry / slow momentum risk rises.
3. A double bottom after a fast down-move is often a trap (to prevent selling), not a reversal signal.
