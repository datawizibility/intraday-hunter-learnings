### Keep permanently
1. After uninterrupted momentum, profitable trend traders usually book quickly because they fear a retracement; do not automatically assume they remain trapped and must be targeted.
2. After prior continuous selling, a **flat or gap-down open** can support continuation selling; a **large gap-up** changes the setup because recent sellers may then become targets.
3. If the market opens flat and starts falling immediately, wait: fresh sellers can pile in and create a trap. A small move upward first can provide a cleaner sell entry.
4. Prefer an entry on the retracement/positive move rather than chasing a direct fall; it improves price and reduces participation with the crowd.
5. Nearby round numbers and the closing price can cause temporary retracements. If the thesis remains valid, control the urge to exit merely because open profit contracts.
6. Let a profitable trade work, but after a prior loss, book a good achieved target rather than stretching unnecessarily for every remaining point.
7. Confirmation improves when multiple indices break in the same direction together; simultaneous sharp selling also helps option premiums expand.
