### Keep permanently
1. **The breakdown-failure mirror: when one index (the largest-quantity one) breaks its lower point while the other two hold *their* lower points, the break is the trap.** *"जब एक इंडेक्स में किसी लेवल का ब्रेकडाउन हो जाए और दो इंडेक्स अगर सपोर्ट वगैरह लेकर बैठे हैं तो जो सपोर्ट लेते हैं वो ऊपर चले जाते हैं। जो ब्रेकडाउन होता है वो फेलियर में कन्वर्ट हो जाता है।"* The copy-cat sellers who short the two holding indices *are* the fuel.
2. **Waiting for the remaining sellers’ SLs to be created is a plan — but it must be bounded by the normal target.** Here he had ₹3 lakh on screen and finished on the normal target, handing back a booked-level profit while waiting for a breakout that arrived without momentum.
