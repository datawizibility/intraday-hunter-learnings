### Keep permanently
1. **Sellers can only be targeted on a gap-up.** A gap-up creates fear in existing sellers; on a flat or gap-down open they are already in profit and confident, so they will not give their stop-loss easily — target the *invested/positional* holders instead, whom the market does target over time.
2. **Don't sell blindly into a large gap-down.** No one buys and everyone sells at once, so recovery can be large; random sellers get activated and can pull the market up sharply. Wait for a rejection/mindset change before entering.
3. **No stop-loss nearby plus continuous selling = momentum trade, not a scalp.** Where there is no available SL around, the market can move in a straight line — but the entry will always be a little plus/minus; that is the risk, and a trade without risk doesn't exist.
4. **If the market keeps crossing your limit repeatedly, get out.** Standing still is a bad habit. A small-sideways or small-breakout outcome is still a loss for you, and holding can turn it into a big loss.
5. **Cut within your limit, not outside it.** You may wait while the loss is shrinking or time is on your side, but you cannot wait once you are outside your loss limit.
6. **Don't treat the market as friend or enemy.** The market does its own thing; it neither favours you nor opposes you. Money is made over 10–15–20 trades, not one trade.
