### What happened / what he did
- Gap-up open; prior day closed **breakout** but NO big momentum → sellers already flushed, not many buyers → market can't go far up.
- Bias from premarket = **SELL**. Entered puts across Bank Nifty (900), Sensex, Nifty (1430).
- Profit started early; waited for **closing-price breakdown** across indices to expand target.
- Market stalled / positive momentum appeared → loss grew; he waited (time + limit) then **cut** because closing price never broke — no point making loss bigger in a non-momentum market.

### Process lessons
- Breakout close WITHOUT follow-through = not a buy; gap-up is a temptation trap → sell.
- Closing price is the line in the sand; no breakdown = no big target, cut early.
- Even when direction thesis was right, if structure turns choppy/sideways → cut; don't wait for confirmation forever.

### Keep permanently
1. Breakout close without momentum = trap → sell the gap-up temptation.
2. No closing-price breakdown = no extended target; cut per limit.
3. Sideways/choppy kills the edge even when the bias is correct.
