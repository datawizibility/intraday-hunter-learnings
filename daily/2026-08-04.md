### Process lessons
- After continued upside without ATH/explosive special case → buyers are the target; sell setups on gap-up / flat / normal gap-down; ignore large gap-down.
- Same shape open ≠ same plan — holiday + retrace-close vs exact round-number hold flips gap-up buy vs sell.
- Sell-the-buyers after gap-up needs **closing-price breakdown** before buyers start cutting.
- If BN leads higher against puts → **cut**; don’t hold for a larger loss.
- Volatile/expiry: wider target/SL ok — still respect cut discipline when the leader invalidates.

### Keep permanently
1. Normal continuation (not ATH / not explosive) → buyers are hunt targets; gap-up / flat / normal gap-down = sell map; **ignore large gap-down**.
2. Holiday-in-between + retrace close → gap-up often **buy with market**; no holiday + exact round-number support hold → gap-up can still **sell buyers**, but wait for **closing-price breakdown**.
3. Put thesis: if **Bank Nifty leads higher** → cut; don’t hold a big loss hoping.
