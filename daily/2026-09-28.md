### Keep permanently
1. **A single large candle is not an entry.** One-candle momentum cannot be trusted, and the premium adjustment means even catching that candle pays nothing — wait for the market to settle and the structure to become readable.
2. **A red candle that is matched by an equal green candle is a "no-trade" market** — the shape is only usable when the market is *stable* and one direction is genuinely in control.
3. **The flush is the precondition for continuation.** Follow the market lower only when the sellers have *already* been chased out by a breakout; had the sellers still been sitting, a red candle could have been faded back up.
4. **Book the momentum, don't marry it.** In a multi-index move the same green candle that builds the profit is the one that can return it on a retracement; "one escape is luck, sitting again is not."
5. **Three-index participation sets the size of the win.** When all three indices move together the target is reachable; one-index moves cannot travel as far.
