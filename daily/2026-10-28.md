### Keep permanently
1. **A gap-down does not confirm the sell — it *removes the hunt*.** *"गैपडाउन में वो कहीं ना कहीं प्रॉफिट वगैरह में आ गए होंगे। डायरेक्टली उनको टारगेट नहीं बनाया जा सकता।"* The seated side has already been paid, so it is no longer fuel; the only trade left is **continuation in the direction the gap opened**, and its quality is much lower than a hunt.
2. **When the gap-down is small and the index reverses, the continuation thesis dies at the loss limit — not at the level.** Today's entry was *"हल्का सा … ज्यादा ही गलत"*; the exit rule that saved the account was the **pre-set loss limit**, not a re-read of the chart.
3. **A ₹3 lakh target on a continuation day needs three indices moving together; when the largest one is the one reversing, the target is unreachable — take the normal one.** *"तीनों इंडेक्स में अगर एक साथ मोमेंटम होता है तो टारगेट को हम थोड़ा बड़ा कर पाएंगे"* was the assumption that cost the trade.
