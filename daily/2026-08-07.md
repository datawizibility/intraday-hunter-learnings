### What happened / what he did (chronological)

**Open & wait**
- **Light gap-down**. Sensex and Nifty showed early **recovery / slight positive** right after open → **wait** briefly (don’t force sell into the bounce).
- Overall still planned selling, but open was drifting positive → pause.
- BN chart looked **ready sooner** for a put entry; still waited a bit for Sensex/Nifty alignment.

**Entry**
- After a short wait: if market goes **further up**, it risks becoming **too positive** → enter puts now.
- BN chart “bilkul sahi”; Sensex/Nifty might give more trouble — **breakout should not** happen; the little upside that was due had already printed.
- Puts: BN **57,900** + **57,800**; Sensex qty **900**; Nifty qty **1430**.
- Early flick: small loss ↔ profit noise — expected.

**Why sell / put (thesis)**
- Prior day (Aug 6): gap-up + continued positive; momentum small but market went **above 58,000** and stayed constructive → **sellers not sitting**; **buyers** are the inventory.
- Light GD → hunt those buyers with puts.
- Early bounce after GD is **bait**: if market dumped straight from the open, buyers might exit in 2–3 minutes; a small recovery gives them **hope** → they **average** / hold → then market delivers a **bigger loss** to them. That average-then-flush is the path being followed.
- Same read on Sensex: prior gap-up / hold-up → buyers, not sellers.
- Same on Nifty: small prior momentum but held **up** → buyers sitting.
- After bait + flush of anyone who tried early sells → overall selling should land; capture it with puts.
- Broader frame: market was going up ~2 days, rejection, then continued → sellers already flushed earlier → **buyers** remain → target them. Selling *will* come (small or large); puts are there to capture it.

**Invalidation / mid-trade risk**
- If price **crosses closing price** → market turns positive and the put is **wrong** (buyers already got their bait bounce; further upside = thesis fail). Prefer fall **before** CP cross.
- Mid: loss started increasing as BN pushed up and SN/Nifty went positive briefly — watched CP carefully.
- Then **Sensex/Nifty started giving profit first**; BN still showed some loss — not a big problem, because the SN/Nifty drop from their highs was the right shape (if they had crossed CP, market could have gone too positive).

**Psychology / resistance ignore**
- Question: if CP is resistance, won’t others sell there? Unlikely — traders who sold into this chart already took repeated losses; mindset after **repeated ups** is “where do I **buy**?” — GD recovery focused them on buying. **Resistance ignored**; buyers add; gradual fall pays the puts.

**Momentum, sideways, target expand**
- BN then joined the sell; SN/Nifty already negative → profit expanding. Wait for target **unless sideways**.
- Sideways is only knowable **after a breakdown** fails to appear — until breakdown, don’t declare sideways. Momentum market: no breakdown → maybe sideways; wait for momentum.
- Strong multi-index sell (all three trying down together) → **expand target**; easier to achieve.

**Second bait → book**
- Mid-move: market paused / bait again — gives remaining buyers hope that “breakdown isn’t coming” (they may not even add size, but hope keeps them in for a **larger loss**).
- Expect **one more momentum leg** → that leg is the **book** zone. Don’t fear the temporary profit dip during the bait pause.
- Booked on that second momentum: smooth mapped sell already paid; more move possible but not required.
- Closing recap: buyers averaged on the GD bounce bait → then large loss; early put loss then fast target once momentum started; **PROFIT jabardast**.

### Process lessons
- After positive hold-up / buyers sitting: need a **decent GD** (farther from RN better) to hunt; flat/GU → buy with market — don’t blanket-sell every soft open.
- Light GD bounce after buyers sat is often **bait to average** — don’t treat the recovery as thesis-kill if CP still holds; instant dump would have flushed buyers fast; bait creates the larger loss path.
- Put invalidation: **closing-price cross** = trade wrong; prefer fall before that.
- Companions (SN/Nifty) can lead profit while BN lags — not automatic cut if the CP map still holds.
- After repeated upside days, retail mindset is “where to buy?” — resistance ignored; that supports the gradual sell path.
- Sideways only knowable after breakdown fails — don’t declare chop early; wait momentum.
- Smooth 3-index sell → **expand target**; mid-move bait pause → expect one more momentum leg → book there.
- Early loss on bait bounce is process noise when thesis is buyer-hunt after hold-up.

### Keep permanently
1. After positive hold-up / buyers sitting: **decent gap-down** (farther from RN better) → sell buyers; **flat / gap-up** → **buy with market**.
2. Buyer SLs often sit at the **lower points** already formed — GD into that zone is the hunt map.
3. Light GD bounce after buyers sat = **bait to average** (hope → bigger loss) — don’t chase the recovery as a thesis kill if **CP** still holds; CP cross = invalidation.
4. After repeated ups, traders focus on **buy** not sell at resistance — supports gradual put path.
5. Sideways only knowable **after** breakdown fails; smooth multi-index sell → **expand target**; mid-move bait → second momentum → **book**.
6. Companions can lead profit before BN joins — not automatic cut while CP map holds.
