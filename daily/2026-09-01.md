### Keep permanently
1. A flat/gap-up open after several sessions of selling can be used to target carried sellers; a large gap-down changes the plan to SELL.
2. One opposite candle/rejection may be noise; a second rejection is a warning. Predefine the loss ceiling and cut if that ceiling is reached.
3. Breakouts need fast momentum. Slow candles give other traders time to join and can weaken the breakout edge.
4. Cross-index confirmation matters: Bank Nifty's breakout became effective when Nifty and Sensex moved with it.
5. Risk is unavoidable. Take only setups where the expected favorable move justifies the predefined loss, then follow the loss limit without panic.
6. Set the profit target before entry and book when it is reached; do not revise the target merely because the move looks capable of continuing.
