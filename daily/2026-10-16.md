### Keep permanently
1. **Momentum does not exhaust in one day.** *"Never think that because momentum came one day, more momentum will not come. Momentum comes even after momentum."* Pre-deciding "the market has already run, so today must fall" is what makes a good trade slip out of hand — and it is what makes you take a *wrong* trade instead.
2. **The ONE-BREAKDOWN / ONE-BREAKOUT rule (SL-availability defines the depth of the counter-move).** If the market must go positive, it will do **one** level's breakdown — **not the second** — *when no counter-side SL cluster exists at the second level*. Take support of the **closing price** ⇒ the second level holds; the same for a round number. Symmetrically, one resistance breakout, not two, if **seller SLs are not available** above. ⇒ Count *where the stops are*, then trade the depth that is actually payable.
3. **A single breakdown is a psychology-change tool, not a trend change.** *"Just one breakdown will change the psychology; after that, overall the market will start covering positive."* ⇒ the flush leg is the entry window for the with-market side, not a reason to flip.
4. **Book into sharp, continuous momentum — that is where booking is best.** *"The more the chance to book in a continuous and sharp momentum, the better."* When **two–three indices** show tremendous momentum together, take the money rather than hold for a fixed target.
5. **No-retracement tapes do not repeat their prior magnitude — tighten, don't extrapolate.** *"It's going up fast without retracement, so it will roughly not do yesterday-like momentum; when it turns it may stop."* ⇒ stay while the **green candle continues**, exit the moment the turn **reduces profit**.
6. **An initial loss does not invalidate the setup; the SL does.** *"The loss will show, but net booking will not happen; the market will not eat our SL."* Separate "P&L flicker" from "setup failed".
7. **Wait out the sharp rejection at the bell before entering.** *"Sharp rejection is happening right now, so let's wait for entry."* Do not enter into the opening flush; let the first move finish.
8. **A laggard index in the basket is managed, not punished.** Bank Nifty gave nothing early while Sensex/Nifty paid; he waited and the laggard contributed a candle later. Judge the **basket**, not the laggard.
