### Keep permanently
1. Keep the open-type plan ready **before** the open so execution can be fast without becoming impulsive — the market can move inside one minute, and the only thing left to decide live is *where* to enter.
2. Do not chase a market that is already falling straight from the open: wait for a retracement, because a direct dump gives no chance to participate with a usable entry.
3. The open type validates the scenario: only a **proper/meaningful gap-up** validates the buy case; flat-to-gap-down keeps the sell case. A small gap-up proves nothing.
4. On this **2–3 day chart** a gap-up and a gap-down build completely different situations — read the structure before the open, then let the open pick the branch.
5. In a day that is meant to keep falling, retracements stay **small**; a large retracement is the market telling you others can now start selling (or that the move is changing).
6. An **option buyer pays rent**: one small green candle can cut a large part of the profit, so time spent is part of the decision — once momentum has paid and time has run long, book instead of waiting for the full mapped path.
7. Bank Nifty 57,000 as a target only promised *"more problems" after a breakdown there* — he booked because the breakdown could take its own time, not because the thesis died.
