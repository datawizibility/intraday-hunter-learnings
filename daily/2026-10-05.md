### Keep permanently
1. **"Traps form for the side that still has stops."** When a retracement has already flushed the nearby sellers and no buyer has seated himself (holiday-thinned tape), the day's traps are built **against sellers**: the market invites a seller on each rejection and then lifts to take those stops. So a gap-up/flat open here is a **BUY-with-market**, not a sell.
2. **The investor is not the target until his level is crossed.** A long-tailed investor pool that has just been swept *up to a level* will not cut further while that level holds — remove them from the target list until the level actually breaks.
3. **A normal target is the correct target when the quantity index lags.** Profit from the companions (Sensex/Nifty) while the largest-quantity index (Bank Nifty) stays behind → **book**, because if BN turns first it will erase the companions' profit and a rejection will keep the loss alive. (Bible §6 / §7 exit #24.)
4. **Each rejection is the entry cue for the next leg up** when the seller pool is the one being flushed — not a reason to exit. Small rejections are the *engine* of such a day.
5. **No directional target without the quantity index.** Expected targets (Sensex ~72,800 / Nifty ~22,660 / BN ~55,400) are only cashable if **BN leads**; the mapped path is discountable the moment BN diverges.
6. **Predefine target / stop / quantity per trade, and scale risk to the target you are actually aiming at.** Repeatedly re-deciding size/target/SL is itself the leak — *"the trader stays behind and cannot work sharply"* (Bible §8/§9).
