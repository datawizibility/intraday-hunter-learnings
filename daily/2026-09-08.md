### Keep permanently
1. After a big fall, don't assume a bounce — first decide whether the move is a followable continuation or a trap.
2. Ask "who is trapped, and with how much quantity?" If the sellers are already in deep profit, or never built big quantity (expensive premiums at the lows), there are no meaningful seller SLs above to hunt — follow the continuation instead.
3. If retail has not yet entered the move, continuation can still be followed; the crowd's later entry is what the market will fade.
4. A sharp counter-spike with no seller SLs above is a mindset-changing move, not a trend change — it should not be able to extend, and the original direction resumes.
5. At-the-money options are the default for momentum trades: deep ITM ties up too much capital and hurts risk-reward; far OTM bleeds heavily if momentum is delayed.
6. Option buying pays "rent" (time decay). Don't sit for long hoping — capture the momentum you can and leave when the move stalls.
7. Take the predefined target and get out; extending the target is allowed occasionally when the market looks strong, or by cutting part quantity and holding part — but never let greed decide.
8. Avoid over-trading in choppy up-down-up-down markets; more entries there means more loss.
9. No regret is required when an exit level later breaks — a plan followed is a plan followed.

### Psychology / discipline
