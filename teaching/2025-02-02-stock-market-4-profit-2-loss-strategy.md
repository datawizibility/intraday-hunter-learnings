### Core idea
- A **scorecard week**: a long weekend, 6 sessions, 6 trades → **4 profits, 2 losses**, with the risk-reward maintained on each: *"इस वीक में हमने छह ट्रेड किए, उनमें दो ट्रेड में हमारा लॉस हो गया और चार ट्रेड में… प्रॉफिट बना है"* / *"अगर आप ट्रेड वाइज भी आरआर देखोगे तो वो भी हमने सही से मेंटेन कर रखा है."*
- Both losses trace to the **same two roots**: going after a side that was **already in profit**, and entering at a **level** that then broke out.
- The durable rule he keeps repeating: **a seated side that is already in profit cannot be targeted directly** — their stop-losses are not available and their money does not enter the market, so you must wait for them to book out *and* for the market to create a fresh seated side before the reversal has fuel.
- The correction is explicit and dated: after the big-gap-down mistake the next day's plan carries the refusal — *"गैप डाउन में भी हम बाइंग कर रहे हैं? वैसा हम काम नहीं करेंगे. यहां पे मिस्टेक रिपीट नहीं करेंगे."* ⚠️ This directly revokes the gap-down-buy licence stated on **2025-01-05** — recorded, **not** adjudicated (see Evolution).
- **Event week (the Budget):** assume the seated buyers are **holding for the event** — which makes them the target — but a **big gap** open voids the plan entirely.
- *"एक दिन का प्रॉफिट, एक दिन का लॉस कुछ कुछ नहीं होता"* — the week is judged on how he worked each day, not on the P&L.

### Lesson 1 — a side that is already in profit cannot be targeted directly
- Setup: continue-selling market, a rejection, a break-down — so he expected sellers seated, and the plan was **gap-up or small gap-down ⇒ target the sellers** (i.e. a buy plan).
- What happened: a **big gap-down** opened. The mechanism as he states it: *"बड़े गैप डाउन के बाद… जो भी यहां पे सेल करके बैठे होते हैं ना वो अच्छे प्रॉफिट में आ जाते हैं. अब जैसे यहां पे किसी ने पुट लिया, ठीक है, उसको प्रॉफिट नजर आ रहा है — वो ट्रेड कट क्यों करेगा? … और अगर कट करेगा भी, तब भी वो मार्केट को लॉस नहीं दे देने वाला."*
- The rule in one line: *"अगर सेलर प्रॉफिट में बैठा है तो उसको डायरेक्टली टारगेट नहीं किया जा सकता"* — and that is why he had to stand aside in the big gap-down.
- His own mistake: when the market recovered he still went for them — *"जैसे ही मार्केट ऊपर आया वो अपना प्रॉफिट वगैरह बुकिंग करके भाग गए"* — the market could not go higher, and because he had built a **call** trade the market had to *"दोबारा से एसल वगैरह क्रिएट करके नीचे आना"*. Loss — cut.
- Reusable mechanism: **the profiting side's fuel is spent.** Their stops are not available and their money leaves the market; the market must *manufacture* a new seated side before a reversal trade can run.

### Lesson 2 — same chart, same plan, different open (and the correction)
- He re-reads the identical structure — sellers seated from the break-down — and reaches the same conclusion, then adds the gate: **flat-to-gap-down ⇒ sell**, and **a gap(-up) ⇒ buy** so that the sellers who accumulate on the rejection become the target. Explicitly **no buy on a gap-down**: *"गैप डाउन में भी हम बाइंग कर रहे हैं? वैसा हम काम नहीं करेंगे."*
- The next day obliged: *"किस्मत अच्छी रही कि मार्केट के अंदर सीधा हमें एक बड़ा गैप मिल गया… तुरंत हमने वहां पे कोल कार ट्रेड बनाया और एक अच्छा खासा प्रॉफिट बना के निकले."*
- The takeaway he insists on: the read was not wrong, the **open type** it was applied to was.

### Lesson 3 — the level gate on entry (where the second loss came from)
- Plan for the event day was **selling**, on the read that buyers were **holding positions for the Budget** and would be taken out: *"जिस प्रकार का साइकोलॉजी था, जिस प्रकार का इवेंट था… बायर को जैसे ही अप साइड का मोमेंटम आया नजर आया होगा ना, तो यहां पे ट्रेड वगैरह बना के वो गए होंगे."*
- He sold after a small retracement; the market went up; **the level broke out** and he exited at once: *"जैसे ही थोड़ा सा ब्रेकआउट हुआ 800 के लेवल का, हम तुरंत मार्केट के अंदर निकल गए."*
- He explicitly did **not** sit and hope: *"ना ही हमने ओवर ट्रेडिंग किया, ना ही हमने ये सोचा कि मार्केट दोबारा से गिरेगा"* — because an event week can print anything: *"इवेंट है, मार्केट के अंदर कुछ भी हो सकता है, वोलेटाइल मोमेंटम दे सकता है."*
- Why the entry was so unforgiving: *"इंट्राडे में क्या होता है — हमारे पास लिमिट बहुत छोटा होता है. अगर 150 से 200 पॉइंट का भी मार्केट ने अपोजिट मोमेंटम कर दिया तो लॉस लेकर जाना पड़ता है."*
- Sequencing correction: the level sat **near the entry**, so the market's move to it was enough to invalidate the trade — *"मुझे लग रहा था कि एक रिट्रेसमेंट के बाद हम ट्रेड करेंगे, लेकिन मार्केट में 800 का लेवल आसपास था, तो मार्केट ने उसका ब्रेकआउट कर दिया."*

### The Bank Nifty vs Sensex / Nifty divergence (30 Jan — a read to reuse)
- Across the 30 January charts he had to decide **which index** carried the clean setup, and the answer differed:
  - **Sensex** — buyers **completely removed**, then the market moved up with **fast momentum**: *"यहां पे बायर को मार्केट कंप्लीट निकाल चुका था… फास्टली मोमेंटम करके ऊपर निकल गया, तो आराम से अगले दिन बाइंग वगैरह की हमने पैसा बनाया"* → *"ये मेरे लिए सबसे अच्छा चार्ट था."*
  - **Nifty** — the same shape: buyers out, foreigners/like-us barely bought, so continue-upside momentum was available: *"जो बायर बैठे हैं वो अच्छे बायर हैं, और हमारे जैसे लोगों ने बाइंग वगैरह ज्यादा किया नहीं"* → continue upside.
  - **Bank Nifty** — momentum had **not been large** (it had not fallen far), so the buyers may have **held their positions**; only Bank Nifty printed a rejection: *"केवल बैंक निफ्टी में रिजेक्शन मार्केट ने दिया, अदर वाइज सेंसेक्स और निफ्टी में रिजेक्शन नहीं दिया"* / *"हो सकता है यहां पे बायर ने अपना पोजीशन होल्ड कर रखा है."*
- Read: **the flush is per-index, not per-market.** The index whose pool was fully cleared gave the clean trade; the index whose pool survived gave the rejection. Do not carry one index's read into another.
- His aside on the operator, restated: *"हमारे जैसे लोगों ने यहां पे बाइंग किया नहीं होगा… ऑपरेटर का अगर कोई पोजीशन है तो उसको मैं वैसे भी टारगेट करना नहीं होता."*

### Keep permanently
1. **Never target a side that is already in profit.** Their stops are unavailable and their money does not enter the market; wait for them to book out and for the market to create a fresh seated side before trading the reversal.
2. **The open type gates the plan, it does not decorate it.** The same chart read yields different actions on a gap-up, a flat/small gap-down, and a big gap — and a **big gap can void the plan entirely**.
3. **Carry the named mistake into the next day's written plan.** *"कल वाली गलती हम रिपीट नहीं करेंगे"* — correct it explicitly rather than trusting memory.
4. **The flush is per-index.** Trade the index whose pool has actually been cleared; a surviving pool shows up as a rejection in that index alone.
5. **Do not enter right at a level you expect to be tested.** A 150–200 point adverse move is enough to end an intraday trade — distance from the level is part of the setup.
6. **Exit on a good profit; do not sit waiting for more.** The market often came precisely to hit that pool and then reverses to create the next one.
7. **In an event week assume the seated side is holding for the event** — that makes them the target, and also makes the tape violent; if the open is a big gap, do nothing.
8. **One day's profit or loss is nothing.** Judge yourself on how you worked each day, not on that day's P&L.
9. **Keep risk-reward maintained on every trade** — the 4:2 week was a risk-reward outcome, not a lucky run.
10. **Follow the spine to the next day:** the side the market has removed is the side it will run against next — buyer removed ⇒ the next plan is buying.
