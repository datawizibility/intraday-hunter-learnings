### Core idea
- The session contrasts **two mindsets** and their consequences. The **beginner** applies every new piece of information immediately — *"जैसे ही उसे कोई नई जानकारी मिलती है, उसके ऊपर वह काम करना शुरू कर देता है"* (Bollinger Bands, RSI, support/resistance — each is turned into a setup the moment it is learned) — and so **grows**. The self-declared **"professional"** builds a narrow mindset — *"अब तो मुझे सब कुछ पता लग गया है"* — and starts **finding faults** in anything new instead of working on it, so he **falls behind**.
- ⭐ **A real professional is defined by method, not by years.** *"भले हम 4 साल हो गए मार्केट के अंदर, भले 10 साल हो गए — वो मैटर नहीं करता; हमारा काम करने का तरीका कैसा है, उसके ऊपर डिपेंड करता है."* The professional checklist: knows **where to deploy capital and where not to**, **where to just sit and watch**, **how to manage a loss**, **how to grow a profit**.
- **Both are needed together:** learn the market **as a beginner**, execute the trade **as a professional**. *"कोई भी ट्रेडर बिग नहीं होता, कोई भी ट्रेडर प्रोफेशनल नहीं होता — दोनों एक साथ चलते हैं."*
- Two of the learning tools named: **candlesticks** and **support/resistance**.

### Beginner vs professional — the difference
| | Behaviour | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginner** | Applies every new input at once; builds setups on it; keeps adding | Knowledge and edge compound — moves ahead |
| **Self-declared "professional"** | Assumes he knows everything; tears new inputs down (*"इसमें तो यह प्रॉब्लम है"*) | Falls behind |

- The **anti-professional tells** he lists: taking a loss then **increasing quantity**; drifting into **over-trading**; putting money in on someone's tip; jumping between stock ↔ option ↔ "something looks good". None of this is a professional method regardless of experience.

### Candlesticks — learn them in the LIVE market
- The best way to learn candlesticks is to **sit in the live market**: *"जितना ज्यादा लाइव चार्ट देखोगे, उतना ही ज्यादा कैंडल स्टिक समझ पाओगे."* On an **offline** chart the candle is already formed, so the mind reads the outcome backwards and the **psychology of how the candle was made is lost**.
- If you cannot watch the Indian market (part-time work), **watch foreign markets in other time zones** — the same live-candle experience transfers.
- Candle types: **very weak / small candles** carry *"कोई हिसाब-किताब नहीं"* — just the market obeying a support/resistance zone. Only the **major candles** matter.

### ⭐ Money candle vs SL candle (the key distinction)
Two red candles can share colour and similar momentum (100–300 pts) yet mean opposite things. The difference is **whether money was deployed**:
| | What made it | Meaning | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Money candle** (पैसा लगाकर) | Capital pushed the market down/up | *"ये कभी घूमेगा नहीं"* — **80–90% chance it will not reverse** | Entry may be taken using its **high as the mark** (a rejection high on a money candle can be sold) |
| **SL candle** | A stop-loss was **hit** (e.g. buyers' SLs below the closing price) | The market **can turn** back | **Only target-booking at its high — never entry** *"क्योंकि मार्केट वहाँ घूम सकता है"* |

- Reading which is which needs the chart context: a candle made right after a gap-open that pushed *against* the expected direction, with no buyer SL yet hit, is a **money candle**; a candle made when traders couldn't cut and stops then triggered is an **SL candle**.
- Because a money candle is a strong directional mark, its high can be used for a **sell-side entry reference**; an SL candle's high can only be used for **booking**, because the move can reverse.

### His own miss (no regret)
- He took a **call-side position in a place that had a money candle** and took a loss — *"मेरा दिमाग किसी दूसरी जगह पर चल रहा था; मैं कंप्लीट चार्ट देख रहा था"* (he was reading the whole-chart story — sellers seated above, expecting an averaging-and-resume — instead of the money candle in front of him).
- His lesson: **no regret.** *"हो सकता है कभी आप पैसा बना लो, कभी मैं बना लूं"* — judge by the **month**, not by one day, because *"एक दिन तो तुक्का भी लग सकता है"*. Find the **logic** the market is working on.
- Focus discipline: he **ignores candles while building a position** and only returns to candles **after** the position is built, to decide **target booking**. During the trade his single focus is *"कहाँ पे डायरेक्शन ढूँढ पाऊँ"* — where the buyers and sellers are seated.

### Support = permanent, resistance = temporary (by trend direction)
- In an **up-trending** market: the **support** (where buyers repeatedly held) is **permanent** — *"सपोर्ट परमानेंट हो सकता है, रेजिस्टेंस टेंपरेरी"* — and the **resistance** must be built by you as a **temporary** line.
- In a **down-trending** market the reverse: **resistance permanent, support temporary**.
- How to build a **temporary resistance**: ask how much momentum a buyer at that spot would need. *"अगर कोई कॉल की पोजीशन बनाए, तो कम से कम 400–500 पॉइंट के लिए बनाने वाला है"* — so a temporary resistance sits short of the next round number where profit-booking / rejection is likely.
- **Accuracy asymmetry:** *"रेजिस्टेंस में एक्यूरेसी कम, सपोर्ट में एक्यूरेसी ज्यादा"* — support is more reliable than resistance.
- Motivation for beginners: when the market falls it may **break your support** — *"तो कोई बात नहीं, उससे डरना नहीं है; लेकिन रेजिस्टेंस नहीं तोड़ना चाहिए"* — if you first learn to find the **permanent support** in the direction of the trend, the temporary resistances follow.

### Keep permanently
1. **Learn like a beginner, execute like a professional** — apply every new input when learning; run a disciplined method when trading.
2. A **professional is defined by method, not years**: where to deploy capital, where not to, when to sit and watch, how to manage the loss, how to grow the profit.
3. **Candlesticks must be learned in the live market** — an offline candle has no psychology; use foreign markets in other time zones if needed.
4. **Small/weak candles carry no information**; only the **major candles** matter.
5. **Money candle = capital deployed ⇒ it will not reverse (80–90%)** — its high can be used for a sell-side entry reference.
6. **SL candle = a stop was hit ⇒ the market can reverse** — use its high only for **target-booking, never for entry**.
7. **In an up-trend, support is permanent and resistance is temporary** (reverse in a down-trend); build temporary resistances by the momentum a buyer would need (≥300–400 pts).
8. **Support reads more accurately than resistance** — learn the permanent support first.
9. **Ignore candles while building a position; watch the seated buyers/sellers instead** — return to candles only for target booking.
10. **Judge by the month, not by one day / one trade** — a single day can be a fluke; no regret over a missed move.
