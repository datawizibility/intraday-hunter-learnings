### Core idea
- ⭐ **Do not trade the event — trade its effect on *your* market.** *"जब भी कोई इवेंट होता है, उसके अकॉर्डिंग अगर हम डायरेक्टली मार्केट के अंदर काम करने की कोशिश करेंगे तो इतना अच्छा रिजल्ट हमें नहीं देखने को मिलता"* — a war (like any event, in any country) is always negative *in the abstract*, so the question is never "is the news bad?" but **"what does it do to the price I am being given?"**
- ⭐ **The one operative mechanism — the LOW CROSS.** *"जब भी मार्केट एक लो बना के आया है … उस लो को अगर मार्केट क्रॉस करेगा"* → the index low crossing *means* individual stocks' lows are crossing too → **long-term investors (1 / 5 / 10 years) get a profit or a stop-loss** → *"या तो वो प्रॉफिट बुकिंग करेगा या फिर उसके एसल मार्केट खाने शुरू कर देगा … तो वो खुद से एग्जिट करेगा, तब मार्केट के अंदर हमें सेलिंग डेफिनेटली देखने को मिलेगा."*
  - ⇒ It becomes a **chain reaction**: cross the low → someone cuts → the next low crosses → the next investor exits → selling continues. *"दोबारा से लो क्रॉस गया, दोबारा से वैसा ही चलेगा."*
- ⭐ **The rule that follows:** *"जब भी ऐसे लो क्रॉस करे, मार्केट फिर आपको ऐसा नहीं कि बाय के बारे में सोचना है"* — **when the low is being crossed, stop thinking about buying**; either ignore the buy or work the sell side.
- ⭐ **And the gate that lifts the ban:** *"जब तक लो वगैरह मार्केट नहीं बनाए, कुछ अच्छा अपसाइड का मोमेंटम करके अपने आप को क्लोज ना करें, तब तक … बाय वगैरह को इग्नोर करो"* — only **after a bottom is built and closed back up** do the chart's normal rules resume.

  - *"ना ही ऐसा था कि युद्ध वगैरह चल रहा है या ये … नहीं रुकने वाला है … केवल हम यहां पे चार्ट में देख रहे थे, अपने एसएल देख रहे थे, अपनी साइकोलॉजी देख रहे थे, उसके अकॉर्डिंग डिसीजन बना रहे थे."*
- ⇒ **The event is the *reason* the crowd is positioned oddly; the pool of stop-losses is still the trade.**

### The plan by open (after the sell and after the bottom)
Both blocks turn on **who is trapped**, and gap size decides:
| Situation | Open | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Right after a big sell — random sellers just entered and kept quantity | Gap-down | **BUY** — the gap-down also parks a **round number** under the market; random sellers are the target |
| Same | Gap-up | **BUY** — the random sellers who opened *are* the target (round number less reliable) |
| After a **bottom** has formed | Gap-down | **SELL** — the low is likely to be crossed again → fresh fall |
| Same | Big gap-up | **SELL** — *"जो सेलर बैठे हैं, मार्केट उनको डायरेक्टली टारगेट कर लेगा";* with no upper-side SLs left available, sell |
| Same | Small gap | **BUY possible** — the sellers parked below are the target; market dips and lifts to flush them |

- ⭐ **The gap-size condition (the reconciling rule he states):** *"अगर गैप अप ज्यादा ओपन हो जाता है तो यहां पे जो सेलर बैठे हैं, मार्केट उनको डायरेक्टली टारगेट कर लेगा … अदर वाइज अगर छोटा-मोटा गैप ओपन होता है, तो यहां पे जो सेलर बैठे हैं, इनको टारगेट करने के लिए हम बाय भी कर सकते हैं."*

### Keep permanently
1. **Never trade an event; trade your market's reaction to it.** War (or any news) is negative in the abstract — the only useful question is what price/position the event has created.
2. **The low cross is the event signal.** When an index crosses a prior low, individual stocks are crossing theirs too, and long-term investors are being forced to exit.
3. **A crossed low means: do not think about buying.** Ignore buy setups, or look at the sell side — the forced-exit chain supplies the downside.
4. **The buy ban lifts only after a bottom is built and the market closes back up with real upside momentum.** Until then, a lower low can keep repeating.
5. **An unexpected fast sell creates random sellers — they are the buy-side target** (they parked quantity they did not mean to hold).
6. **Gap size is the plan's variable, not the direction.** A big gap-up after a bottom takes out the trapped sellers and leaves no upper SLs (→ sell); a small gap lets you buy to reach the sellers parked below.
7. **A gap-down parks a round number under the market** — read it as a level, not only as a gap.
8. **Round numbers still create intraday momentum** — keep watching them regardless of the narrative.
9. **After you get trapped once, your odds of being trapped again rise** — the wrong psychology you build is itself a risk; reset before the next trade.
10. **When the market turns to a one-way fall, the chart is not "wrong" — the investor-exit chain is simply overpowering it.** Trade the chain, not the drawing.
