### Core idea
- Trading has two parts — the part where we lose and the part where we make money. Today he takes **his own losing trades** and reverse-engineers them so the same loss does not repeat.
- *"लॉस एक बिन बुलाया मेहमान जैसा होता है"* — it arrives one day, then the next, then five days, then ten, and can take the whole month. But **loss is the base**: *"लॉस कर-कर के हम मार्केट में प्रॉफिट बनाना सीखते हैं."* Understand the loss and the profit follows.
- **Loss-analysis method:** do NOT start from "how much did I lose / where did the breakout happen". Start from where the story began — **the entry**: *"अगर हम ट्रेड ही सही ले लेते तो बात ही खत्म हो जाती; हमने ट्रेड ही गलत लिया था, इसीलिए लॉस हुआ."* Rebuild **why that trade was taken**.
- Tool note: he analyses on the **1-minute timeframe** (5-minute hides too much; 3-min/5-min are fine to *look* at, but for analysis he prefers 1-min).

### The pre-trade checklist (restated — the spine of the session)
**To SELL:**
1. **Sellers' SLs must NOT be nearby** — if some exist at all, they must be sitting in **good profit** (not marginal). *"या तो सेलर के एसल होने नहीं चाहिए, अगर है तो अच्छे प्रॉफिट में होने चाहिए."*
2. **Buyers' SLs MUST be available** in the market — *"अगर आप सेल करना चाहते हो, तो एसल तो सेलर के मिलेंगे नहीं, फिर बायर के मिलेंगे"* — you need the other side's stops to target. Without them *"मार्केट कुछ अलग करने की कोशिश करेगा, या एसल क्रिएट करेगा"* — and then it starts moving **towards you**.

**To BUY:** the mirror — buyers' SLs absent (or in good profit) **and** sellers' SLs present.
- If the conditions are not met ⇒ **wait for the SLs to be CREATED**, then take the trade — *"एसल क्रिएट करने का वेट करेंगे … ट्रेड बनाने में हमें बहुत ज्यादा बेनिफिट मिलेगा."* Acting in a hurry without the SLs is exactly how his losses happened.

### Loss #1 — sold into a fast fall that had no buyer SLs left
- The setup looked right: market fell hard, a **seller sat in ~₹10 lakh profit** (condition 1 OK), and the chart showed support taken and a close above support, so he **assumed buyers were seated** (condition 2 OK) → he sold to target them.
- **What actually happened:** the fall was so fast that the buyers were **flushed before they could build**: *"जब मार्केट इतना तेजी से गिरा, तो वो निकल के नहीं गए होंगे?"* A trader who was ~₹10 lakh in profit saw it erode *"9 लाख, 8 लाख, 7 लाख, 6 लाख … थोड़े बहुत लॉस में"* and cut. ⇒ **No buyers' SLs existed at all** ⇒ nothing for the market to fall through ⇒ the trade failed.
- Corroboration on the chart: he could not point to **a single green candle** — buyers never got a chance; the market closed **straight down** (he refers to a time near the close *"करीब 3 बजे 29 … सीधा ही नीचे"*, ASR-unclear).
- ⭐ **What he should have done — the market must first CREATE the SLs.** If there are no SLs, the market has to manufacture them: take **support at a spot visible to others**, print a **pattern** (he points to something like an **inverse head-and-shoulders** on the 1-min chart), give a small retracement and a **breakout** so a side builds positions — *"ऐसी जगह से सपोर्ट लेगा जहाँ से दूसरों को सामने से नज़र आए"* — and only **then** fall. **Selling was the right side; the timing was wrong.**

### Loss #2 — the SLs were created on the wrong side, so the market went the other way
- Chart: fast momentum had already **removed the buyers**; next day the plan was to **buy** (buyers' SLs gone / in good profit ⇒ the seated **sellers** become the target ⇒ market should go up).
- Reality: flat open, and the market went **straight up within a minute or two** — too fast to enter. He reasoned the sellers seated there were **mistake/random sellers** (*"मजबूरी में सेल कर रखा था, गलती के कारण सेल कर रखा था"*) — good traders never sell like that — and as the market took support and rose, **their positions were cut**.
- Then the market **turned completely** ⇒ now the **sellers' SLs were also gone**. He expected a retracement to **create** fresh seller SLs so the market could resume up. It came down again instead.
- **Why? — two reasons:**
  1. **Above the highs (the 51,800 area / the 52,000 level) only BUYERS would arrive, nobody would sell.** The market had taken support *exactly at the closing price* and kept retracing up, so continuing up would attract **only buyers** — and with no sellers, **no seller SLs would ever be created**.
  2. If the market kept moving where only one side arrives, it *"कंटिन्यू मोमेंटम कर नहीं पाता"* — a one-sided run has no fuel.
- ⇒ ⭐ **The market moves to the direction where BOTH buyers and sellers will work** — *"मार्केट हमेशा ऐसी जगह पे काम करता है जहाँ बायर और सेलर दोनों काम करें"* — i.e. towards **confusion**, not towards a clean single-side runway. Where only buyers (or only sellers) can arrive, *"वहाँ पे मार्केट जाकर कभी काम नहीं करता"*; it turns the **opposite** way. That is exactly why it went down (both sides were active down there) instead of up.

### The one exception — a big-level trap
- If you ever see the market go **straight up (or straight down) with an SL sitting there**, that happens **only when a big level is being trapped** — *"वो एक ही कंडीशन में होगा जब बड़े लेवल का ट्रैपिंग होगा."* It is **rare** (not often seen), and in that case you take **only small losses** and cap yourself at **one or two trades**: *"1-2 पर सब कुछ चलेगा, उससे ज्यादा नहीं होना चाहिए."*

### Time, not tips
- Closing: the market is a place to make money and can be a real business, but *"कब देगा? जब आप इसको टाइम देने वाले हो"* — it pays only after you give it **time**. *"एक-दो दिन में ये सब सीख जाऊँगा — ऐसा नहीं होने वाला."*

### Evolution vs earlier

So the entry is: *wait for the SL-creation*, not *hurry into the direction*.

### Keep permanently
1. **Analyse losses from the entry, not from the loss** — ask *why was this trade taken*; a correct entry would have had no loss, so the trade itself was wrong.
2. **Pre-trade gate to SELL:** sellers' SLs **absent** (or in *good* profit) **and** buyers' SLs **present**. To buy, the exact mirror. If not met ⇒ **no trade**.
3. **"Slightly in profit" does not count** — residual SLs must be comfortably in profit or they are the ones that get targeted first.
4. **When the required SLs are missing, the market will CREATE them first** — via a visible support/pattern, a retracement and a breakout. Wait for that; do not hurry into the direction.
5. **The market runs only where BOTH buyers and sellers will act** (towards confusion). A direction where **only one side** can arrive has **no fuel** and the market goes the other way.
6. **A one-sided runway is a trap flag:** if you expect only buyers (or only sellers) above/below, expect the market to turn the opposite way instead.
7. **Fast momentum before you enter = the side was already flushed** — a trader whose profit is eroding (₹10L → 6L) cuts; so the SLs you were counting on may already be gone.
8. **A planned trade you cannot enter because it moves in 1–2 minutes is not a missed trade — it is a missing setup** (the stops had not been created yet).
9. **The one exception — big-level trapping:** a straight run against an obvious SL is rare; treat it as small size and **1–2 trades max**, never more.
10. **Give the market time** — it is a business, not a tip; *"टाइम ही तो मनी है"* — the returns come from the time you put in.
