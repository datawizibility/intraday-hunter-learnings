### Core idea
- **Chart reading is psychology reading, and it is the first step — not a side skill.** The whole session is one method applied to five consecutive days: before each open, ask *who is seated and whose stop-losses are still available*, then let the open type and the market's own reaction decide the side.
- **The trading decision window is brutally short.** *"ट्रेड करते टाइम हमें 2 से 3 मिनट में डिसीजन लेना होता है कि मार्केट के अंदर बाय करें या फिर सेल करें"* — entry, profit-taking and cut size all have to be decided inside that window, which is why the plan is written offline and only executed live.
- **The apparent ease is the trap.** *"ट्रेड जितना आसान लगता है उसको करना उतना ही मुश्किल हो जाता है"* — traders arrive expecting easy money, then find they must learn, work and put in time.
- **The week's spine is a single question asked five times:** did the previous move spend the other side's money (they are gone), or only put them in a small loss (they are still seated — and then *they* are the target)? Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri answered one way, Thursday answered the other and produced the loss.
- **Honest accounting:** four working days traded (Mon call, Tue put, Wed sell, Fri sell — the Fri trade cut short before a scheduled event), **Thursday 5 June a stated loss**, and the loss is what he spends the most time dissecting.

### Monday 2 June — the plan is built offline, the open tests it
- Sensex was **sideways**, so the pre-open plan was deliberately narrow: *"तो यहां पे हमने अच्छे गैप में बाय का प्लान बना रखा था"* — buy-side only. Flat was void: *"इसलिए हमने फ्लैट को इग्नोर किया"* (sideways + a second flat open makes the direction too hard to call).
- Gap-down branch was conditional, not automatic: buy **only** if the market opens with positive momentum — the good buyer who re-deploys his money. *"तो यहां पे कोई अच्छा बायर हो सकता है"*.
- The market did neither of the planned branches: *"गैप डाउन दिया और ओपन होते ही सेलिंग आना शुरू हो गया"* ⇒ the buyer he had assumed *"वो पैसा नहीं लगा पा रहा है या फिर वो भी ट्रैप हो गया"*. The plan's premise was dead, so the plan was dropped — **the new chart becomes the plan**.
- **The big open selling made both sides untradeable, and he deliberately waited:** *"तो यहां पे हमें रुकना पड़ा"* … *"हमने यहां पे 10 से 15 20 मिनट का वेट किया"* — he was prepared to buy, not to sell, so he did not manufacture a sell out of a big red open.
- **What he waited for:** a small breakdown, a short hold, then a big candle and a turn — *"मार्केट ने यहां पे थोड़ा देर के लिए होल्ड किया फिर अचानक से बड़ा कैंडल बनाया"*. His read: the move had already flushed the buyers — *"अगर किसी ने बाय किया भी होगा उनको कंप्लीटली मार्केट निकाल चुका क्योंकि काफी बड़ा मोमेंटम हो चुका है"* — so the up-move's fuel was the retracement sellers who step in above and then get their stops hit: *"तो इसलिए हमने यहां पे मार्केट के अंदर कॉल का ट्रेड बनाया कि दूसरे लोग सेल करेंगे"*.

### The lagging index (Bank Nifty) on the same day
- Nifty and Sensex had fallen fast while Bank Nifty had not, so the divergence read selected the instrument — *"जब दो इंडेक्स ज्यादा गिर जाते हैं और अगर कोई इंडेक्स पीछे रह जाता है तो कभी भी कभी वो ज्यादा नहीं गिरता"*, instead *"वो वहीं पर घूमता रहता है या फिर अपोजिट मोमेंटम करता है"*.
- He names the crowd behaviour that creates it: seeing Sensex/Nifty already down, traders sell Bank Nifty expecting it to follow — the market does the opposite. Because he deploys **more money in Bank Nifty**, this cross-index read is not academic for him.

### Tuesday 3 June — seller money exhausted ⇒ continuation, not reversal
- After Monday's fall-then-full-recovery he drew the opposite conclusion for Tuesday: *"जो सेल करने वाले थे वो अपना पैसा मार्केट के अंदर खत्म कर चुके हैं"* — and if the market continues up, no fresh seller will step in (they will fear repeating Monday), **and no seller stop-losses remain overhead**.
- Therefore the next-day plan was a sell in either open: *"गैप अप मिल जाए फ्लैट टू गैप डाउन ओपन हो जाए वहां पे हम सेलिंग करेंगे"* — he describes the market doing exactly that and hands the momentum to a put trade.
- **The no-target side of the rule:** where selling continues *with* retracements — *"रिट्रेसमेंट के साथ कंटिन्यू सेलिंग हो रहा है"* — he declines to target it, *"तो ऐसे में यहां पे इस सेलर को तो हम टारगेट नहीं बनाएंगे क्योंकि कभी-कभी क्या होता है जब रिट्रेसमेंट के साथ गिरता है तो कोई अच्छा ट्रेडर भी मार्केट के अंदर बैठ सकता है"*.

### Wednesday 4 June — the divergence rule as an EXIT
- A sell trade was taken and it worked in Bank Nifty only: *"बैंक निफ्टी के अंदर तो ब्रेकडाउन हो चुका है"*, while *"निफ़ी और अपना जो सेंसेक्स है वो क्लोजिंग प्राइस का ब्रेकडाउन नहीं कर रहे हैं"*.
- The rule, stated in both directions: *"जो ब्रेकडाउन होता है वो फेलियर में कन्वर्ट हो जाता है और जो सपोर्ट लेता है वो कंटिन्यू काम करता रहता है"* — because traders pile into the other index expecting the same breakdown and the market turns instead. The mirror for breakouts: the index that holds resistance normally works; the one that broke out converts to failure.
- So the exit was taken while the trade was still green — *"तो ऐसे में हमें यहां पे निकल जाना चाहिए"* — because the read that justified the short (the breakdown) would fail if the other two indices refused to follow.

### Thursday 5 June — THE LOSS, and its mechanism
- Plan: sell-side, targeting the buyers. Big gap-up ⇒ no plan; small gap or gap-down ⇒ *"लेकिन अगर कोई छोटा-मोटा गैप ओपन होता है या फिर गैप डाउन वगैरह ओपन होता है"* (target the seated buyers). He notes he did **not** rush the entry — he took it with a retracement.
- The market opened, fell, then kept climbing. Outcome in his words: *"तो ऐसे में हमें लॉस वगैरह लेकर जाना पड़ा"*.
- **The diagnosis (the session's most valuable paragraph):** a big momentum flushes a losing side so thoroughly that it exits and stops coming back — but **without** a big momentum the losing side simply *holds*, waiting for the fall that never comes: *"जो सेल करके बैठे थे ना उन्होंने अपना पोजीशन होल्ड कर लिया"*, on a small paper loss — *"हम यहां पे ट्रेड को होल्ड कर लेते हैं"*.
- Then the market used them as fuel: *"जो सेलर लॉस में था उसको थोड़ा बहुत लालच दिया एवरेज वगैरह करवाया मार्केट को और फिर अचानक से ऊपर चला गया"* — i.e. the seated-and-losing side, not the already-flushed buyers, was the real target.
- His own verdict: *"क्योंकि सेलर यहां पे बैठे रह गए थे इसलिए अपना ट्रेडिंग का डिसीजन गलत बन गया"* — and the corrected plan for the next day was **buy-side**, not sell-side.
- **The honest use of the loss:** *"हम गलती को तुरंत सुधारते हैं अगली पहली दिन हमने मिस्टेक को इसको कंप्लीट कर लिया"* — he explicitly says that if this trade had been a winner he would never have done the analysis that found the mechanism.

### Friday 6 June — the corrected side, then a scheduled-event exit
- Same logic rotated: after the big up-move it was the **buyers** now seated in loss, so *"फिर जब मार्केट ऊपर गया तो यहां पे बायर बैठे होंगे"* and the plan became sell-side; then *"बायर को निकालने के बाद मार्केट ने अपना एक्चुअल मोमेंटम देना शुरू कर दिया"*.
- The entry was placed where stop-losses were actually visible: *"तो मुझे यहां पे डायरेक्टली बायर के एसएल नजर आ रहे थे उसके अकॉर्डिंग मैंने ट्रेड किया"* — a normal profit was booked rather than sitting through a three-way rotating tape.
- **He left early on purpose, because a scheduled event was due:** *"जब भी कोई आरबीआई वगैरह पॉलिसी आना होता है तो मैं पहले भाग जाता हूं"* — *"जो मुझे काम करना होता है न्यूज़ से पहले या फिर अगर मुझे न्यूज़ का पता होता है तो मैं दूर हो जाता हूं मार्केट से"*. He accepts that the market moved later without him.

### Method: how a mistake should be used
- Do not study the P&L, study the mechanism: *"आपको उसकी साइकोलॉजी के ऊपर फोकस करना चाहिए कि जब आप ट्रेड कर रहे थे तो आपने वहां पे कैसा माइंडसेट बना रखा था और मार्केट ने वहां पे क्या किया"*.
- Risk and time are unavoidable inputs: *"मार्केट में थोड़ा हमें रिस्क लेकर काम करना पड़ता है"* … *"मार्केट के अंदर एक-एक बातों को सीखना पड़ेगा और सबसे बड़ी बात टाइम देना पड़ेगा"*. Sitting out entirely is allowed, but then it is not a business.

### Evolution vs earlier
- 🔵 **NEW — THE FLUSH-SIZE TEST: whether the previous move was BIG decides whose stop-losses are the target.** The corpus already had *whose SLs are available* (2022-09-18 onwards) and *a side sitting in profit cannot be targeted* (2025-02-02), but it had never separated **a side that a big momentum flushed out for good** — *"अगर किसी ने बाय किया भी होगा उनको कंप्लीटली मार्केट निकाल चुका क्योंकि काफी बड़ा मोमेंटम हो चुका है"* — from **a side left holding a small loss** without a big momentum, *"जो सेल करके बैठे थे ना उन्होंने अपना पोजीशन होल्ड कर लिया"*. ⭐ It is also the corpus's first **loss-debrief built on this distinction**: the market *"उसको थोड़ा बहुत लालच दिया एवरेज वगैरह करवाया मार्केट को और फिर अचानक से ऊपर चला गया"*, and his conclusion is that the seated loser, not the flushed winner, was the fuel. Counterpart sessions (2025-03-02 trader-vs-trader hunting · 2025-03-30 the week that trapped both sides) describe the *phenomenon*; this session supplies the **discriminator** (size of the prior momentum) and a dated loss that needed it.

### Keep permanently
1. **The pre-open plan is a hypothesis, not an order.** Build it offline from who is seated, but let the open's actual behaviour kill it — *"वो पैसा नहीं लगा पा रहा है या फिर वो भी ट्रैप हो गया"* — and then trade the new chart.
2. **Side-less opens are not trades.** When the market opens with a big selling move that matches nothing in the plan, wait deliberately (*"10 से 15 20 मिनट का वेट"*) instead of inventing the missing side.
3. **Ask the flush question before you name the target:** was the prior move big enough to empty the losing side (they are gone → continuation), or was it small (they are still seated in a small loss → **they** are the target)?
4. **A big momentum does the flushing; a small momentum manufactures a trap.** Without a big move the losing side holds — *"उन्होंने अपना पोजीशन होल्ड कर लिया"* — gets lured into averaging, and the market then reverses against the trade you built on the *other* side.
5. **Never target a side sitting in good profit, or a side that has been selling into a retracement** — a good trader may be seated there.
6. **Two or three indices are a check, not decoration:** when only one index breaks down (or out) while the others hold support (or resistance), the break is the failure and the holder continues — use it to select the instrument **and** to exit.
7. **Exit when the read that justified the entry stops holding** — the Wednesday sell was closed green precisely because Nifty and Sensex refused to confirm Bank Nifty's breakdown.
8. **Be flat before a known scheduled event** (RBI policy, scheduled news). News is not tradeable intraday; leaving first is the executable version of that rule.
9. **Debrief the mechanism, not the P&L:** *"आपको उसकी साइकोलॉजी के ऊपर फोकस करना चाहिए"* — and fix it on the very next day's plan.
10. **Nobody is exempt from the work:** 2–3-minute decisions, mistakes that must be studied, and *"सबसे बड़ी बात टाइम देना पड़ेगा"*.
