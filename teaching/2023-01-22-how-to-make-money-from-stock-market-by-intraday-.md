# 2023-01-22 — Teaching: How To Make Money from Stock Market by Intraday Hunter

Educational notes only — not SEBI advice. Your risk rules win.

## Teaching
**Video:** How To Make Money from Stock Market by Intraday Hunter — Sunday session, 22 JAN 2023
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v94zUtFtNNE
**Transcript:** Hindi ASR of `v94zUtFtNNE` (2304 words, ~10:47) — `transcripts/2023-01-22-teaching-v94zUtFtNNE.md`

### Core idea
The session has no new chart technique. It answers *"how do you actually make money here?"* with a
**process audit** of his own trades, and the answer has two legs:

1. **A REASON for every trade** — *"आप मार्केट के अंदर ट्रेड क्यों करते हो, आपको रीजन आईडेंटिफाई
   करना पड़ेगा"*. The reason is what converts a result into learning: a loss tells you what you did
   wrong, a profit tells you what you did right — and *that* knowledge, not the P&L, is what becomes
   **experience**. *"जो नॉलेज आपके पास होगा यही आने वाले टाइम में आपका एक्सपीरियंस बन जाएगा."*
2. **CAPITAL first** — the first rule is preserving capital by following trading rules, because
   *"अगर आपके पास कैपिटल ही नहीं होगा तो आपके पास एक्सपीरियंस कैसा बनेगा"*. Without capital there
   is no experience; watching charts without trading builds nothing.

Everything else in the session is either a worked example of leg 1 or an enforcement rule for leg 2.

### Two trading methods (the taxonomy he opens with)
He frames the whole thing as two methods — both require risk-reward and discipline; they differ in
where the edge comes from.

| Method | What it is | Where the edge comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Own method (condition-based)** | Count the *non-trend* states — triangle, straddle, an event ahead, a big momentum building. Buy on that knowledge; exit when **risk** is hit, exit when **reward** is hit. | The condition. *"डिसिप्लिन के साथ काम करोगे तो मार्केट के अंदर इनकम जनरेट हो जाएगा."* |
| **2. Direction method (build position)** | Find out *where* the market is going and *how far* — up-target / down-target — then build the position accordingly. | **Experience.** *"जितना आपके पास एक्सपीरियंस होगा उतना ही आप मार्केट को सही से प्रिडिक्ट कर पाओगे."* |

Both still carry risk-reward and discipline; method 2 is explicitly **accuracy-limited by experience**,
which is why he does not present it as the beginner's route.

⚠️ **ASR**: *"जो भी नॉन ट्रेड होती है"* is rendered unclearly — read as **non-trend** states (triangle /
strangle-straddle / event / building momentum). If it is in fact *"नॉन-ट्रेंड"* the meaning holds; do
not build a rule on a broader reading.

### The complaint he is answering
*"सर हमने तो 2 साल मार्केट को दे दिए, 3 साल हो गए, लेकिन मार्केट में अभी भी हमें ट्रेड करना नहीं
आता … हमने ट्रेंडलाइन पर भी काम किया, सपोर्ट रेजिस्टेंस पर भी काम किया, इंडिकेटर पर भी काम किया,
लेकिन कुछ भी काम नहीं कर"*. His diagnosis is **not** that the tools are wrong — it is that the trader
never identified the **reason**, so nothing ever converted into experience and the same mistake ran
on repeat: *"कभी प्रॉफिट में जाओगे, कभी लॉस में जाओगे"*.

### The LOSS case (worked, his own trade)
He reconstructs a sell trade he took and lost, and narrates the mistake in his own words.

- **The reason he sold:** upside had **stop-losses parked** above *("अपर साइड यहाँ पे स्टॉप-लॉस पड़े थे
  तो ये अपनी एक प्रोबेबिलिटी को बढ़ा रहा था")*; Nifty looked **negative**, had **taken resistance**, and
  had built a small range — *"अगर रेंज का ब्रेकडाउन होता है तो मार्केट में अच्छा खास डाउनफॉल देखने को
  मिल सकता है"*; candles were decent.
- **What he ignored:** on **Bank Nifty** there was **support** and the index could just as easily go
  **up**: *"बैंक निफ्टी में यहाँ पे सपोर्ट है, ऊपर भी जा सकता है"*. And he had taught himself the opposite
  habit — *"आपको ध्यान होगा मैंने आपको क्या बोला था कि आपको बैंक निफ्टी का चार्ट देखने की आवश्यकता नहीं
  है; अगर हमने सेलिंग का ट्रेड बनाया तो कम्पलीट अपना निफ्टी के ऊपर भरोसा है"*.
- **The mistake, as he names it:** he **pre-identified** the outcome — *"हमने पहले ही आईडेंटिफाई कर लिया
  था कि निफ्टी ज्यादा गिरेगा"* — and then, when the market came **back near resistance and looked like
  breaking out again**, the setup he had relied on dissolved.
- **The exit:** *"हमारा ₹1 लाख का लॉस हो जाता, ₹80,000 में निकल गए तो कोई बड़ी बात नहीं होती, अच्छी बात
  है — ये हमारे लिए ₹20,000 बच गए"*. Taking the smaller loss is presented as a **win**, not a defeat.
- **The counterfactual:** *"अगर हम थोड़ा सा सोच लेते कि एक इंडेक्स जो पॉजिटिव नजर आ रहा [है] उसमें
  [बाय कर लेते], निफ्टी को हम इग्नोर कर देते, तो ऐसे में हम बच सकते थे."*

### The index-pair material (restated + extended)
He explicitly says he is **repeating** what he taught earlier (*"मैंने आपको इंडेक्स के बारे में दो-तीन
बातें बता रखी हैं"*), then adds one more:

- 🟢 If one index is **taking resistance** and another is **breaking out** → the one taking resistance
  keeps taking resistance, the breakout one **fails** (*"फेल हो जाएगा"*).
- 🟢 If one index is **positive** and one **negative** → the positive one **holds itself positive**, the
  negative one **stays negative**.
- 🟡 **New condition added:** *"जब कोई भी इंडेक्स ज्यादा ही पॉजिटिव हो जाए या फिर ज्यादा ही नेगेटिव हो
  जाए तब वह दूसरा इंडेक्स उसको कुछ न कुछ फॉलो करना शुरू कर देगा — कम्पलीट फॉलो नहीं करेगा, लेकिन कुछ
  फॉलो करना शुरू कर देगा."* Extreme divergence is treated as a **condition that breaks**, not a state
  to keep leaning on.
- 🟡 **The execution corollary:** from this trade he takes the practical form — **act on the index that
  shows the trade's side, and ignore the other one.** *"एक इंडेक्स जो पॉजिटिव नजर आए उसमें बाय कर लेते
  हैं, निफ्टी को हम इग्नोर कर देते हैं."* In the loss above, that would have been **buy Bank Nifty**, not
  sell Nifty.

### The WIN case (worked) — expiry-day flush of the call writers
- It was an **expiry day**, and the reason was structural: *"हमें पता था कि जैसे ही मार्केट नीचे जाएगा,
  यहाँ पे कॉल राइटर्स होंगे … तो मार्केट उनको निकलने के लिए आएगा."* The move exists because of the
  **writers' obligation**, and the flush of those writers is the trade.
- He **waited** for the market to actually arrive, then sold, then **booked the target**, and — this is
  the part he stresses — he booked a **small** target, not a big one:

  > *"अगर आप एक दिन पीछे जाओगे तो हमने यहाँ पर सेलिंग का ट्रेड बनाया था, टारगेट हो चुका था, लेकिन
  > लालच के कारण टारगेट अचीव नहीं किया और यहाँ पर अपना लॉस हो गया — तो आगे मैं क्या ध्यान रखना चाहिए
  > कि लालच नहीं करना."*

  So the **size of yesterday's mistake set the size of today's target**. Greed on day *n* is paid for by
  banking early on day *n+1*.
- Second application of the same lesson: a level where *"अगर सेल आने की कोशिश करें तो मार्केट यहाँ से
  सपोर्ट ले सकता है"* → he booked rather than held, *"अगर टारगेट बुकिंग नहीं करते तो यहाँ पे भी कुछ न
  कुछ लॉस लेके निकलते"*.
- His summary: *"अब ट्रेडिंग में आपने भी किया होगा, लेकिन आप यहाँ पे पैसा नहीं बना पाए होंगे — लेकिन
  अगर आपने यहाँ पे कुछ न कुछ लॉस बुकिंग किया होगा, तो नेक्स्ट [दिन] आपने समझदारी से काम किया होगा."*

### The enforcement rules (his own closing list)
*"क्वांटिटी साइज़ को सही से मैनेज करो, लॉस को सही से लो, ओवर-ट्रेडिंग से दूर रहो, रिस्क-रिवॉर्ड मेंटेन
करो, डिसिप्लिन के साथ अपने सेटअप को फॉलो करो"* + **do not forget the lesson**. And, near the end, the
scope warning he repeats *"दोबारा से"* — **option trading needs no direct jump**; the ladder is process
first.

### Evolution vs earlier
- 🟢 **STABLE — stops as fuel / stops as probability.** *"अपर साइड स्टॉप-लॉस पड़े थे तो ये अपनी एक
  प्रोबेबिलिटी को बढ़ा रहा था"* — the 2022-01-30 founding statement (*momentum is fuelled by stops; the
  stop you hunt is always someone else's*) restated four months after the corpus opened. First
  anniversary evidence that the spine survives.
- 🟢 **STABLE — the index-divergence pair** (first stated 2022-09-18, used as an exit trigger 2022-10-30).
  Both halves repeat verbatim in substance: resistance-taker keeps taking resistance / breakout fails;
  positive index holds, negative index stays negative.
- 🟡 **REFINED — the index-divergence rule gains (a) a breaking condition and (b) an execution side.**
  (a) *When one index becomes **too** positive or **too** negative, the other partially follows* — i.e.
  extreme divergence is a state that ends, which is new and checkable against 2022-11-06's "trust the
  non-breaking index" and 2022-12-11's positional material. (b) *Act on the index that shows the side
  of the trade and ignore the other* — this is the first time he states the rule as **which instrument
  to press**, not which index to read. ⚠️ He also quotes his **own earlier instruction** (*"if you've
  taken a sell trade, trust Nifty completely, you don't need to look at Bank Nifty"*) as the habit that
  cost him this trade. He does **not** say that instruction was wrong; he says the divergence check was
  missing. **Both versions stand; do not average.** This widens the index-family open question.
- 🟡 **REFINED — greed now sets the next day's target, not just the next day's rule.** Greed as a loss
  cause is old (2022-07-02 expiry temptation; 2022-07-03 greed → fear; 2022-10-30 comfort). What is new
  is the **feedback into target selection**: because day *n* lost its target to greed, day *n+1* was
  traded with a deliberately **smaller** target that was actually booked. Single-session — check
  whether it recurs before treating "book smaller after a greedy loss" as a rule.
- 🟢 **STABLE — expiry-day flush of the writers.** *"कॉल राइटर्स … मार्केट उनको निकलने के लिए आएगा"* is
  the same law as the 2022-09-18 writer-side expiry read and the 2022-10-08 queue logic: the move exists
  because of an **obligation**. Here it is used as the *reason field* of a trade, which is the session's
  own theme.
- 🟢 **STABLE — segment progression / no direct jump into options.** 2022-05-08's *cash/futures/index
  first, options afterwards* is repeated with unusual emphasis (*"ऑप्शन ट्रेडिंग स्पेशली बोल रहा हूं,
  दोबारा से बोल देता हूं — ऑप्शन ट्रेडिंग में डायरेक्ट जंप नहीं करना है"*).
- 🔵 **NEW — the two-method taxonomy, and it re-opens the scope question.** This is the corpus's first
  explicit split into (1) **condition-based own method** and (2) **direction-prediction → build
  position**. Method 2 is *not* described as intraday-only and is exactly the "find the direction, build
  the position" language of the positional material — while he still talks about income generation and
  intraday execution. ⚠️ **Do not resolve this.** It sits beside open question #12 (2022-01-30's
  intraday-only scope vs 2022-11-12's positional allowance) and **widens** it a third time; the label
  "method" changes nothing about the object (what the method covers).
- 🟢 **STABLE — capital first, over-trading, size, taking losses.** The standing enforcement list of
  2022-07-02 / 2022-09-25 / 2022-10-22 / 2023-01-01, now stated as the *precondition for experience*
  rather than as risk control: capital → you can trade → trading produces emotions → handling those
  emotions is the experience. That causal chain is worth keeping even though no element of it is new.
- ⚠️ **Nothing contradicts anything.** One self-cited rule is corrected by a missing check (above); no
  teaching is withdrawn in this session.

### Keep permanently
1. **A trade without a stated reason teaches nothing.** Write down *why* before the entry — the reason
   is what turns both a loss and a profit into experience; without it the same mistake just repeats.
2. **Capital is the precondition for experience, not the reward for it.** Preserve capital by rules
   first; a trader without capital cannot buy the repetitions that make skill.
3. **Watching charts is not experience.** *"अगर आप केवल चार्ट को देखते रहोगे तो बात नहीं बनेगी"* — take
   small trades; small trades are how emotions get produced and then handled.
4. **Decide the segment by the capital you have** — *"आपके पास कैपिटल कितना है, उसके अकॉर्डिंग ट्रेडिंग का
   सेगमेंट डिसाइड करके वहाँ पे काम करना पड़ेगा."*
5. **A trade side must be confirmed by the index that shows that side.** Extreme divergence (one index
   far more positive/negative than the other) is a state that **breaks** — the lagging index starts to
   follow — so do not press a read that depends on the divergence surviving.
6. **Exit at the level you planned, not at the price you hoped for.** Taking a smaller loss than the
   position's full risk (*₹80,000 instead of ₹1 lakh*) is a **win** — he states it plainly: *"ये हमारे
   लिए ₹20,000 बच गए."*
7. **Greed on today's trade sets the size of tomorrow's target.** If a target was hit and not booked,
   the correction is to *bank early* next time, not to look for a bigger one.
8. **Expiry moves exist because of the writers' obligation.** Read the day from the writer's side (who
   must act, and where), then wait for the market to **arrive** before selling — the arrival, not the
   anticipation, is the entry.
9. **The enforcement list is short and non-negotiable:** manage quantity size, take losses properly,
   stay away from over-trading, maintain risk-reward, follow the setup with discipline — and **do not
   forget the lesson** (*"इसका भूलना नहीं है"*).
10. **Follow the process, do not jump it** — and specifically: *no direct jump into option trading*.
    Process learned in order is what produces the experience; jumping sends you back.
