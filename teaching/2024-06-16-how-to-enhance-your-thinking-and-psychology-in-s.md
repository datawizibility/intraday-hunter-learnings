### Core idea
- The session is on **enlarging the "thinking circle"** — *"हमारा जो थिंकिंग सर्कल है, जितना बड़ा होगा उतना ही हम ट्रेडिंग में अच्छा कर पाएंगे."* Best-in-market means commanding **timeframe, entry, exit, profit-booking and loss management** together.
- ⭐ **The framework: the market as a BATTLEFIELD (युद्ध का मैदान).** Split it into two halves — *"एक में बायर रहते हैं, एक में सेलर रहते हैं; बायर सेलर के ऊपर हमला करते हैं, सेलर बायर के ऊपर."*
- To see **who is winning you must step back** so both armies are in one view — *"दूर होकर देखना पड़ेगा, तभी आपको एक नज़र में दोनों सेनाएँ नज़र आएंगी."* ⇒ in charts: **use a BIG timeframe for structure/trend, never a small one.**

### The GENERAL (सेनापति) ZONES — the session's central device
- Players here are the **सेनापति (generals)** — the strong fighters. Overall one army may be advancing, yet *"एक पार्टिकुलर एरिया होता है जो ज्यादा आगे जा रहा होता है"* — a **seller's general** can sit in a specific zone and **push an advancing buyer army back.**
- **Seller's general zone = the RESISTANCE level.** *"मार्केट ऊपर जा रहा था, लेकिन किसी रेजिस्टेंस लेवल के आसपास पहुँच गया, तो वहाँ सेनापति आ जाएगा"* ⇒ rejection, even for strong buyers.
- **Buyer's general zone = the SUPPORT level.** *"मार्केट ऊपर जा रहा है, फिर एक सपोर्ट लेवल के आसपास आ चुका है, तो वहाँ बायर का सेनापति बैठा मिलेगा — वो और भी तेजी से पुश करेगा."*
- ⭐ **So: the big timeframe shows the WAR (trend); the small timeframe shows the GENERALS (the entry zones).** *"जब आपको इस प्रकार के एरिया देखने हो … तो आपको छोटे टाइम फ्रेम पे आना पड़ेगा … बड़े टाइम फ्रेम पे आप एंट्री करोगे तो यह बातें समझ में नहीं आएँगी."*

### TARGET SIZE depends on WHERE you entered (a new, concrete rule)
| You entered… | Overall context | Target |
|--------------|-----------------|--------|
| at a **support zone**, sitting with the **buyer's general**, market moving up | with the trend | **BIG target** |
| at a **resistance zone**, against the **seller's general**, market moving up | against the trend | **SMALL profit** |

- His words: at support, *"आपका जो टारगेट होगा वो हमेशा बड़ा होना चाहिए"*; at resistance, *"छोटा प्रॉफिट बुक करना है … बड़ा टारगेट सोचो तो गलत हो जाएगा."*
- **Why the small target at resistance:** the overall army is the buyer's and *"वो क्या कर सकती है — सेनापति को भी हरा सकती है"* ⇒ a loss becomes possible there; at support (with the advancing army) the loss chance is low and the target can be large.

### The week worked out (10–14 June 2024)
- **Structure read (big timeframe):** a **buyer's general** had come in with a **double bottom** and taken the market up ⇒ **buyers winning**; then the market reached a **resistance zone** and gave **repeated rejections** ⇒ a **seller's general** is seated.
- ⭐ **The honest scorecard:** *"इस वीक हमने लॉस भी ज्यादा झेले — हमने पाँच ट्रेड किए, जिसमें दो ट्रेड अपने लॉस में चले गए, और वो भी कंटिन्यू चले गए"* — because he held **one direction (selling)** as the market kept offering only that option.
- **Mon 10-Jun plan:** *gap-up OR even gap-down ⇒ SELL* (at the 50,000 level, a seller's general with money power will bring it down on a gap-down, and will attempt it even on an up-open). **Flat ⇒ stay away.** *"फ्लैट में हमें पता नहीं लगेगा कि सेलर का सेनापति बैठा या नहीं बैठा"* — a flat open also keeps the **breakout** possible, and the buyer is still putting money in. The market opened **flat → gave a breakout → then he sold** on the ~50,200 re-test. He notes: *"जो रिटेल सेलर होते हैं वो तो भाग जाते हैं इस प्रकार के मोमेंटम में, लेकिन जो अच्छा पैसा वाला होता है वो अगले स्ट्राइक में फिर से आ जाता है."*
- **Mid-week:** the market fell and rose again — his plan repeated: gap ⇒ sell, *"जितना ज्यादा गैप ओपन होगा उतना ही अच्छा,"* even gap-down ⇒ sell (a good seller's general can drop it). The next day a gap-down came, he tried selling, **the market turned** — because it had come into a **support zone** with the buyer's general, and the overall army was the buyer's ⇒ **loss**. *"ट्रेंड पॉज़िटिव है, वहाँ पर सेलिंग करोगे तो हो सकता है नुकसान हो जाए."*
- **Then it worked:** a small gap-up opened and gave an open rejection ⇒ *"क्लियर हो चुका था कि सेलर का सेनापति बैठा है और दोबारा से अपना पैसा लगा रहा है"* ⇒ he **sold and made good money** — but **kept the target small** (he had to break the strike; the market did not give much). Note the mechanism he gives: **after a breakout only buyers come in, and the good general sells.**
- **His counterfactual:** had he worked on the big timeframe — *"मार्केट कंटिन्यू ऊपर जा रहा है, आज गैप अप खुला, चलो मैं बाय कर लेता हूँ"* — he'd have been hit; on the small timeframe the seller's general was visible.
- **Fri 14-Jun setup (support at 49,800):** plan = **good gap-up (~100) ⇒ BUY** (a support taken well implies the buyer's general is coming, hence a gap must appear); or **gap-down ⇒ BUY** *if positive momentum appears right at the open*; or **big gap ⇒ BUY**; **flat ⇒ do not buy** (flat can also turn into selling).

### Why the market stopped where it did (the stop-pool close)
- *"मार्केट ने सपोर्ट लिया, 50000 के ऊपर गया, आसपास के सेलर निकाल दिए; फिर सपोर्ट लिया, फिर से सेलर आए, उनको भी निकाल दिया; फिर 49,000 का सपोर्ट लेके ऊपर क्रॉस किया — इसके ऊपर सेलर के ज्यादा एसएल अवेलेबल नहीं थे, इसलिए ये मार्केट रुक गया; अदरवाइज़ सेलर के एसएल पड़े होते तो मार्केट कंप्लीट भाग जाता."* ⇒ **the market stops when the target pool runs out of stop-losses.**

### Keep permanently
1. **Big timeframe = the war (who is winning); small timeframe = the general's zone (where to enter).** Structure on the big chart, execution on the small.
2. **A general sits at a zone:** a **seller's general at a resistance** can push back even an advancing buyer army; a **buyer's general at a support** pushes the market up harder.
3. **Size the target by your entry:** with the trend army at a **support zone ⇒ BIG target**; against the trend at a **resistance zone ⇒ SMALL profit** — the overall army can defeat the general.
4. **Flat = stay away.** A flat open hides whether a general is even seated and keeps the breakout live; work only when the open reveals a general.
5. **The open tests which general is seated:** at a resistance with a seller's general, a gap-down and an up-open-rejection both license selling; at a support with a buyer's general, a good gap-up (or a gap-down that shows positive momentum at the open) licenses buying.
6. **Retail runs, big money reloads:** *"जो अच्छा पैसा वाला होता है वो अगले स्ट्राइक में फिर से आ जाता है"* — a breakout that retail chases is where the good general re-enters and sells.
7. **The market stops when the stop-pool is empty** — after each support it removes the fresh sellers; when no seller stop-losses remain above, it stalls.
8. **A right read can still lose, and holding one direction through a wrong-trend week bleeds:** he owned 5 trades / 2 continuing losses and named the cause — selling against an overall buyer army.
9. **Loss management and over-trading control are half the result** — probability must be accepted, not argued with; the winner is the one who manages the loss when the read is off.
10. **Confidence follows understanding, not the reverse** — learn how the market works and the thinking circle grows; *"बिना मेहनत के बड़ा मुश्किल होता है."*
