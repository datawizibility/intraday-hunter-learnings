### Core idea
- For a while, market momentum targets **positional buyer/seller SLs** that are already sitting.
- After those positional SLs are **exhausted**, the next momentum goes where **demand/supply is highest** — more participation → operator can harvest more.
- Don’t only ask “who is sitting?” — also ask **where will D/S be high next** so the next SL pool can be created.

### How market raises D/S (and SL pools)
- Market creates SLs where D/S can go high: **round numbers** (e.g. 58k), **breakdowns**, **breakouts** — not always where structure “looks” safe.
- **Support take → surge** activates demand → buyers pile in → later hunted.
- **Breakout / breakdown** increases quantity (people size up when they “confirm”) → **bigger SL pool** even if the move itself isn’t huge.
- **Fake breakout / breakdown** often raises activity, then reverses next session.

### Example themes from the video
- BN **58k breakout** activates buyers → next day those buyers become the target.
- **500-level breakdown** activates sellers with size → market can reverse and hunt that supply.
- After a seller hunt completes, further direction = where **more SLs are creatable** (highest next D/S zone), not where the chart “should” look clean.

### Process link to daily notes
- Inventory (“buyers/sellers sitting”) still starts the plan.
- Once that inventory is flushed, open-type alone isn’t enough — map the **next high-participation zone** (RN / BO / BD) for the following session’s SL creation.

### Keep permanently
1. After positional buyer/seller SLs are done, next momentum prefers zones of **highest demand/supply** (more participation to harvest).
2. Round numbers, breakouts, and breakdowns are SL-creation tools — they raise activity even when the print itself isn’t huge.
3. Fake BO/BD often exists to **raise participation**, then reverse.
4. Don’t only follow “who is sitting”; also ask **where will D/S be high next**.
