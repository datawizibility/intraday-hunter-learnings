### Core idea
The market is not a perfectible object: it has no rule that binds it, it does not travel a straight line, risk is involved in every window, and however good your entry or target plan is, something will still come out different. The mantra is therefore **long-term consistency instead of perfection** — be genuinely expert in **one** leg of the trade rather than demanding perfection in all of direction, entry, target and stop, and judge the account on the stretch rather than on one trade. Seeking perfection everywhere is itself the failure mode: it produces pressure, mistakes in the wrong place, and the three capital-killers (over-trading, averaging, letting the loss grow).

### Keep permanently
1. The market is **not built toward perfection** — no rule of yours binds it and it does not travel a straight line; assume that asymmetry instead of denying it.
2. Judge the account on the **stretch**, never on one trade: what you extracted over the long term is the only thing that matters.
3. Be genuinely expert in **one** leg — direction, entry, target or stop — and stop demanding perfection in all of them; one leg held well repays many weak ones.
4. One good trade is not to be let go: even after several stops, when a trade finally comes right, take a better target instead of scalping out of relief.
5. **Risk is involved everywhere** — no window and no time of day is risk-free; if a spot feels low-risk, you have stopped seeing it.
6. Do not be perfect in the wrong place: when a trade that looked good is proven wrong **forcefully**, exit — never defend a read by averaging or by sitting in it.
7. The three capital-killers are **over-trading, averaging, and letting the loss grow**; the market will mostly forgive everything else.
8. **Cut the stop on time.** A nervously shallow target is survivable; an unbounded loss is not.
9. **Seated-side read:** once a side has been flushed, the opposite side left seated is the target — and the chasers of a retracement (who book out fast) are *not* it.
10. If a losing run is only spoiling your mindset with nothing readable in the tape, **do not touch it directly** — stand aside and wait for a readable situation.
