### Core idea
- The question answered: when trading **Bank Nifty**, must you also track **Nifty / Nifty Fin / Sensex**? Normally the Bank Nifty chart should suffice — *"बैंक निफ्टी का चार्ट इनफ होता है"* — **but** the answer does not depend on your own opinion: **it depends on what the crowd is doing.** *"डिपेंड करेगा कि जो मार्केट में काम कर रहे हैं, वो ज्यादातर लोग कैसे काम कर रहे हैं."*
- ⭐ **Never read the market from your own perspective.** *"कभी भी हमारे अपने प्रॉस्पेक्टिव से मार्केट को नहीं देखना चाहिए."* The market is a **crowd** — *"क्राउड वर्क की तरह"* — buyers and sellers together, one person's money going to another; the setup is built from **what the others are thinking**, not from *"I had a resistance and it broke out, so I should buy."*

### Never build a setup from your own perspective
- The mistake: reading a **breakout of *your* resistance** as *"अब तो बाय करना चाहिए"*. On your own perspective the setup *"सही से काम नहीं कर पाएगा."*
- The correct question, always: *"दूसरे लोग मार्केट को देखकर क्या सोच रहे होंगे?"* When this momentum / breakout / breakdown happened, what did the crowd do?
- **Because the crowd watches cross-charts, you must too:** *"बैंक निफ्टी में जो भी ट्रेडर काम कर रहा होता है, वो नॉर्मली निफ्टी का चार्ट देख ही रहा होता है; और निफ्टी में काम कर रहा है तो सेंसेक्स का चार्ट देख ही रहा होता है."* So watch both — **and if the crowd watched only Bank Nifty, you would not need Nifty.** The decision is the crowd's, not yours.

### Cross-index SL-hunting — where to aim
Worked example (his chart): **Nifty 50 made a big upside momentum; Bank Nifty showed only a small momentum.**
1. A **money-making trader** buys where it is moving → he buys **Nifty 50** (already up).
2. A **losing trader** sees Nifty premiums as **expensive** (*"प्रीमियम महंगे मिल रहे हैं"*) → he turns to **Bank Nifty** and buys the **CALL side** (cheaper premium).
3. ⇒ To **target the call side**, you hunt **in Bank Nifty** — *"यहाँ बैठा है कॉल वाला"* — **not** in Nifty. You can only see this by watching **both** charts.

- **The stop-clearing read:** after Nifty's big momentum, the **put sellers** seated near Bank Nifty got **scared** and exited — *"जब एक इंडेक्स ज्यादा बढ़ता है, तो डर पैदा कर देता है"* — so their **SLs cleared** without needing a big move in Bank Nifty.
- **⇒ Prediction:** Bank Nifty now has **call buyers seated** and **no nearby seller SLs left** above → the next step is **flat-to-gap-down** → go **down and take the call buyers' stops**.
- **Mirror / refute case:** if the operator knows the sellers' SLs **did NOT clear** despite the momentum, then he prints a **gap-up** instead — *"हमें गैप भी चाहिए होता"*. In that case **wait** and rebuild the psychology **after a breakout**; do not act on the earlier read.
- **Reverse example:** if Nifty had **fallen** while Bank Nifty stayed up, the Bank Nifty sellers would **not** cut (Nifty is weak) → put buyers do not arrive, sellers do not exit → **gap-up**.
- **The clean conditional:** *"एक रिजेक्शन आया, उसके बाद ऊपर चला गया ⇒ बायर बैठे हैं ⇒ मार्केट इनके सेलर खत्म कर चुका है"* → then look for a **flat / gap-down** and **sell**.

### Round number + all-time-high caution
- **52,500 was held as exact support**, then the market **continued up without any retracement** → **buyers seated**.
- But at a **round number broken + all-time-high area**, the crowd psychology is *"यहाँ से गिरेगा, यहाँ से गिरेगा"* — and *"जब तक ऐसा सोच रहे, मार्केट बढ़ता जा सकता है."* So a **direct sell there is not the right entry**; instead: on **gap-up / flat ⇒ try buying**, and on a **gap-down ⇒ target the buyers**.
- **His own hedge that week:** he **bought a put** near the exact **52,000 support** — *"हमें लगा कि यहाँ एसएल ही नहीं है, मार्केट यहाँ से गिर जाएगा"* — and the market gave him a **loss**. Lesson: *"मार्केट का एक आउटलुक होता है — दो दिन की एसएल हंटिंग करना चाहता है, तीन दिन की… या बड़े लेवल की."* The **next day** he read that the market was **giving him a loss** and **switched the plan**: flat-to-gap-up ⇒ **buy**, gap-down ⇒ **target buyers** — even where SLs sat above.

### ⭐ Chart SL-hunting vs psychological SL-hunting
- **Chart SL-hunting** — the stops are visible on the chart: *"चार्ट के अंदर जो एसएल पड़े हैं, उनको हिट करने के लिए लगभग **70% से ऊपर चांस** मिलेंगे."*
- **Psychological SL-hunting** — the stops are positional/behavioural, not charted: *"~30% ऐसी जगह हो जाता है."* Example: a level like **53,500** where the market keeps **holding/giving rejection** — chart shows buyers' SLs **below**, but psychologically **many have sold near 53,500** → you must **buy and target those sellers**, *"भले ही नीचे बायर पड़े हों."*
- *"साइकोलॉजिकल जो एसएल हंटिंग होता है, वो सबसे ज्यादा खतरनाक होता है"* — it needs the **most experience** and the deepest **root-value** (रूट वैल्यू) reading.
- **Root value** = always find out **how SL hunting will be done** — chart-based or psychological — before building the plan; *"रूट वैल्यू के ऊपर जितना काम करोगे, उतना बेस्ट कर पाओगे."*
- **Risk control near an all-time high** (where chart-SLs are less reliable): take a **small trade** and, if it goes your way, manage with **small quantities — first, second, third** — instead of one large position. *"वहाँ ट्रेड अपना गलत हो सकता है, तो उसमें ऐसा रिस्क नहीं लेना है."*
- Study tip: *"एक-एक बात याद रखने से बजाय लिखकर याद रखो"* — write the details down; detail is what builds the root-value read.

### Keep permanently
1. **Never build a setup from your own perspective** — always from the crowd's: *what are others doing/thinking here?*
2. **Track the charts the crowd tracks** — Bank Nifty traders watch Nifty; Nifty traders watch Sensex → watch both while the crowd does.
3. **Hunt SLs where the seated side is** — to target call buyers, hunt in the index where the call buyers sit (the cheaper-premium choice of the losing trader).
4. **Momentum in one index can clear the other's seller SLs** → read that as *no seller fuel above* → expect flat-to-gap-down → target the buyers.
5. **If the sellers' SLs did NOT clear despite momentum, expect a gap-up** — and then **wait** and rebuild the psychology after a breakout.
6. **At a round number + all-time-high, don't sell directly** — the crowd expects a fall and the market can keep rising; buy on gap-up/flat, target buyers on gap-down.
7. **Chart SL-hunting works ~70%+; the remainder is psychological SL-hunting** — the dangerous kind; read **root value** (which kind is in play) before planning.
8. **Near an all-time high, trade small and scale in (1st/2nd/3rd quantity)** — protect capital where chart-SLs are unreliable.
9. **Write down the details** — memory of individual cases is what builds the root-value read.
