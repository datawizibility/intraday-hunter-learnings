### Core idea
- **The "number 4" is the break-even point between loss and profit — not a profit-maker.** If **4 trades run right consecutively** you move from loss → profit; if **4 trades run wrong consecutively** you move from profit → loss. He frames the whole session around *"चार नंबर"*: it tells you *when* a losing trader turns profitable and *when* a profitable trader stops being one.
- **The window is the next ~10 trades / ~10 days.** *"आने वाले 10 ट्रेड के अंदर"* — 4 right in a row ⇒ likely profitable within the coming 1–2 weeks; 4 wrong in a row ⇒ you are at risk of becoming loss-making inside the same window.
- **4 is a break-even, not a target.** *"चार नंबर क्या यह प्रॉफिटेबल बनाता है? नहीं, यह आपको एक ब्रेक इवन पॉइंट देता है."* Crossing 4 tells you the direction has probability behind it; profit comes after.
- **Consistency ("लगातार") is the whole point.** *"1 by 1 कभी हो गया, कभी नहीं हो गया — यह तो हमेशा चलता ही रहता है."* A sporadic win proves nothing; **4 in a row** does two things — it raises the *probability* you are on the right side, and it resets your **motivation/psychology** (you *feel* what profit is like).

### Why 4 — the market's baseline is 2
- **Market statement:** in any **10 trades, 2 are normally wrong** — for *everyone*, even a good trader. Reason: a news item lands, or a setup simply does not work for a day or two. *"ये तो आपका मिनिमम पॉइंट है."*
- The mirror is also true: even a **losing, greedy** trader still gets **2 trades right** out of 10 by default — a bad setup cannot stay bad on all 10.
- ⇒ **2 is just the minimum level everyone gets for free.** Raise the run from 2 → **4 consecutive** and you are now **above the average** — i.e. **above the break-even point**.
- **RR shifts the number.** He takes **1:1** here; if your risk-reward is **1:2 or 1:3**, the number of trades/days/duration needed moves up or down accordingly. 4 is the 1:1 anchor, not a universal constant.
- **Occasional wins do not count:** *"एक दिन बनाना, दो दिन बनाना, फिर भूल जाना — मार्केट में पैसा नहीं बना."* Only a **continuous** run moves the probability read.

### The ladder — trades → days → weeks → months
| Run (consecutive) | What it means |
|---|---|
| **4 trades right** | out of loss → into profit; *"आने वाले छह ट्रेड/छह दिन में भी profitable"* |
| **4 trades wrong** | out of profit → into loss within ~10 trades |
| **4 days profitable** | dependable for the next ~10 days |
| **4 weeks profitable** | *"तीन महीने तक आराम से काम चला देंगे"* — can ride comfortably for ~3 months |
| **4 months profitable** | *"एक साल में प्रॉफिटेबल ट्रेडर बन जाओगे"* — profitable over the year (how much doesn't matter — only that you stayed profitable) |
| **4 months losing** | *"प्रॉब्लम आ चुकी है"* — you must **re-analyse** the market and your setups |

- **Drop the monthly target, keep the 4-day/4-trade target:** *"महीने-मंथ छोड़ दीजिए, आप लगातार चार दिन या चार ट्रेड 10 में से किसी भी तरह बना लीजिए."* Big monthly goals *"इस महीने इतना करूंगा"* do not get hit; a 4-day run does and then pulls the whole month.
- **How it compounds a month:** 4 winning trades give you **courage** for the next 10 → 6–7 of 10 right → the next trades grow → *"पूरा मंथ ठीक हो सकता है."* Everything downstream is **psychology maintained from the 4-run**.

### Building a setup that can deliver the 4-run
- **Normal range setup:** market consolidates in a **range** → **flat open** → **breakdown** → take a **sell** trade. *(Range → break → sell.)*
- **Gap-down open:** do **NOT** sell the gap directly — **wait for the retracement** and sell into it. His reason: on a direct gap-down *"दूसरे लोग भी अचानक सेल कर सकते हैं"*; selling in the retracement lets *"दूसरों के SL"* get taken first and then the market falls again → better profit.
- **Breakout setups** are also usable, but he pushes to the next level: **read the MOMENTUM** — *"जितना ज्यादा मोमेंटम को समझोगे, उतना ज्यादा सुधरोगे."*
- **Momentum read (his live chart example, day-specific):** small **double bottom** → up → back down (the earlier buyers get no benefit) → then a **double top**. The double top came **after a breakout of the last high, a brief sustain, then a fast fall**.
  - **Fast candles invite sellers** (*"फास्ट कैंडल … सेलर को इनवाइट करता है"*); those sellers had to be **flushed out**.
  - **Continuous green candles** create **greed in buyers** (the other side's call/put position looks safe → they add) so the market **traps buyers well** and **removes the sellers**.
- **Entry gate for that momentum sell:** the market **must not go above the range/resistance level.** Once a **resistance level** is marked and the market comes back down, that resistance becomes the spot where the **SL sits** — take the setup only while price stays under it.
- **SL sizing formula (stated explicitly):**
  - If the SL sits **100–150 points** above your level → **take the trade**.
  - If the SL needed is **too big**, **shrink it by splitting the entry-to-level distance 50/50** — e.g. a **300-point** zone ⇒ place SL at **~150 points**.
  - If the SL **still** sits too big after halving ⇒ **halve the quantity**.
  - *"अगर आप प्रॉफिटेबल ट्रेडर हो तो पूरा रिस्क ले सकते हो"* — a profitable trader can carry the full risk; a non-profitable one reduces size.

### Why a trade goes wrong — his 2–3 named reasons
1. **Greed (लालच):** you **enter early** — the entry was never formed but greed pulled the trigger → *"एक ग्रेड आपको छोड़ना पड़ेगा."*
2. **Fear (डर):** the trade was **working**, a **small red candle** appeared, you cut early → you turned a working trade into a loss.
3. **The setup itself did not work.** (The only legitimate loss of the three.)

- ⇒ The fixes he names: *"थोड़ा लालच कम करो, डर को कंट्रोल करने की कोशिश करो, और अपना सेटअप ठीक बनाओ"* — reduce greed, control fear, and build the setup by **reading momentum, support/resistance and SL-hunting**, not by reaching for big targets.
- **Everything above is psychology:** *"पूरा गेम साइकोलॉजी का है, इससे ऊपर कुछ भी नहीं है. सही सोच रहे हो तो सही जाओगे, गलत सोच रहे हो तो गलत हो जाओगे."*
- **The final test:** out of 10, **how far above** the 2-loss average are you? *"बस दो की एवरेज को तोड़ना है."* Beat the 2-average and you have broken the loss/profit chain — the **loser** should be happy they crossed the averaging point, and the **profitable trader** must get *more* careful (fewer wrong trades, more focus on setup), because >2 wrong out of 10 is the warning.

### Keep permanently
1. **"Number 4" is the break-even, not the profit.** 4 consecutive right moves loss → profit; 4 consecutive wrong moves profit → loss — and the effect plays out over roughly the **next 10 trades / 10 days**.
2. **The market's baseline is 2.** Expect **~2 losses in every 10 trades** (news / setup not working) even when you trade well; every trader — good or bad — also gets ~2 right for free. **Beat the 2-average**, don't aim for zero losses.
3. **Only a continuous run counts.** Sporadic wins ("one day yes, one day no") prove nothing; **4 in a row** is what shifts probability and resets psychology.
4. **RR moves the number.** The 4-trade rule is anchored to **1:1**; with 1:2 / 1:3 risk-reward the required run length changes.
5. **Use the run-length ladder instead of big monthly targets:** 4 profitable days → dependable next 10 days; 4 weeks → ~3 months; 4 months → a profitable year. Conversely **4 losing months = stop and re-analyse**.
6. **Gap-down open: sell the RETRACEMENT, not the gap.** Selling the gap directly hands the trade to other sellers; wait for their SLs to be taken, then sell so the market can fall again.
7. **Size the SL by splitting the entry-to-level distance 50/50**, and if the SL is still too big, **halve the quantity** (a profitable trader may carry full risk; a non-profitable one must cut size).
8. **A trade fails for only three reasons — greed, fear, or a broken setup.** Greed = entered before the setup formed; fear = cut a working trade on a small red candle; the setup not working is the only acceptable loss. Fix greed/fear and the count improves.
9. **Entry gate for a momentum sell: price must stay below the marked resistance.** That resistance is the SL anchor; if price goes above it, there is no trade.
10. **It is all psychology.** Motivation from a 4-run carries the month; the loser should be *glad* to cross the 2-average and the profitable trader must **get more careful** — the game is played above the setup, in the mind.
