### Keep permanently
1. **When a trend refuses your read for two–three days, sit with the trend.** *"जस्ट उसके साथ बैठ जाओ — कम से कम हमारा पैसा तो नहीं जाएगा."* Fighting a persistent tape is optional; joining it is a decision you can make.
2. **Take no trade when both boundaries are public and close.** A known resistance above and a known support below, ~100–150 points apart, belong to the market — not to you.
3. **Do not target the side sitting in good profit.** If the seated side is deep in profit, the trade is on the *other* side or no trade at all — never a fade of the winner.
4. **A closing-price support re-taken cancels an existing bearish plan.** Re-read the plan when the level you were trading against gets reclaimed.
5. **Let a loss change tomorrow's side when the tape has not changed.** A losing fade of a continuing trend is evidence about the *side*, not only about the execution.
6. **The target level is an exit line, not a second trade.** Once the level you planned for is crossed, leave — do not stay for the next one.
7. **Hold while the profit grows; leave when it erodes.** Manage the position by profit and loss, not by a story about the market.
8. **A trend can be right for several days and then break without warning.** Expect the same tape to stop working — *"उसके बाद कोई सेंटीमेंट बना के अचानक से कोई गैप डाउन ओपन होना."*
9. **Join the trend with the risk stated out loud.** No stop-losses above means a sharp retracement is always available; the reason to accept that risk is *"कम से कम हमारा पैसा तो नहीं जाएगा."*
