### Core idea
- The option chain has exactly **one job**: to answer *is this an up trend or a down trend* — bullish or bearish momentum.
- ⭐ **But the chain alone will not give the read you need.** *"अगर हम केवल ऑप्शन चैन को देखकर ट्रेंड को आइडेंटिफिकेशन हमें नहीं मिलेगा जो हमें चाहिए होता है."* **The chart must be combined with it.** Chain without chart ⇒ a read that looks right and then doesn't work.
- ⭐ **Two expiries are read together:** **monthly expiry sets the MAJOR trend; weekly expiry sets the near-term** — and only after you know *where the writers are seated* in each. *"मंथली में जो काम होता है, अगर उसके नीचे मार्केट है तो उसी के अकॉर्डिंग मेजर ट्रेंड रहने वाला है."*
- The session is a **reader's manual**, not a strategy: first *where the OI actually matters*, then *what the two expiries say*, then *whether the written premium allows a defence*.

### What you are looking at: OI is CONTRACTS, not positions
- *"ये पोजीशन नहीं होती, ये कांट्रैक्ट होते हैं."* An open contract forms when a buyer's call quantity meets a seller's call quantity at the same strike.
- The trap he warns about: seeing a big OI and taking a position "the same way" as it. *"तो वहां पे गलत हो जाएगा."* At best OI gives a **rough estimate — "an estimate, a guess"** of the position sitting there; it is not a position table.
- Practical value: **who is seated above, who is seated below.** *"अगर कॉल राइटर ज्यादा है … तो हम ये समझेंगे मार्केट गिरने वाला है; या जिस लेवल के ऊपर ज्यादा ओआई है, वो **रेजिस्टेंस** लेवल की तरह काम करने वाला है."* Mirror: strikes with heavy **put** OI work as **support**.

### The strike filter — take 5 to 7 strikes up and 5 to 7 down
- ⭐ **Do NOT take the highest-OI strike blindly.** Go **5–7 strikes above** and **5–7 strikes below** the spot, in **both** call and put, and read the band — *"पांच से सात [स्ट्राइक] ऊपर ले सकते हो, पांच से सात नीचे."*
- **Why (his stated logic):**
  - **Very HIGH premium strikes** — the premium is attractive to sell, *but* **there is no volume**: the buyer–seller distance is too wide, so a big trader cannot work quantity there. *"बड़े वॉल्यूम में यहां पे काम नहीं होता."*
  - **Very LOW premium strikes** (its examples: 36, 27, 21) are **hedging contracts** — someone who wrote a large call quantity hedges alongside. *"जो आपको छोटे प्रीमियम नजर आ रहे हैं … ये हेजिंग वाले होते हैं."*
  - **The usable band** is where **premium and volume are both healthy** — his examples of the "proper premium" strikes: **110, 144, 180, 225, 276**, then 300s/400s-500s (ASR-rough). *"यहां पे वॉल्यूम भी अच्छा होता है, बायर-सेलर एक्टिवेट रहते हैं."*
- ⭐ **Rule of the filter:** read the levels **only from the volume band**; discard the extreme-high (no volume) and extreme-low (hedges) strikes *"ज्यादा बाहर मत जाना."*

### Call OI vs put OI, and CHANGE in OI
- **Static read:** call OI heavier than put OI ⇒ call writers seated ⇒ market can fall; put OI heavier ⇒ market can rise. On the day's chart he read them as **roughly equal, with a slight put-side tilt** ⇒ a slightly bullish lean.
- ⭐ **The dynamic read (ΔOI) is where the information is:**
  - **Call side, negative ΔOI** ⇒ *"कॉल राइटर भागने की कोशिश कर रहे हैं"* — call writers are **running away** (market is rising against them).
  - **Put side, positive ΔOI** ⇒ puts are being **added**.
  - Put adding + call running ⇒ **bullish tilt**. *"मार्केट कहीं ना कहीं पॉजिटिव ट्रेंड में जा सकता है."*
- ⚠️ **But he immediately caps it:** *"लेकिन ऐसा नहीं है, हमें चार्ट का भी हेल्प लेना होगा."* Chain-only ⇒ *"मार्केट ऊपर जाने वाला है"*; chart-only ⇒ *"यहां से भी ऊपर जा रहा है"*. Take both and the **opposite** can be true.

### Monthly vs weekly — the two-expiry read (the day's worked case)
- The day's charts (Jan-2024) had a **completed monthly expiry** and a **live weekly expiry** — and they pointed **different ways**:
  - **In the MONTHLY expiry they had written CALLS** ⇒ the market must not go completely up; the month's high **will not be crossed**. *"मंथ में ऐसा नहीं हो कि मार्केट कंप्लीट बढ़ जाएगा … कंप्लीट ऊपर नहीं जाने वाला."*
  - **In the WEEKLY expiry they had written PUTS** ⇒ near-term support below; the market can go up but not far.
- ⇒ **Net for the chart he was reading: sideways-to-negative momentum** — *"ऊपर से नीचे आ गया, नीचे से ऊपर चला गया"* — with a **cap above and a floor below**.
- ⭐ **The swing factor — the OTHER index.** *"हमें दूसरा इंडेक्स भी देखना पड़ेगा."* The second index (Nifty) had **crossed its monthly-expiry level**: *"इसको क्रॉस कर चुका है, यानी कि पुट राइटर तेजी से आने की कोशिश कर रहे हैं"* ⇒ **that** chart was a **positive trend, and its high was NOT yet made** (*"ये हाई नहीं है मार्केट का, अभी मार्केट और भी बढ़ने वाला है"*).
- **Composite rule stated:** *"मंथली में भी नीचे जा रहा है और वीकली में भी नीचे जा रहा है — फिर तो ट्रेंड रहने वाला है. लेकिन अगर वीकली में शुरू होते ही मार्केट ने बॉटम बना के ऊपर जाने की कोशिश करा है — तो समझ लेना … साइडवेज़-टू-नेगेटिव मोमेंटम होने वाला है."*

### The premium-distance test — when a writer will actually defend
This is the session's most operational new tool. On the day's put support at the round strike:
- The put writers had written at a premium of about **343** (spoken as LTP ≈159 **+** change ≈84 ⇒ ≈**343** — his own addition, ASR-rough).
- ⭐ **Until the premium returns to ≈343, the put writers are under NO threat** — *"जब तक ये प्रीमियम यहां तक नहीं पहुंचेगा, तब तक पुट राइटर को कोई भी खतरा नहीं है."* Even if the strike is broken and the market trades **below** it, a big put writer is **not afraid yet**.
- ⭐ Therefore: *"पुट राइटर मेहनत नहीं करने वाला"* — he will only start working when the premium **or** the price reaches his level. So a heavy-OI support **can break down** — *"इतना अच्छा ओपन इंटरेस्ट था, फिर भी ये ब्रेक डाउन हो गया"* — because the writer was never defending yet.
- ⚠️ **Do-not-do:** *"हम गलती से कॉल लेके बैठ जाए कि थोड़ा बहुत ब्रेक डाउन करेगा, फिर से मार्केट बढ़ जाएगा — तो ऐसा नहीं होगा."* Big OI is **not** a licence to buy the support.
- ⚠️ **And the reverse caution:** *"बड़ा सेलिंग ऐसे नहीं समझना कि हो ही जाएगा"* — anywhere a recovery is possible; *"ऐसे नहीं कि रिकवरी से पहले हम काम करना शुरू कर दें."* Inside selling you may work, but do not pre-empt the recovery.

### Fake breakout / fake breakdown — read off the chain
- ⭐ Where writers are **seated nearby** and **not afraid** (premium far from their written level), a **small breakout/breakdown is fake** — *"हमें ये नजर आ जाएगा"* — the market is not obliged to run.
- The day's chart printed **exactly** this pattern: *"गैप ओपन हो गया, फिर से गिर गया नीचे हो गया, तो मार्केट ऊपर चला गया"* — gap, dip, then up.
- ⇒ The chain converts a chart shape into a **credibility test**: *does anyone big have a reason to defend this level yet?*

### The reader's checklist he gives at the end
1. **Monthly:** where are the call writers / put writers seated?
2. **Weekly:** where are the call / put writers seated?
3. **Below the monthly level ⇒ major trend is down; above the weekly-writing ⇒ near-term opposite ⇒ sideways-to-negative.**
4. **Confirm the direction on the SECOND index** (which crossed its monthly level?).
5. Then (for a sell) **wait for the breakdown** of the level that the chain says is not yet defended — *"47500 के लिए उसका ब्रेक डाउन का वेट कर सकते हैं"* — and **never buy that level as support**.

### Keep permanently
1. ⭐ **OI shows contracts, not positions.** Read it as *who is seated above and below* — never as a position table to copy.
2. ⭐ **Read the 5–7-strike band only.** Very-high-premium strikes have **no volume**; very-low-premium strikes are **hedges**. The tradeable levels are where **premium and volume are both healthy** (his working premium band: ~100–500).
3. **Direction = call-OI vs put-OI, and ΔOI.** Call writers running (negative ΔOI) + put writers adding (positive ΔOI) ⇒ bullish lean — **but always cross-check the chart**; chain alone gives a wrong read.
4. ⭐ **Monthly expiry sets the major trend, weekly sets the near-term.** If both point the same way, that is the trend; if an expiry has been written against you, the market will not run through it.
5. ⭐ **A writer defends only when the premium returns to his written level.** A huge-OI support **can break down** if the put writer wrote far above the current premium — big OI is **not** a licence to buy the support.
6. **Confirm the direction on the second index** — the one that **crossed its monthly level** supplies the read; the other follows its net direction.
7. ⭐ **A small breakout/breakdown where the seated side is unafraid is FAKE** — use the chain to give the chart shape its credibility test.
8. **Do not pre-empt a recovery inside selling** — trade the selling, but do not start buying (or stop selling) ahead of the reversal; recovery is possible anywhere.
9. **The level set is not permanent** — as the market adds new 200/500/800 levels, the working levels change with it.
10. **Both sides can have operators seated at once** — read which side's strength is currently built, and do not work against it.
