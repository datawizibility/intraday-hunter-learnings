### Core idea
- One-line thesis: big one-way moves are not created by news or by the level — they are created to cut the other side's stop-losses. He says it outright: *"ट्रेंड लाइन और सपोर्ट से को है"* … *"किसी का पैसा नहीं बनेगा"* … *"पैसा तभी बनेगा"* … *"जब एक दूसरे के स्टॉपलॉस मार्केट में काटे"*.
- The setup he dissects is the breakout/breakdown print: price takes support, holds, then breaks — *"मार्किट में कुछ देर यहां पर होल्ड है"* … *"उसके बाद यहां पर ब्रेड डाल दिया तो अगर"* — and the break is exactly where the crowd enters, *"काफी लोग यहां पर जो रिटेलर होते हैं वह"*.
- The crowd split is stated numerically as a 90/10 majority-versus-operator split: trade with the obvious ninety percent and you do not make money — *"90% के साथ रे तो मार्केट में आप पैसा"* … *"नहीं बना पाओगे"*; you must model the minority's plan instead, *"उनके थिंकिंग को आपको मैच करना"*.
- The retail delusion is named and rejected: the market does not reverse because it is watching your position — *"मार्केट आपके पास यह"* … *"रिवर्सल नहीं करवाएगा"*; a big turn needs a large pool of positions that already exist.
- The homework is single and repeated: locate the other side's stops yourself — *"देखना पड़ेगा कि चार्ट में कहां पर सैलरी"* … *"के स्टॉपलॉस हैं तब जाकर अपना काम कर सकते"* — because that is where price must travel.

### Smart money vs the retailer — who actually moves the market
- The opening taxonomy splits the market into two sides — *"स्मार्ट मनी एक होता है आप रिटेलर-रिटेलर"* — and the smart side is not a single person: it carries many traders and many resources, *"हैं और उनके पास अलग-अलग तरह के रिसोर्सेज"*.
- The structural edge is a division of labour: the entry-taker, the target-watcher and the position-handler are different people — *"जो टारगेट देखने वाला प्रसन्न होता है"* (⚠️ *"प्रसन्न"* renders **person**) and *"लिए अलग पर्सन होता है"*.
- The retailer must do all of it alone, and that is the stated cause of the loss — *"रिटेलर अपने साइड क्लोज लूप कंट्रोल नहीं"* … *"नहीं कर पाता वही प्रॉब्लम है"*.
- His anti-anthropomorphism argument: a position of the retail scale does not turn the market — *"मार्केट आपके पास यह"* … *"रिवर्सल नहीं करवाएगा"* — the reversal happens only where many positions already sit (the ₹10 lakh figure is illustration, not a threshold).

### Why a big one-way move prints — the stated causes
- Cause one is news/sentiment — *"कोई न्यूज़ व्याख्या सेटिंग मार्केट के"* (⚠️ *"व्याख्या"* is ASR noise; plain reading is news-based sentiment) — but he refuses certainty about the cause: *"नहीं करने वाला कि मैं सौ पर्सेंट सही हूं"*.
- His method is a probability reconstruction, held consciously as a guess — *"मैं जस्ट प्राय जंक्शन के अकॉर्डिंग एक बस"* (⚠️ *"प्राय जंक्शन"* renders **probability**; *"एक बस"* is garbled, plain reading **एक guess**).
- He insists the counterparty's map is the thing to reconstruct — *"आप को समझाना होगा कि दूसरे लोग किस"* … *"प्रकार काम करते हैं क्योंकि दूसरे लोगों"*.
- The complete cause-list is given at the close: news, a price-zone built there, or too many positions stacked so one side must be emptied — *"कोई न्यूज़ बेस होता है"*, *"बहुत ज्यादा पोजीशन बन जाती है यह"*, *"पर्टिकुलर को लेफ्ट को निकालना होता है"*.
- The repeated mechanism is removal: the fast print exists to empty the trapped side — *"तेजी का मुमेंटम करके इनको"* … *"निकाला जाता है"*.

### The breakout/breakdown is a stop-hunt, not a signal
- The chart sequence he walks through is support → hold → a print that breaks the line, and the break is the invitation: *"मार्किट में कुछ देर यहां पर होल्ड है"*, *"उसके बाद यहां पर ब्रेड डाल दिया तो अगर"* (⚠️ *"ब्रेड"* renders **break / trade**).
- The crowd that sells the break is the liquidity the operators need: *"काफी लोग यहां पर जो रिटेलर होते हैं वह"* — the mechanical line-reader sells exactly where the position is being absorbed.
- He rejects the mechanical read outright: a trendline or support print does not make money — *"ट्रेंड लाइन और सपोर्ट से को है"*, and restates the rule that money is only made when the two sides' stops are cut — *"जब एक दूसरे के स्टॉपलॉस मार्केट में काटे"*.
- The mirrored trap is a top made to *look* like resistance so the crowd shorts it — *"मुमेंटम आयोग बेवजह नहीं है लेकिन किसी ना"*, *"किसी ने सेटिंग किया है उनके स्टॉपलॉस"*, then *"वह नेक्स्ट डे"* … *"व्यापक ओपन कर आखिर उम्र का स्टॉपलॉस खा"* (⚠️ *"व्यापक"* renders **gap**; *"आखिर उम्र"* is ASR noise — plain reading: next day it gaps and eats the stops).

### Expiry mechanics — why the momentum is manufactured near expiry
- The exits are separated by instrument and by clock: option holders carry premium decay and are forced out earlier, futures holders leave later — *"क्योंकि उनका प्रॉफिट आलरेडी कम होने लगता"*, *"है लेकिन फ्यूचर वाले कुछ बाद में निकले"*.
- The expiry factor is named as a reason the manufactured move pays — *"एक्सपाइरी भी नियर बाई है तो मार्किट में"* — near-expiry the positioning is heavy and the stop pool is deep.
- Where both sides hold stops he suggests the two-sided play (a spread), precisely because the stops on both sides are available — *"स्प्रेड कीजिए क्योंकि यहां पर यह वाले"*, *"स्टॉपलॉस आपको मिल जाएंगे"* (⚠️ the clause around *"डांस एंड कमेंट मार"* is garbled; plain reading is a two-sided/sticky spread trade).
- The gap-open scenario makes the expiry logic concrete on a hypothetical session: *"यहां पर मान लेते हैं मार्किट ओपन हो जाता"* … *"है 38 200 के आसपास तो फिर मार्केट यहां"* … *"मार्केट उनकी स्टॉपलॉस बनवाकर"* — the market opens, fails to continue down, the down-side operators' stops are taken, and then it continues (see the numbers section: this is an illustration, not a read level).

### Find the other side's stops — the money rule and the homework
- The money rule is restated as the session's spine: *"पैसा तभी बनेगा"* … *"जब एक दूसरे के स्टॉपलॉस मार्केट में काटे"*.
- The real question is not the shape but the inventory — how much loss the trapped side can be made to take: *"लिए पॉइंट है कि मार्केट में जिसने यहां"*, *"कितना कर सकता था"*.
- But the stops must be **seen**, not assumed — an absence of stops means price cannot run there: *"सैलरी केस्टोपुर अवेलेबल नहीं है यह आपको"* (⚠️ *"सैलरी"* renders **seller**; *"केस्टोपुर"* is ASR noise for **के stop**), and only after seeing them can you work — *"के स्टॉपलॉस हैं तब जाकर अपना काम कर सकते"*.
- The learning posture is self-sourced, not mentor-copied — *"देखना पड़ेगा कि चार्ट में कहां पर सैलरी"* — and if a trap is not clear today it need not be forced, because understanding arrives over time — *"समझ में नहीं आ रहा था जरूरी नहीं कि आप"* … *"आए उसको समझाना जरूरी है"*.

### Keep permanently
1. **Nobody moves the market for your position.** A retail-sized trade is not the reason a market reverses; a reversal needs a large pool of positions that already exist — check the pool, not your own P&L.
2. **A trendline or support/level break is not a signal and not a source of money.** Money is made only where the two sides' stops get cut; the break is often the print that *does* the cutting.
3. **The obvious break is the crowd's entry, which is exactly the liquidity the operators need.** If the trade is visible to everyone, the levels and the reads you are using are the fuel, not the edge.
4. **Trade on the minority's plan, not the majority's.** You must be able to state what the operator side is positioning for; the majority side's conviction is the thing being consumed.
5. **Verify the stops exist before you trust a level.** An absent stop pool means price cannot run there — the inventory check comes before the entry, every time.
6. **Price an option position with the clock in it.** Around expiry the option holder is forced out earlier than the futures holder because of premium decay; the same direction pays differently depending on who is holding it and when.
7. **Hold every cause as a probability, never as 100% certainty.** Assume a plausible reason, act, and if it does not fit, assume a different situation happened — never insist you are always right.
8. **Read for the other participant, not just the chart.** Naming what the counterparty will build, think and plan is the exercise; the level is only where his stops sit.
9. **The stop-hunt runs on removal.** A fast one-way print is frequently made to empty out the trapped side; a top that merely *looks* like resistance is part of the same manufacture.
10. **Learn it from your own eyes.** The chart must be read by you — *"देखना पड़ेगा कि चार्ट में कहां पर सैलरी"* — and if a trap is not clear today, it need not be forced; it will make sense later.
