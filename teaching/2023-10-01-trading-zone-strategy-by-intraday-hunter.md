### Core idea
- ⭐ **A "zone" is the price at which the market hands you the thing** — not a drawn support/resistance, a *valuation of the offer*. He reduces every chart to one question: *"मुझे प्राइस किस पॉइंट पे मिल रहा है"* — and there are only three answers:
  - **Inflated (महंगा / इन्फ्लेटेड)** — a ₹1 thing offered at ₹400–500+; fear keeps most away, but a trader with capital can take the risk, so **buy**.
  - **Discount (सस्ता / डिस्काउंट)** — the same thing offered cheap; the crowd is afraid of it, but with capital the risk can be taken, so **buy**.
  - **Fair / average (फेयर प्राइस)** — *"₹1 वाला चीज आपको 120 130 140 ऐसा कुछ मिल रहा है"* — **no benefit here**, because small-capital buyers also buy at that price; **do not buy at fair price; sell or follow the market.**
- ⭐ **The operating rule, stated flatly:** *"हमें फेयर प्राइस के ऊपर गेम नहीं खेलना है, हमें इन्फ्लेटेड प्राइस या डिस्काउंट प्राइस के ऊपर गेम खेलना है"* — **trade at the extremes, never at fair value.**
- **Why fair price is the trap:** *"फेयर प्राइस कौन देने वाला है ... फेयर प्राइस में मतलब वही है कि या तो कुछ ना कुछ ट्रैपिंग हो गया या फिर कुछ अपोजिट होने वाला है"* — the market hands you a fair price only when it is about to trap or reverse.
- ⭐ **"Stock market fair kuch nahi hota, unfair aapko sab kuch milte hain"** — *"स्टॉक मार्केट में फेयर कुछ नहीं होता, अनफेयर आपको सब कुछ मिलते है."*
- **His proof-of-concept on his own chart:** a stock he had watched at ₹1 that broke out and ran to ₹550 — *"ब्रेकआउट होते हु 550 पे पहुंच गया, आप बाय करके देख लेना वो ट्रैप कर देगा, वो आपको नहीं जाने देगा"* — while the *same* thing at ₹800 (inflated/cheap in his frames) *"1000 का होते टाइम नहीं लगाएगा."*

### The three zones and what to do in each
| Zone | What the market is offering | Plan |
|------|-----------------------------|------|
| Discount (सस्ता) | price below value, crowd afraid | **BUY** — take the risk with capital |
| Inflated (महंगा) | price far above value, crowd afraid | **BUY** — something is clearly happening there |
| Fair / average (फेयर) | price *at* value — everyone can buy | **SELL** (or follow the market) — never buy |

- **Capital is the permission slip.** The one who can size down and risk can buy the discount and the inflated; the small-capital buyer is the one who buys the *fair* price and becomes the fuel: *"मान लेते 1000 स्टॉक लेने थे यहां पे मैं 100 स्टॉक ले लूंगा"* — the risk-taker keeps his risk small.
- ⭐ **Circular confirm:** if there is a risk-taker at the discount, the market is going up — *"जो रिस्क लेने वाला है, क्योंकि ... मार्केट बढ़ने वाला है."*

### Where the plan-by-open sits (chart is continuous selling)
- Plan read from one chart, three open-types, three answers (day-specific to this chart, but the *method* is the lesson):
  - **Gap-down** → *"बाइंग के लिए अपना प्लान रख सकते हैं"* — **buy plan**.
  - **Gap-up** → *"बाइंग के लिए अपना प्लान रख सकते हैं"* — **buy plan**.
  - **Opening below the resistance** → *"सेलिंग के लिए अपना मार्केट के अंदर प्लान रख सकते हैं"* — **sell plan**.
- A worked example: after a volatile up-move, a *large gap-down* offers an inflated price → sellers who wanted to sell there could not sell → market can fall; and when it later comes to a **discount**, the market went **up** — *"जैसे डिस्काउंट पे मिला, मार्केट ऊपर निकल गया."*

### Zone definition — Volume Profile → the fair-price level
- He uses **Volume Profile** to *locate* the fair-price zone (this is the bridge from the 2023-09-24 session into today's valuation framing):
  - *"वॉल्यूम प्रोफाइल से मुझे ये पता चल जाता है कि किस पर्टिकुलर प्राइस के ऊपर कितना वॉल्यूम आ चुका है"* → that high-volume red line is the **fair-price line**.
  - ⭐ **The rule he derives:** the *next* resistance visible on the chart beyond the volume profile becomes the zone's edge → *"जहां पे फेयर प्राइस या एक एवरेज प्राइस मिलेगा, तो वो सेलिंग के लिए जोन होगा"* — **the fair-price band is a SELL zone.**
  - He does **not** place the level on the volume profile line itself: *"इसका रेजिस्टेंस नहीं बनाना है क्योंकि ये तो प्राइस हो गया, प्राइस के ऊपर नहीं लगाना है"* — go to the **next** candle resistance.
  - **Worked proof he shows on-screen:** the resistance he had marked (≈ *x*930, spoken *"932 वाला रेजिस्टेंस"*) was touched exactly — *"मार्केट ने एगजैक्टली रेजिस्टेंस लिया."*

### Probability, not confirmation
- ⭐ *"मैं पॉसिबिलिटी के ऊपर ट्रेड करता हूं, प्रोबेबिलिटी के ऊपर ट्रेड करता हूं, कंफर्म कुछ नहीं है"* — he refuses to promise certainty on small zones and admits he uses experience/mindset to read the small ones; only the **big gaps** are self-explanatory.
- ⭐ **The fair-price discipline as a personal rule:** *"फेयर प्राइस मुझे नहीं लेना, आप अपने पास रखो — ये अगर आप अपना डिसिप्लिन रखोगे तो ज्यादा हेल्पफुल रहेगा."*

### Keep permanently
1. **A zone is a valuation, not a drawing.** Ask only *"what price is the market giving me?"* — inflated, discount, or fair.
2. **Never trade at fair price.** The fair price is where the crowd can buy too; it offers no edge and usually precedes a trap or a reversal.
3. **Trade the extremes.** Buy the **discount** (crowd too afraid) and buy the **inflated** (something real is happening); sell the fair band.
4. **Capital is the permission to trade the extremes** — size small, take the discount, and let the small-capital buyer of the fair price become your fuel.
5. **The market is unfair by design.** *"फेयर कुछ नहीं होता"* — stop expecting fair value from a market that prices by fear.
6. **Define the fair zone with Volume Profile.** The price where the most volume traded is the fair-price line; the **next** candle resistance beyond it is the zone edge — that band is a sell zone.
7. **Do not draw the level on the profile line itself** — take the *next* resistance visible on the chart; the profile line is a price, not a level marker.
8. **A fair price is a warning, not a gift.** When the market offers fair value, expect trap or reversal — hold your discipline and let it go.
9. **Probability over confirmation.** *"कंफर्म कुछ नहीं है"* — read probability, and accept that small zones are an experience read, not a certainty.
10. **Free tools are enough to learn this.** Volume Profile → Fixed Range Volume (free), applied manually to the range each time.
