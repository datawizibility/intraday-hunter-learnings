### Core idea
- A general election is a big **scheduled** event the market relates to directly. His question is not *who wins* but whether the event changes **how momentum is created at all** — *"जिस प्रकार डिमांड सप्लाई क्रिएट हो रहा है, किस प्रकार दूसरे लोग अपना पैसा लगा रहे हैं."*
- The prior election was a near-certainty ⇒ nobody had to position ⇒ **no momentum ⇒ no money.** *"मोमेंटम अगर मार्केट के अंदर नहीं आता तो पैसा बनता नहीं."*
- ⭐ **The headline — WHEN AN EVENT ARRIVES, THE MARKET STOPS WORKING BY CHART SL-HUNTING AND STARTS WORKING BY PSYCHOLOGICAL SL-HUNTING.** *"जैसे ही इवेंट वगैरह मार्केट के अंदर आता है वो [SL hunting] काम करना बंद कर देता है, फिर मार्केट क्या कर देता है — चार्ट की एसएल से ज्यादा साइकोलॉजिकल एसएल हंटिंग ज्यादा करता है. फिर हमें साइकोलॉजी के ऊपर ज्यादा फोकस करना चाहिए."*
- Consequence: inside an event regime **continuity momentum is almost impossible**; the money sits in a **direct big GAP** (up preferred), not in riding a trend.

### The straddle arithmetic — why ~2,000 points is not enough
- Setting party bias aside, at the money (~49,000) the two sides were priced roughly **~1,000-pt call + ~1,000-pt put** (*"थोड़े बहुत ऊपर नीचे हो सकते हैं लेकिन लगभग इसी के आसपास"*) ⇒ a both-sides position costs ≈2,000 points.
- So up to a ~2,000-pt move you only get your premium back — *"जितना हमने पैसा लगाया उतना ही मार्केट ने दिया, तो वहां पे हमें कुछ नहीं मिला."* ⇒ **you need at least ≈3,000 points of momentum before an event-momentum position pays.** His framing for the whole session then becomes a single question: *will the market deliver 3,000 points, and in which direction?*

### The base case — an event that is a known quantity
- In the previous election the result was nearly confirmed in advance, so money sat comfortably and no momentum came. That is why the market's **direction** must be re-derived from the **positioning** (who is seated where), not from the headline — the corpus's standing *news-is-noise* base, applied to an election.

### Chart review — where SL-hunting was still working (pre-event)
He walks three recent charts and labels each *"एसएल हंटिंग के अकॉर्डिंग काम कर रहा था"*:
- **Chart 1.** A rise with retracements, a rejection near **49,000**, **closed below 49,000** ⇒ he needed at least a **100-pt gap-up**; the next day gave exactly that ⇒ he bought calls and *"बहुत अच्छा पैसा बनाया."* His read: *"ये चार्ट भी एसएल हंटिंग के अकॉर्डिंग सही से काम कर रहा था."*
- **Chart 2.** A rejection ⇒ his plan was *either* a **big gap-up above 49,500** *or* a **direct gap-down** ⇒ buy; **inside the range ⇒ volatile, do not buy.** The market opened to plan but turned early, so *"मुझे ठीक-ठाक लॉस वगैरह बुकिंग करके जाना पड़ा"* — a real, qualitative loss (he first saw a small profit and expected a 49,500 breakout).
- **Chart 3 — Bank Nifty expiry.** With **call writers seated above** the level and the level sitting near **49,000** on expiry, a **flat-to-gap-down** puts the **put writers** in trouble ⇒ plan a **SELL**; *"यहां पे हमने सेलिंग किया भी और बहुत अच्छा पैसा भी बनाया."*
- ⭐ The pattern he is deliberately building: **one same chart kept working by pure SL-hunting, three times — then the expiry completed and the election window opened, and the same method stopped working.**

### The regime flip — the plan that failed
- Post-expiry he read the sellers as **sitting in good profit** (the market had fallen far and then been given a retracement, so nearby sellers were cleared away), and it had **closed above 48,500** ⇒ his plan for a **flat-to-gap-down ⇒ SELL**.
- The market **opened on plan** — and then: *"ओपन होते मार्केट ने क्या कर दिया — बहुत बड़ा ग्रीन कैंडल बना के ऊपर भागना शुरू कर दिया."* The SL-hunting read failed, and he states why: *"सेलर अच्छे प्रॉफिट में बैठा था, आसपास सेलर निकाल चुका था, तो मार्केट को यहां पे गिरना चाहिए था लेकिन मार्केट बढ़ गया."*
- ⭐ The mechanism he names: the chart that had been working **stopped working, and the market started working on other people's PSYCHOLOGY** — *"टोटल दूसरे लोगों की साइकोलॉजी पे काम करना शुरू कर दिया."*

### What "psychological SL-hunting" actually prints
- Once the event regime is on and people suddenly start putting money in, the tape repeats **up → down → up → down → up → down → rejection close** — *"मार्केट क्या नहीं कर रहा — उनको पैसा बनाने नहीं दे रहा."*
- Buyers keep buying every push (majority-government logic); sellers keep selling every rejection (coalition logic); the market lifts and fades each in turn. ⇒ **continuity momentum is denied because the crowd keeps re-entering into it** — *"जब तक दूसरे लोग पैसा लगा रहे हैं मार्केट को बढ़ने नहीं देंगे, और जब वो दूसरे एग्जिट करेंगे तब जाकर वो दूसरे अपना पैसा लगाते हैं."*
- Instruction: in this regime **do not do continuity trading** — *"जब तक ऐसा चल रहा है आपको इस प्रकार काम नहीं करना है; आपको डायरेक्टली गैप के अकॉर्डिंग अपने सेटअप को ढूंढना होगा."*

### Gap-up vs gap-down — his preference and WHY
He expects a **gap-UP** slightly more than a gap-down, on **operator-manageability** grounds — not on who wins:
- **Gap-up:** others feel the market got away from them ⇒ *"ऑपरेटर को आसानी होगी मार्केट को आगे भी मैनेज करने के लिए."* Money can be made even on **calls** in a big gap-up, then on puts later.
- **Gap-down:** stocks fall ⇒ the public reads it as an *investment opportunity* and buys the dip (a behaviour he says did not exist before ~2020: *"दो 2020 से पहले … इतना नॉलेज नहीं होता था"*) ⇒ *"मार्केट को बढ़ने नहीं देंगे."* A big gap-down open risks a long grind/range — *"एक रेंज के अंदर फंसा रह जाएगा"* (possibly ~2,000 pts, possibly bigger because the event is big) with **time wasted** and no clean momentum.
- His one-line verdict: **gap-down = नो, gap-up = यस** — *"गैप डाउन को बोलो नो और गैप अप को बोलो यस."* And, regardless of who forms the government: *"मैं चाहता हूं सरकार किसी की भी बने, लेकिन हमें गैप अप मार्केट के अंदर मिलना चाहिए."*

### Keep permanently
1. A big scheduled macro event can **switch the market's operating mode**: continuation/chart-SL-hunting weakens and **psychological SL-hunting** takes over — read psychology, not just level geometry, around events.
2. In an event window, expect **repeated up/down rejection candles, not clean continuity** — the crowd keeps re-entering and the market refuses to pay it.
3. **Symmetry pricing test**: when both sides are priced (~1,000 call + ~1,000 put ≈ 2,000), a ~2,000-pt move only returns premium ⇒ **demand ≈3,000 points** before an event-momentum position can pay.
4. On event days, the money is in a **direct big gap**, not in riding a trend — plan the gap setup, do not trade continuation.
5. **Gap-up is operator-friendly** (others fear missing out; the operator can manage the market forward) ⇒ the higher-probability tradable case.
6. **Gap-down invites the public to buy the dip** (a post-2020 behaviour) ⇒ the market is pinned in a range and momentum is denied ⇒ the poor case for both buying and selling.
7. An event's **direction is not the headline** — it is *which side is comfortably seated* and whether momentum can be created at all.
8. A method that works in the normal regime can **stop working the very session an event window opens** — re-check the regime before repeating yesterday's plan.
9. **Expiry + event stacking**: with a Bank Nifty expiry and writers seated at the round number (~49,000), a flat-to-gap-down is a put-writer problem ⇒ a sell; but once the event takes over, the same open can give the opposite response.
