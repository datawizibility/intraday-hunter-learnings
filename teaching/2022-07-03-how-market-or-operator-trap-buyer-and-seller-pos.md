### Core idea
- He states the reading habit before the topic: *"जब मैं मार्केट में [काम] करता हूं तो मैं आपको काफी बार बोलता हूं कि मार्केट को मिनिमम इस लेवल तक आ जाना चाहिए, इसलिए उनको टेस्ट करना चाहिए, कोई ट्रेंडलाइन बना उसका ब्रेकआउट आ जाना चाहिए"* — a chart is read by **what the level still owes**: which level must be tested, whose positions must be activated there, and whether a big enough level breaks out or down. The doc is only a *possibility* he names aloud — *"मार्केट में यहां पे इस प्रकार की पॉसिबिलिटी बन रही है."*
- Only two participants exist: *"मार्केट में दो लोग [काम] करते हैं — बायर और सेलर… किसी न किसी का तो प्रॉफिट होने वाला है."* The whole session is one question: **how do you know which side has been activated?**
- ⭐ **The three ways a trader loses**, stated up front and in this order: (1) **greed** (*लालच*), (2) **fear** (*डर*), (3) **a setup taken from a course or from hearsay** — *"अगर आपने मार्केट को ऑब्जर्वेशन करके कोई सेटअप बनाया, उसकी बात नहीं कर रहा हूं"*, but a setup built from someone else's teaching will show you the market running **opposite** most of the time. Same guru-boundary logic as the rest of the corpus, here turned into a **loss cause**.

### ⭐ The two levers — greed first, fear after
- His sequence on the first chart, in his own order: the market **creates greed** (*"पहले [मार्केट ने] काम किया, आपको लालच दिया"*), and only then comes the **fear** (*"फिर क्या कहता है, डर कैसे बनाता है"*).
- The greed was specific and he names the exact hook: Nifty was already sharply negative while **Bank Nifty was still holding**, so the obvious read was *"जो सेलिंग होगा वो बैंक निफ्टी में ज्यादा होगा, निफ्टी तो ऑलरेडी गिर चुका है."* That is the greed — a laggard that "must" catch up. After the hook came **continuous selling**.
- ⭐ The counter-side is described as **deliberate holding, then instant conversion**: the other side *"अपनी इच्छा को होल्ड कर रहा था… लेकिन जब मार्केट में कंटिन्यू नेगेटिव सेलिंग देखने को मिली, उसने तुरंत अपनी पोज़िशन बनाना शुरू कर दिया."* Greed creates the crowd; the move is built only once the crowd is in.
- His one-line diagnosis of the trap: *"लालच और डर, मार्केट एक साथ [दे] रहा है."*

### ⭐ The retail sequence he walks through (why the obvious trade loses)
- Step 1 — the crowd sells the laggard (greed at the hook above). Step 2 — the market gives a **breakout of a major resistance**, with *"सस्टेनेबिलिटी भी नज़र आई"*. Step 3 — the people who decided *"मार्केट में केवल आज बाय करेंगे"* take positions there. Step 4 — continuous selling follows and *"उनका [स्टॉपलॉस] हिट हो गया."*
- ⭐ **How you know it was a trap:** *"यहां पर रेड-रेड कैंडल नज़र आ रही है"* — the red candles are the evidence, not the opinion.
- The other side of the same coin: the trader who instead **bought the support** when the market dipped also saw momentum and *"कुछ न कुछ प्रॉफिट ज़रूर नज़र आया होगा"* — and still could not hold it once rejection came. ⭐ His reason for why the rejection *had to* happen: *"यहां पर सेलर को एक्टिवेट करना था और जो यहां पर बायर बैठा था, उसको मार्केट को डराना था."* **Both sides get activated before the move** — this is the stop-inventory thesis of 2022-01-30 restated as a *sequence*.

### ⭐ Where to place a sell inside an up-move (his placement rule)
- Chart 2 is a sell taken while the market was rising: *"जब मार्केट बार-बार ऊपर जा रहा था, तब हमने अपना सेलिंग [साइड] का पोज़िशन बनाया."*
- ⭐ The rule, spoken plainly: in a rising market you sell where **(a) the sellers already in the market have run out of courage** (*"जो ऑलरेडी सेलिंग करने वाले हैं वो अगर थक चुके हैं, मार्केट में उनकी हिम्मत खत्म हो चुकी है"*) **and (b) buyers' greed has started** — buyers who made money here and now think *"छोटा-मोटा रीटेस्ट आएगा, दोबारा ऊपर चलने लगेगा"*, so they add quantity rather than exit.
- *"सभी की हिम्मत को खत्म कर दिया… उसके बाद जब सेलर की हिम्मत खत्म हो गई और बायर को लालच आने लगा — तो यहां पर जाकर हमने सेलिंग करना शुरू किया."* The entry is placed on **the exhaustion of the losing side plus the confidence of the winning side**, not on a level alone.
- Cross-check with 2022-06-05 (*"a resistance that keeps being sold is the fuel"*): the same observation, now used as a **placement instruction** for the trader's own sell.

### ⭐ The direct test of a level — rejection over reversal
- He flags the condition deliberately: *"मार्केट में एक डायरेक्टली मोमेंटम आया हुआ था… जब मार्केट एक लेवल को डायरेक्टली टेस्ट करेगा तो वहां पर रिजेक्शन या रिवर्सल दोनों पॉसिबिलिटी होते [हैं] — रिजेक्शन तो बहुत ही ज्यादा प्रोबेबिलिटी होती है."*
- ⭐ The reason he gives is mechanical: *"एक लेवल को मार्केट कंटिन्यू मोमेंटम में टेस्ट कर[ता है] तो वहां पर बायर की हिम्मत बनेगी नहीं, तो सेलर को आकर एक्टिवेट करना [पड़ेगा]"* — in a continuous one-way move the **opposite side never got its activation**, so the level has nobody to reverse from; *"इसलिए मार्केट तैयार करता है, रीटेस्ट करके यहां पे सेलर को एक्टिवेट करता है."* The market prepares the level (retests, activates the other side) because *"अगर डायरेक्टली ब्रेकआउट हुआ तो वहां पर बाय एक्टिवेट हुए नहीं — तो वो एक प्रॉब्लम होती है."*
- Which side he names as needing activation is ASR-ambiguous (the sentence mixes बायर/सेलर); the **mechanism** is what is recorded: **a level tested directly out of momentum lacks fresh positions, so the market first builds them.** Flagged in the fidelity section — the direction of activation is NOT usable from this video.

### ⭐ Big candles are stop-losses, not buying
- On the sharp prints on chart 3: *"क्या यहां पर कोई बाइंग कर रहा था? बिल्कुल भी नहीं — यहां पर [एसएल] है. जो आपको बड़ी-बड़ी कैंडल नज़र आएंगी वो हमेशा [एसएल] की होगी."* (ASR renders the word as *"असल"*; SL is the only reading that fits the surrounding sentences.)
- ⭐ **Why** — the size argument, which is new to the corpus in this form: *"अगर मुझे यहां पर ₹1 करोड़, 10 करोड़, 20 करोड़ रुपये लगाने हैं तो मैं एक ही कैंडल पे नहीं लगता; धीरे-धीरे accumulate करता हूं, फिर मार्केट में मोमेंटम करने की कोशिश करता हूं."* And: *"ऐसा नहीं कि कैंडल भाग रहा है, चलो बाय कर लेते हैं — वहां पर हज़ारों में क्वांटिटी लगती है."*
- The mirror: *"जब नेगेटिव मोमेंटम आया होगा, जहां सभी लोग सोच रहे होंगे 'सेलिंग करता हूं', वहां बाय करके मार्केट को अपसाइड ले जाया जाता है, तब डराया जाता है, धमकाया जाता है, उनको निकाला जाता है, उनके [स्टॉपलॉस] हिट करवाए जाते हैं."*
- This is 2022-01-30's load-bearing idea (*a print made of stops has no participation behind it*) plus 2022-04-02's failed-breakout case — now with the **position-size mechanism** that explains *why* the print cannot be participation.

### The target check (why a big level alone is not enough)
- *"यहां पर काफी लेवल ब्रेकडाउन भी दे रहा था… मार्केट ज्यादा से ज्यादा नीचे जाएगा तो थोड़ा बहुत नीचे जा सकता है, लेकिन ऊपर जाएगा तो मिनिमम इस लेवल को टेस्ट करेगा — तो मार्केट में हमारे 100 से 150 [पॉइंट] बन जाएंगे."*
- The other side of the check: *"टारगेट भी होता ना — अगर टारगेट सही से बैठ नहीं रहा… तो वहां पर हमारा एंट्री नहीं हो पाता."* He measures the **up-side travel** first (*"क्लोज़ का ऊपर कुछ था नहीं ज्यादा, तो ज्यादा से ज्यादा 50-60-70 पॉइंट जा सकता है, उसके बाद रिट्रेसमेंट करना चाहिए"*) and only then accepts the entry. Same two-sided discipline as 2022-06-11's 100–150 band and 2022-06-18's two-sided check.
- And the flat statement that a breakout by itself is not a trade: *"कोई ब्रेकआउट से बाय/सेलिंग नहीं होता — आपको देखना पड़ता है कि मार्केट ऊपर जाएगा तो क्यों जाएगा, नीचे जाएगा तो कितने पॉइंट जाएगा."*

### The level where both sides sit
- He explains the target level as a **double-activation line**: *"यह वो लेवल है जहां पर काफी लोग बाय भी करेंगे, [और] ब्रेकडाउन होने के बाद सेलिंग भी करेंगे."* His chart reading: *"पहले बायर को एक्टिवेट किया, कुछ कैंडल बनाए, उसके बाद ब्रेकडाउन दिया, तब यहां पर काफी लोग सेलिंग करने लगे"* — and *"तब हमें एक अपसाइड मोमेंटम देखने को मिला, जहां पर स्टॉपलॉस [हिट] हो गए."*

### ⭐ The disclosed cut — his own mistake, in his words
- *"जब डाउन साइड मोमेंटम आने लगा तो हमारी हिम्मत [हार] गई… ऑलरेडी 11 बज चुके थे और मुझे पता था मेरा मोमेंटम आए [गा] लेकिन 12, 2, 3 बजे भी हो सकता है, तो टाइम लग जाए… तो मैंने सोचा चलो पोज़िशन काट लेते हैं, अगले दिन देख लेते हैं. मैंने यहीं पे पोज़िशन काटा — उसके बाद हमें एक अपसाइड मोमेंटम देखने को मिला."*
- He does **not** present this as the market cheating him; he files it as his own error: *"गलतियां करोगे, गलतियां होंगी, लेकिन उसको सुधारना है, उसको समझना है."* ⚠️ No size, no premium, no P&L is given anywhere in this session.
- ⭐ This is a genuine **intraday timing** disclosure in the teaching corpus: **11:00 with no momentum yet, and the window he expects is 12:00–15:00** — the reason he cut was **time, not the chart**.

### What he tells the viewer to do instead
- *"आपको अपने लिए कभी भी मत सोचना कि मार्केट आपके लिए क्या करने वाला है… मार्केट में एक रेज़िस्टेंस बना, उसके according ट्रेड करूं या न करूं — उल्टा सोचो: इस रेज़िस्टेंस को देखकर मार्केट में दूसरे लोग क्या सोच रहे होंगे."*
- Time budget, unhedged: *"भले ही आपको एक महीना लग जाए, दो महीने, तीन महीने, चार महीने — लेकिन आपको ये चीज़ सीखना जरूरी है"*, and to a beginner: *"आप 6 महीने इस चीज़ को टाइम दीजिए."*
- ⭐ **The test of a candle-book pattern:** *"अच्छा [बुलिश] हैमर होता है, [इनवर्टेड हैमर]… लेकिन आज तक आपने कितनी बार देखा इससे आपको बेनिफिट मिला, और इसके अपोज़िट चलते हुए कितनी बार देखा?"* — pattern credibility is judged by the sample, not the name.
- Closing (sixth consecutive session): *"मैं आपको डायरेक्शन दे सकता हूं, लेकिन उस डायरेक्शन को कैसे करना है वो आपके ऊपर निर्भर है — क्योंकि वहां पर पैसा मेरा नहीं लगने वाला, पैसा आपका लगेगा."*

### Evolution vs earlier
- 🔵 **NEW (load-bearing)** — ⭐ **greed → fear as the market's two-lever sequence**, with the hook named each time: greed is installed by an *obvious* read (a laggard that "must" catch up; a clean breakout), fear is installed after the crowd is in. Earlier sessions had *temptation* (2022-04-02: a setup handed to you repeatedly = trap) and *psychology levels* (2022-03-20); this is the first time the corpus states the **emotional order of operations** and ties both levers to the activation of the *other* side.
- 🔵 **NEW (load-bearing)** — ⭐ **the placement rule for selling into strength**: sell where the existing sellers are exhausted **and** buyers' greed has turned into add-on quantity. 2022-06-05 said a repeatedly-sold resistance is *the fuel*; this says **where to stand** to trade that fuel.
- 🔵 **NEW** — ⭐ **the direct-test rule**: a level tested directly out of continuous momentum lacks fresh positions, so the market **prepares** it (retest, activate the other side) before the real move; hence rejection is far more probable than reversal. First statement of the *retest* as the market's own preparation (2022-06-05's "wait for the retest" was an instruction to the trader, not a description of the market's mechanism).
- 🔵 **NEW** — ⭐ **the intraday time-of-day element**: an entry can be structurally right and still be cut because the expected momentum window (**12:00–15:00**) had not opened at **11:00**. 2022-05-08 had option-buyer *time-accuracy* (10–20 minutes); this is the first appearance of a **session clock** in the teaching corpus.
- 🔵 **NEW** — ⭐ **the level where both sides sit**: one line where the crowd buys, and after breakdown sells — which is exactly why the market has to travel to it. A concrete extension of "stops are consumed in order" (2022-01-30 → 2022-02-13).
- 🟡 **REFINED** — the **big-candle idea gets its mechanism**: 2022-01-30 (*a print made of stops has no participation behind it*) and 2022-04-02 (*the failed breakout*) are restated as *"बड़ी-बड़ी कैंडल हमेशा [एसएल] की होती है"*, and for the first time the **reason** is stated in size terms — large capital cannot be deployed in one candle, so it accumulates slowly and then manufactures momentum. **You are the momentum it manufactures.**
- 🟡 **REFINED** — **the target check** (2022-06-11's 100–150 band; 2022-06-18's two-sided check) returns as a **travel measurement in both directions** before entry: how far down can it still go, how far up can it go before the retracement *should* start.
- 🟢 **STABLE** — ⭐ **stops before the move**: *"बायर को एक्टिवेट किया… ब्रेकडाउन दिया… काफी लोग सेलिंग करने लगे… फिर अपसाइड मोमेंटम, जहां स्टॉपलॉस हिट हो गए"* — the 2022-01-30 spine, unchanged, seventh-plus appearance.
- 🟢 **STABLE** — **never take the visible trade** (2022-04-02, 2022-04-23, 2022-06-05): here the visible trades are *buy the laggard's catch-up* and *buy the clean breakout of a major resistance* — and both are shown losing.
- 🟢 **STABLE** — **read the chart for the other participant, not for yourself** (2022-04-23, 2022-06-05): *"आपको अपने लिए कभी भी मत सोचना… उल्टा सोचो."*
- 🟢 **STABLE** — **pattern credibility judged over a sample**: the hammer test (*how often did it benefit you?*) repeats 2022-02-19's sample-over-accuracy rule and 2022-04-02's method-hopping critique.
- 🟢 **STABLE** — **guru boundary / instruction does not transfer** (2022-04-23, 2022-05-29, 2022-06-04, 2022-06-05, 2022-07-02): sixth consecutive session; here it is also the third named **cause of loss** (a setup copied from a course).

They are stated at different levels — one is about *which side got taken*, the other about *whether a level was prepared at all* — so both dates stand; **not averaged.**

> **Evolution verdict:** after the corpus priced capital (2022-05-08), time (2022-05-29), the read (2022-06-04 → 2022-06-18) and the player (2022-07-02), this session prices **the order in which the market installs emotion** — greed first, fear after — and turns it into two placements: *sell into the winners' confidence*, and *do not trade a level that has not been prepared*. ⭐ Durable adds: **the two-lever sequence**, **the exhaustion-plus-greed sell placement**, **the direct-test rule (a level must be prepared)**, **the level where both sides sit**, and the corpus's first **session clock (11:00 cut, 12:00–15:00 window)**.

### Keep permanently
1. **Every move needs both sides activated first.** Before accepting any direction, name whose positions were built and whose are now trapped — greed is used to *install* the crowd, fear to *collect* it.
2. **Big candles are almost always stop-losses, not participation.** Crore-size capital cannot be deployed in one candle; it accumulates quietly and then creates the momentum you see.
3. **Do not trade a level that has just been tested directly out of momentum.** With no fresh positions on the other side there is nobody to reverse from — the market will prepare the level (retest / activate the other side) first.
4. **Sell into strength only where the existing sellers are exhausted and buyers' greed has become add-on quantity** — not because a resistance "looks" strong.
5. **Measure the travel before you enter**: how far the market can still go against you, and how far it can go for you *before* the retracement should begin. No viable travel, no trade — a level alone is not a setup.
6. **A breakout is not a signal.** Ask *why* it would travel and *how many points* it would go; the shape only tells you where the positions are, never that the trade exists.
7. **Watch the clock.** A structurally correct entry can be cut for time alone — if the momentum window (his: roughly 12:00–15:00) has not opened, the chart being right is not enough; and if you cut, understand the error rather than blaming the market.
8. **Never size up because the candle is running.** Positions are built in quantity, slowly — if your plan requires chasing a fast print, the plan is standing where the accumulation is not.
9. **A setup copied from a course or from hearsay is one of the three ways you lose** — the other two are greed and fear. A setup that came from your own observation is the only kind you can defend.
10. **Pattern names are worthless without a sample count**: how often did the hammer (or inverted hammer) actually pay you, and how often did the opposite happen? Judge the shape over its history, not its name.
