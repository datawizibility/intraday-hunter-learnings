### Core idea
- One subject only: **how to hold a position through a volatile tape** — the corpus's first session named after the *hold*. Not finding a setup, but what to do with a live position where the option premium and the chart do not move together. His framing — *"ज्यादा इंपॉर्टेंट होता है जब आप ऑप्शन"*.
- The observation that makes the session necessary: a live option position can **shrink while the chart looks unchanged** — *"धीरे-धीरे प्रॉफिट कम होना शुरू हो जाता"*. Price is flat, the premium is not, and the impulse is to chase a bigger target instead of managing what is left.
- The session runs one shape: **the decay problem → the greed limit → define the loss and define the profit → why a rule is never mechanical → the two cut triggers (time and feel) → size and the stage you are at → build confidence first → do not follow others blindly.**
- **Evolution weight:** the corpus had *entry* material (2022-04-24, 2022-08-13) and *exit* material (2022-01-30/02-06, 2022-06-19, 2022-12-11) but no session on **the interval between**. This supplies two cut triggers not previously named (time, feel), a stage-based size rule and a confidence protocol — restating the 2022-06-19 paired control unchanged.

### Why an option position decays while the chart looks unchanged
- He opens on the market's condition — a run big enough that a chart can convert into a very large profit — *"है की आपका न्यूज़ चल रहा हो चाणक से बहुत"* (⚠️ `चाणक` is ASR damage; the neighbours place the point on the **option premium**, not the spot print).
- The mechanism he names is the premium moving on its own — *"विल कम या ज्यादा होने का आपके ऑप्शन के"* (⚠️ `विल` = ASR for *value*) — which is why profit starts varying while the chart gives no signal.
- The consequence for the holder: the profit is running and the chart still shows the same thing — *"वही नजर ए रहा होता है लेकिन आपका"* (⚠️ `ए रहा` = ASR for *आ रहा*) — while the profit slowly shrinks. **The same chart that says *wait* is, in the premium, already charging you for the wait** — which is why the hold needs rules of its own rather than a wider target.
- ⚠️ No instrument is named (grep: निफ्टी 0, बैंक 0, सेंसेक्स 0, स्ट्राइक 0, एक्सपायरी 0, फ्यूचर 0) — this is stated as a general property of an option hold, not read off today's tape.

### The greed limit
- He does not deny the opportunity: in this tape the greed is to aim at one big target — *"लेकिन इस प्रकार के मोमेंट में लालच यह"* … *"रहता है की एक बड़ा टारगेट अचीव करेंगे"* — and his answer to *can money be made here?* is an unqualified yes — *"बिल्कुल सही है देखिए ऐसी मार्केट में अगर"*.
- But the permission has a ceiling, stated as the session's governing line: greed may be indulged up to a limit — *"लेकिन फिर भी देखिए लालच को एक लिमिट तक"* — and beyond it the problems begin to be created — *"तो है वहां पर प्रॉब्लम क्रिएट होना शुरू"* (⚠️ carried sense; the preceding clause is ASR-damaged).
- This is a **hold-discipline** version of the corpus's greed theme: not *greed is wrong*, but *greed has a limit*, and the limit is a management instruction, not a moral one.

### Define loss and define profit
- His own method comes first and is symmetrical: he keeps a defined loss — *"अपनी पोषण के लिए लॉस डिफाइन रखता हूं"* — and defines the profit — *"अपनी पोषण के लिए प्रॉफिट डिफाइन करता हूं"* (⚠️ `पोषण` = standing ASR corruption of *position*).
- The failure is the opposite shape: the trader who plans a one-lakh loss and a one-lakh profit — *"लाख का लोन लेने वाले हैं ₹1 लाख का"* (⚠️ `लोन` = ASR for *लॉस*) — and then, the moment the market turns and starts giving loss out of that profit — *"प्रॉफिट से मार्केट पलट कर लॉस देना शुरू"* — cuts and runs — *"करता है वह अपनी पोषण काट कर भाग जाते हैं"* — because he cannot control himself.
- The rule for himself is a **line on his own P&L**, not a feeling: above or below it he will not work the position — *"नीचे मैं कम नहीं करूंगा अगर इसकी आसपास"* (⚠️ `कम` = ASR for *काम*/trade). He calls it a **definition** — *"अपना एक क्या होता है की डेफिनेशन होता है"* — it fixes *where* and *how* the position is worked.

### No trade follows a stated rule mechanically
- The blunt admission: no trade works the way a rule is recited — *"नहीं होता की आप जो रूल कोई बोलता है जैसे"*. He tells traders to keep a risk:reward — *"मैं कहता हूं की आप रिस्क एंड रिवॉर्ड"* — and says he varies it himself.
- **His stated asymmetry, verbatim:** losses taken almost complete — *"हूं वो लगभग कंप्लीट ही लेता हूं लेकिन"* — while the profit he varies — *"प्रॉफिट मैं काफी बार कम या ज्यादा करते"*. The stated reason: every trade works differently — *"कम करता है और अलग-अलग कंडीशन में होता है"* — and his trades are not transferable — *"वैसे मैं कम नहीं कर पाऊंगा क्योंकि एक"*.
- **The doctor analogy draws the line between a rule you drop and a rule you must complete.** If the doctor says the medicine is for ten days — *"हैं आपको डॉक्टर कहता है की आपको 10 दिन"* (⚠️ `दुआ` = ASR for *दवा*) — you at least follow two or three — *"दो या तीन दिन तो कम से कम उनको फॉलो"*. A rule is followed for a **minimum period**, not dropped at the first bad print — and not recited mechanically either.
- Where the position is actually built from: his timing and gut feel — *"फीलिंग देखता हूं की मार्केट में क्या हो"* — plus the candle forming — *"कैंडल बन रहा है उसके अकॉर्डिंग ही मैं"*.

### The two triggers for cutting a position (time and feel)
On *how* he takes a loss he gives exactly two triggers — both cut instructions, not stop-losses.

**Trigger 1 — TIME.**
- *"कैसे लेता हूं या तो मेरा टाइम हो चुका"* … *"होता है क्योंकि मैं अगर 10:00 बजे से"*: if the position was built at 10:00 and given two or three hours — *"परेशन बनाया तो ऐसे में अगर दो-तीन घंटे"* — with neither profit nor loss appearing — *"प्रॉफिट नहीं हो रहा है या मेरा लॉस नहीं"* — then the market starts moving on whatever chart it built — *"लगभग दो-तीन घंटे के बाद जैसा भी चार्ट"* … *"बना उसके अकॉर्डिंग चलना शुरू करेगा जो"* — and **not** the way he positioned for — *"वो नहीं चलने वाला अब मार्केट में अपना"*.
- The cut: close and leave with whatever profit is showing — *"मैं काट के निकल जाता हूं जो भी प्रॉफिट"*; lucky, it pays — *"अगर लकी हुआ तो प्रॉफिट हो जाएगा adervise"* (⚠️ `adervise` = ASR form of *otherwise*) — otherwise a loss was coming anyway.

**Trigger 2 — FEEL.**
- The second route is experience: trade long enough and a sense begins to form — *"थोड़ा-थोड़ा अंदाजा होना शुरू हो जाता है"*. You sense the market will not make you money — *"मार्केट अब आपका पैसा नहीं बना नहीं देने"* — and the feeling arrives that it is troubling you — *"तंग कर रखा है चलो निकल लेते हैं"*.
- The cut: a smaller profit — *"सिचुएशन फिर भी मैं प्रॉफिट कम में निकल"* — and he names what makes it worse: adding quantity (see *Quantity, and the stage you are at*).
- **Why no single mechanical line can be drawn:** the situation differs each time — *"के लिए ये नहीं बना सकते की ये लाइन है और"* — and he concedes some rules must be broken — *"उड़े-थोड़े रूल काफी बार ब्रेक करने पड़ते"* — only then does money get made — *"हैं तभी जाकर यहां पे मार्केट में पैसा"*.

### Quantity, and the stage you are at
- **The stage you are at decides how size is handled** — *"काफी बार क्या होता है किस स्टेज पर आप"* … *"अपनी पोषण को हैंडल करोगे तो यह तो हो गया"*. A veteran just works to his own rules — *"करो उसके अकॉर्डिंग कम करते रहिए अपना"* … *"डिफाइन रखिए जहां पर लॉस हो जाए वहां पे"*.
- **A new trader gets an explicit size cap.** If the norm is ten lots — *"क्वांटिटी में 10 लौट में कम करते हो और"* (⚠️ `लौट` = ASR for *लॉट*, lot) — then the target trade is done in **two or three lots, not ten** — *"टारगेट करोगे तो आप यह दो या तीन नोट में"* (⚠️ `नोट`/`स्टॉल` = ASR for *लॉट*).
- **Why the cap:** repeated small trades leave nothing behind — *"दिन के बाद 10 दिन के बाद मार्केट में"* … *"पैसा ही नहीं बचेगा और अगर पैसा नहीं बचा"*.
- **The simple rule he reduces the session to:** know your own condition — what type of trader you are — *"कंडीशन को समझो किस टाइप के आप ट्रेड हो"* — how far you have come — *"कहां तक आप पहुंच चुके हो आपने कितना"* — and how much loss you can take — *"कैपिटल आपके पास है कितना आप लॉस ले सकते"*.

### A big target needs a small quantity
- If you want a big target, keep **some** quantity aside — *"कुछ क्वांटिटी को रख लो उसको रख कर आप"* — and the feeling of a big target arrives separately, but confidence comes first — *"तो पहले क्या आपके अंदर कॉन्फिडेंस लाओ"*.
- **The premium ladder he gives as the worked shape:** a put taken at ₹100 and running — *"₹100 का कोई फुट लिया अगर वह चल रहा है"* (⚠️ `फुट` = ASR for *पुट*) — it went to 300 — *"300 से 40500 चला गया तो आप सो रुपए पर रख"* (⚠️ `40500`/`सो रुपए` are ASR damage for the 500 rung; read as 300 → 500) — then you sit at 500: if it falls back to 100 you cut — *"जाएगा या तो 500 से वापस से 100 ए जाए तो"* — and if it runs to 1000 you ride — *"मैं काट दूंगा या फिर 500 से 1000 का हो"*. The plan is stated with the number it is planned at.
- **Why small and not the regular size:** in these spikes a small quantity is what survives — *"क्वांटिटी के साथ इस प्रकार के कम नहीं"*. **And in this market the big target is withdrawn** — both call-holders and put-holders suffer — *"वालों का भी पुट वालों का भी ऐसे ही"* (⚠️ `ratement` = ASR for *retracement*) — so whatever target is achieved is good enough — *"बड़ा टारगेट छोड़िए आप जो टारगेट अगर अचीव"* … *"कर लोग वह भी बहुत अच्छा है लेकिन कम करो"*.

### Building confidence first
- Confidence is earned from a **track record**, not from a good read — *"है देखिए पहले तो आपको कुछ ना कुछ पैसा"*.
- **The protocol, for when the market normalises:** build roughly one trade at a time — *"लगभग एक ट्रेड बनाना है लगभग एक या दो"* — continue it for one to three months — *"महीने तक 3 महीने तक और नेट में आपको"* — and check the **net**: anyone can *do* trading — *"कोई भी कर सकता है लेकिन मार्केट में"* — but making profit must be proved — *"प्रॉफिट बनाने के लिए कम से कम आपको साबित"*.
- **Why the one-trade discipline:** repeated trading is what breaks discipline — *"क्या करता है बार-बार ट्रेड करता है"* — damages it — *"डेसिबल खराब करता है दो-तीन दिन माइंडसेट"* (⚠️ `डेसिबल` = ASR for *discipline*) — and spoils the mindset in two-three days. Put in the screen time while you learn — *"में पूरे दिन मार्केट को देखो एक-एक कैंडल"* — then check the result.
- The loop closes on where confidence comes from: nothing needs to be learned from someone else first — *"सीखना जरूरी है पहले आप कम करो एक-दो"* … *"महीने अपने अंदर कॉन्फिडेंस लाओ"*.

### Do not follow others blindly
- Watch others' trades, but follow only when you completely understand them — *"उनको देखो जरूर लेकिन उनको फॉलो तभी करना"* … *"जब कुछ उनके अकॉर्डिंग आपको समझ में आए"*.
- If the full circle is not clear to you — *"आपको कंप्लीट सर्कल समझ में ए नहीं रहा"* — and you still trade there — *"लेकिन आप वहां पे कम करने की कोशिश करोगे"* — you get trapped — *"तो वहां पे ट्रैप हो जाओगे तो ये आपके लिए"*.

### Keep permanently
1. **Hold with rules, because the premium decays while the chart does not.** A flat chart is not a reason to wait when the position you hold is shrinking — the chart that says *wait* may already be charging you for it.
2. **Greed has a limit, not a ban.** In a good tape the greed to aim big is right; set a **ceiling** on it before the position runs, because past the limit the problems start.
3. **Define the loss and define the profit as one pair, and treat the lines as binding.** Write the numbers above and below which you will not work the position; a one-sided definition is not a definition (2022-06-19).
4. **A rule is never mechanical, but it is not optional either.** Some rules must be broken for money to be made — yet his losses are taken almost complete while the profits vary, and a rule you set is followed for a minimum period (the doctor's-two-or-three-days point), not dropped at the first bad print.
5. **Cut on TIME.** A position built at 10:00 and given two-three hours with no result will be moved by whatever chart the market built — not necessarily the one you positioned for. Cut and leave with the profit showing.
6. **Cut on FEEL, and never add quantity into trouble.** With enough screen time you sense the market is troubling you; exit in a smaller profit. Adding quantity at that moment is what creates the problem, because the market then moves opposite.
7. **Size is decided by the stage you are at.** Know your own condition first — type of trader, how far you have come, capital, tolerable loss. A veteran works to his own rules; a new trader caps the target trade at **two-three lots, not ten**.
8. **A big target needs a small quantity — keep a piece aside.** Reserve some quantity for the spike rather than deploying the whole size, and ride it on the small piece (the ₹100 → 300 → 500 → 1000 shape: cut if it falls back to entry, ride if it doubles).
9. **Build confidence before size.** Make some money first; then trade one at a time for one-two-three months and check the **net**. Repeated trading breaks discipline and the mindset in two-three days — the trade is the proof, not the volume.
10. **Watch others, but follow only what you completely understand.** If the full circle is not clear to you, trading there traps you. Watching is for orientation, never for copying.
