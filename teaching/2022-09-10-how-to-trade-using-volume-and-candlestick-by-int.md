### Core idea
- His opening claim is the mission of the session: *"मार्केट में जो गेम होता है… जितनी अच्छी आपकी [रीडिंग] होगी, उतना ही अच्छा आप मार्केट के अंदर ट्रेडिंग कर पाओगे"* — and today the reading is built from exactly two inputs: **candle structure and volume**.
- ⭐ The question the session answers, stated first and then demonstrated chart after chart: *"अब हमें इसमें आइडेंटिफाई करना है — कैंडलस्टिक एंड वॉल्यूम को देखकर — कि मार्केट यहां से रिवर्सल करने वाला है या मार्केट कंटिन्यू [जाने वाला] है."* **One decision — reversal or continuation — taken from a candle plus its volume.**
- This is the **first processed session that gives a candle-and-volume *setup*** rather than using candles as one item in a list of things buyers react to. The whole thing is location-based: the pattern only counts **at a level**, and only when the surrounding chart is clean.
- **No entry, no exit, no size, no outcome, no P&L** is disclosed — the session is method only, on screen-shared charts.

### ⭐ The core setup — green candle at a level, high crossed, volume up
Stated once, cleanly, and then repeated on four different charts:
1. **The market tests a level** (support / resistance / a level it is continuing into): *"मार्केट जब [लेवल] को टेस्ट करता है…"*
2. **A green candle forms there** — the first sign that someone is buying that level: *"वहां पर अगर कोई ग्रीन कैंडल बनाता है…"*
3. ⭐ **The market crosses that green candle's HIGH** — *"अगर उस ग्रीन कैंडल के हाई को मार्केट क्रॉस करते हैं…"*
4. **And then breaks the level itself** — *"और फिर उस लेवल का [ब्रेक] डाल देता है…"*
5. **Then momentum arrives and the move runs** — *"तो जो मार्केट में मुमेंटम आएगा…"* — take the points: *"वह [ज्यादा] मार्केट पॉइंट निकालेगा."*
- ⭐ **The negative case is equally specified and is the reversal read:** *"लेकिन जब मार्केट में ग्रीन कैंडल बना, उस हाई को किसी भी [तरह] क्रॉस नहीं किया — उससे पहले मार्केट में बदलाव आने लगा — तो [कम] पॉइंट निकालेगा, या फिर रिवर्सल आने के चांसेस ज्यादा रहेंगे."*
- ⭐ **So the green candle is a *conditional* trigger**: the candle alone means nothing; **the cross of its high is the confirmation**, and its absence is itself information.

### ⭐ Whose money is behind the high — the inference that makes the setup work
He then explains *why* crossing the high carries information, in the same "who is positioned" language as the rest of the corpus:
- *"अगर किसी ने यहां [बाय का] पैसा लगाया हुआ [है] — [वह] मार्केट को नीचे लेकर जाएगा, [हाई] पर नहीं आने देगा. लेकिन अगर वह पैसा नहीं लगा रहा है… उसकी हाई को क्रॉस करें — इसका मतलब किसी ने [सेलिंग] का पैसा लगाया हुआ [है]."*
- ⭐ **Translation of the rule:** the candle's high is the line the *buyer's* money is supposed to defend. If the market crosses it, the money that should have defended it **was not there** → the position is on the other side → *"वह मार्केट को नीचे ले जाने के लिए पैसा लगाएगा"* → **negative momentum / reversal**, and the buyers who entered on that candle *"पोज़िशन में फँसेगा."*
- This is the candle-level version of the level logic taught a month earlier (2022-08-06: *a level is somebody's open position; a break with immediate selling was deliberate*).

### ⭐ Volume — the confirmation, and the entry trigger
Two distinct jobs are given to volume in this session:
1. **Confirmation that a break/level is real.** On the chart where the market came back to resistance, formed candles there and then crossed the red candle: *"जो मार्केट इस रेड कैंडल को क्रॉस करे, तो आप इसको ब्रेकआउट कंसीडर कर सकते हैं — अब आप यहां पर वॉल्यूम को देखोगे, तो वॉल्यूम यहां पर आपको इंक्रीमेंट देखने को मिल जाएगा."*
2. ⭐ **Entry trigger — the green candle's volume must beat the selling candle's volume.** *"जो भी [बिक्री वाला] कैंडल मिला, उसके वॉल्यूम से ज्यादा अगर [ग्रीन] साइड का [कैंडल] मिलेगा… तभी आपको यह काम करना है"*, and procedurally: *"जब भी इस ग्रीन कैंडल [के बाद] वॉल्यूम बढ़ता हुआ [मिले], नेक्स्ट [कैंडल] में — तभी आपका एंट्री प्वाइंट बन जाएगा."*
- **What that entry is expected to deliver, as he states it:** *"उसके बाद छोटा-मोटा [पुलबैक], उसके बाद आपको गिरावट [मिलेगी]… सौ-डेढ़ सौ, दो सौ प्वाइंट तक आपको आराम से मिल जाएगा."* (An illustration of scale, not a target.)
- ⭐ **Volume is a gate, never a standalone signal:** *"केवल एक वॉल्यूम को देखकर… [इंट्रेस्ट] नहीं [करें] — बाकी जो कंडीशन [हैं], वह भी मार्केट कंप्लीट करे — तो हम तभी मार्केट के अंदर [ट्रेड] कर पाए."* Candle, level, location and volume all have to agree.

### ⭐ Location conditions — where the setup is allowed and where it is not
- ⭐ **The chart must be "clean" around the level — no competing level nearby.** *"आपको यह भी ध्यान रखना है कि आस-पास दूसरे चार्ट [की विज़िबिलिटी] नहीं होनी चाहिए, अन्यथा मार्केट [उस दूसरे चार्ट] में काम करना शुरू कर देगा."* He checks this aloud on two of the four charts — *"आस-पास ऊपर भी कुछ [लेवल] अवेलेबल नहीं है, नीचे भी कुछ [लेवल अवेलेबल] है"* — and says that a chart with a level sitting just below or just above it will get pulled to **that** level instead: *"अगर यहां पर कोई [लेवल] होता, तो मार्केट कंटिन्यू [वहां] जा सकता था."*
- ⭐ **The market takes support *before* support.** *"कंपलीटली सपोर्ट के आसपास नहीं होता — सपोर्ट से पहले ही मार्केट सपोर्ट लेने की कोशिश करता है, क्योंकि [पिछली] कैंडल का हाई एंड लो रेजिस्टेंस एंड सपोर्ट की तरह काम करेगा."* A practical reason not to wait for the exact line.
- ⭐ **The rule is site-specific and he says so twice:** *"हर जगह आप यह [नुस्खा] काम नहीं आएगी — जो चीज़ जहां पर यूज़ करनी [है], वहीं पर करनी है."* The volume test belongs at a support/resistance test, not anywhere on the chart.
- **The reversal plan is tied to the low being taken out** (the ASR is garbled here — reproduced as heard): *"अब तक आप रिवर्सल का यहां पर प्लान नहीं करोगे, कब तक जब मार्केट इस [लो] को क्रॉस नहीं करेगा"* — i.e. the protective/confirming reference for the reversal side is the low of the structure, not the entry price. ⚠️ **Wording uncertain; see fidelity.**

### Keep permanently
1. **A green candle alone is not a signal.** At a level it means *someone tried*; it becomes tradeable only when the market **crosses that candle's high** and then breaks the level.
2. **If the high is not crossed and the market turns first, treat it as a reversal warning** — fewer points, higher reversal odds. The absence of the cross is information, not a non-event.
3. **A crossed high tells you the defending money was on the other side.** Whoever should have defended that line did not — so the market will be taken the other way and the buyers on that candle get trapped.
4. **Volume must confirm the direction, and the green candle's volume must beat the preceding selling candle's volume.** The next candle that carries that volume higher is the entry.
5. **Volume alone is never the trade.** Candle, level, location and volume must all complete together — one volume bar is not a setup.
6. **Only trade the pattern where it belongs.** The volume candle test is a support/resistance-location tool; applied anywhere on the chart it stops working.
7. **Check the neighbourhood before you trade the level.** If another level sits immediately above or below, the market will work to *that* level instead — a clean chart around the level is part of the setup.
8. **Do not wait for the exact level: support is usually taken before support**, because the previous candle's high/low already acts as support/resistance.
9. **Cross-check more than one instrument** (index and the heavy banking names) so that your chart is not repeating an already-used story.
10. **Candles and volume confirm a location — they do not replace the level work or the stop check.** The level, its position-availability test and the stop placement come first; this setup decides *when* to act on them.
11. **Build your own setup from these pieces.** Following his words alone does not transfer; the concepts are his, the setup has to become yours.
