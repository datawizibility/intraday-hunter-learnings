### Core idea
- **Success in any field = aligning yourself to that field.** Whatever the field demands, you must become that person. The doctor example: a surgeon must be **emotionally strong**, because if the family starts crying and the surgeon cries too, *"फिर ऑपरेशन कौन करेगा?"*
- Trading is exactly the same: **money-making is the goal, but the emotion you carry while the trade is ON has to be a different level.** The whole session is the mindset + the learning method that make the setups actually work.

### Money as a NUMBER while the trade is live (the profitable-trader habit)
- A profitable trader, the moment he is in a trade, treats the running P&L **as a number** — *"मेरा समथिंग प्रॉफिट चल रहा है, समथिंग लॉस चल रहा है."*
- He looks at the **real** result only at **closing** — *"जब सब कुछ क्लोज़ हो जाएगा… तब वो जाकर देखेगा कि हाँ मेरा पैसा लॉस में चला गया, हाँ प्रॉफिट में चला गया."* Before that he watches only "my number is running".
- That does **not** mean he has no emotion (the doctor has a family-mindset and an operation-mindset) — it means the emotion is **switched** for the operation.
- Getting **attached to money** is what breaks this: *"जब आप ट्रेड कर रहे होंगे तब आपको जो प्रॉफिट-लॉस नज़र आ रहा होगा उसको एज़ अ नंबर देखना पड़ेगा."*
- But **loving money is still required** — *"पैसे से प्यार होना ज़रूरी है"*, otherwise you won't give yourself the direction ("मुझे मार्केट से पैसा बनाना है"). The point is: love money **before** the trade, not **during** it.

### Money management buys the emotional strength
- You must manage money in a way that **the feeling "my money is going" never arises** — *"अगर एक बार भी ऐसी फीलिंग आई तो फिर वह मार्केट के अंदर काम नहीं कर पाएगा."* Money management is the *precondition* for emotional strength.
- The right amount is **personal — there is no fixed figure**. *"किसी के लिए एक लाख कुछ नहीं होगा, किसी के लिए 10,000 भी बहुत ज़्यादा इंपोर्टेंट होगा."*
- His rule: **trade at the level you can stay stable at.** *"अगर आपको 10 लाख में इमोशनली बैकअप नहीं दे सकते तो 1 लाख में काम करो; अगर 1 करोड़ में डर लगता है तो 1 लाख में काम कर लेना."* Working at a level you *shouldn't* be working at is the mistake.
- Growth is **staged**, like a doctor: small operations first, then bigger — *"पहले छोटा ऑपरेशन करेगा, फिर बड़ा ऑपरेशन करेगा… ऐसे बढ़ता है, रेगुलरली."* Never start at the top.
- **Practice is the only route** — *"सर ने आज बताया, कल इम्प्लीमेंट कर दूंगा"* will not happen; if it were that easy everyone would be a trader and there would be no money left.

### Learning method 1 — read candlesticks at the TURNING POINTS
- Do **not** hunt candles in the middle of a chart — *"बीच में कैंडल ढूंढते रहते हैं… कुछ समझ में नहीं आएगा."* The structure the market builds, **it builds around the turning point** — *"जो गेम चलेगा वो टर्निंग पॉइंट के आसपास बनेगा."*
- Method: **find where the market turned** (जहां से मार्केट घूमा) and study the candles there — how it formed candles on the reversal, on the **gap-up / gap-down**, and at **closing** time, across **daily, 15-min, 5-min**.
- **Use a BIGGER timeframe for practice** (5 / 10 / 15 / 30 min), not 1-min: *"1 मिनट के कैंडल का शेप कोई ज़्यादा वैल्यू नहीं है"* and you can't remember them (too many). A bigger frame gives **structure** and **grip** — you'll actually remember how the candle formed.
- Expect **10–20 charts regularly**; the timeline is **2–4 months** depending on your interest — *"एक महीने में काम चल जाएगा, यह भी नहीं है."*

### Learning method 2 — direction & momentum, easiest first
- Read direction the same way: where the market **came from** below and **turned up**, what it did when it **changed direction**, and what happened **after the breakout** — the market that came from far below suddenly went up; how?
- Learning order he gives: **first the 2–4-day momentum, then the middle momentum, then the 1-day momentum** — *"पहले यह पकड़ना सीख लो, फिर बीच वाले मोमेंटम भी सीख लोगे, और फिर एक दिन वाला मोमेंटम भी."*

### The chart example — operator's put-side entry and the SL hunt (SL-hunting restated)
- Chart walk-through: market went up → **breakout** → **buyers gathered** above. As the buyers assembled, **the same operator built a put-side (selling) position**, and immediately a **sharp sell-off** appeared.
- What the operator did was only **add quantity up to that level** and build his selling position — then **every retail buyer / good trader sitting there had their SLs hit**. *"उन सभी के एसएल हिट किए हैं ऑपरेटर ने."*
- The tell: it was **not simply selling** — plain selling would have capped the market and stopped it going up. After the SL hit the market went **down and up again**, so *"बायर बैठा होगा"* — a buyer was sitting there too. That is the analysis: **find where the buyers sit and where the sellers sit.**
- His summary: *"एसएल हंटिंग देखना है कि बायर कहां पे बैठे हैं, सेलर कहां पे बैठने — ये ढूंढ लेना, कामयाब हो जाओगे. किसी भी मार्केट में ढूंढ लेना."*

### Roadmap + patience (closing)
- You must have a **roadmap for money**: not *"पैसा लगाऊंगा, पैसा बन जाएगा"* — *"मुझे कुछ करना है मार्केट के अंदर, फिर पैसा बनेगा."*
- Time and patience are the price: *"टाइम तो देना पड़ेगा, मार्केट को पेशेंस तो रखना पड़ेगा, लेकिन मिलेगा ज़रूर."*

### Keep permanently
1. **In a live trade, see P&L as a NUMBER** — read the real result only at close; watching the running number is what feeds the emotion that breaks the trade.
2. **Love money before the trade, not during it** — the direction ("I will make money") needs the love; the operation needs the switch-off.
3. **Money management is the precondition for emotional strength** — if the feeling "my money is going" ever appears, you cannot work the market.
4. **Trade at the size you can stay stable at** — there is no fixed figure; 1 lakh or 10,000 depends on the person. Working above your stable level is the mistake.
5. **Grow in stages** (doctor analogy): small operations first, then bigger — never start at the top.
6. **Practice is the only route** — "sir told me today, I'll implement tomorrow" never happens; that ease is why the edge stays with the few.
7. **Learn candles at the TURNING POINTS** — the market builds its structure around the turn; mid-chart candles teach nothing.
8. **Practice on a BIGGER timeframe (5/10/15/30m), not 1-min** — 1-min shapes carry no value and can't be remembered; a bigger frame gives structure and grip.
9. **Learn momentum easiest-first:** 2–4-day momentum → middle momentum → 1-day momentum.
10. **SL hunting is a skill with a location:** find where buyers sit and where sellers sit — the operator builds his put-side position as buyers gather, then takes their SLs; if the market then moves both ways, a buyer is still sitting there.
11. **Have a roadmap** — "put money → money made" is not a plan; you must DO something in the market, then money is made.
