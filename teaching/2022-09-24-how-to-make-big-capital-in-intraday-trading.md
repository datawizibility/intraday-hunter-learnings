### Core idea
- Big capital is **not** produced by the entry. It is produced by **what the trader does with the money one
  trade happens to hand him**: *"पैसा बनने की शुरुआत हमेशा गलती से ही होती है"* — the money starts coming by
  accident, because the market sometimes runs far past the plan.
- **The two symmetric errors that cancel each other out:**
  - In a **loss** — hold, then average (*"अंग्रेजी कर लेते हैं"*), then exit at the big loss.
  - In a **profit** — the market runs, the first red candle appears, and you book and run: *"जहां पर इतना कोई
    आपने प्लान नहीं बनाया था कि मार्केट यहां तक चला जाएगा."*
  Net effect: you always absorb the full loss and always clip the accidental win. **That is why the process
  never compounds.**
- The session's real subject is therefore **profit management**, and its central claim is one sentence:
  **the money in your hand is not "the market's money".**

### The "market's money" fallacy (the load-bearing rule)
- His illustration, in rupees: you make **₹25,000** by about **11:00**. If you now think of it as *market ka
  paisa*, your fear is switched off — *"मार्केट का पैसा मार्केट में चला जाएगा तो कोई दिक्कत नहीं"* — and you
  take a random trade risking **₹3,000–4,000** to make ₹6,000–7,000.
- The random trade loses. Now you are at **₹21,000**. Regret → another risk → it goes lower still → you end
  up standing at **₹10,000**, which is exactly **yesterday's loss** — *"तो ज़ीरो ज़ीरो और बन गए."*
- **Fear and greed are a seesaw:** *"जहां डर खत्म हो जाता है वहां लालच आ जाता है, और जहां लालच खत्म हो जाता है
  वहां डर आ जाता है."* The ₹25,000 is what ended the fear; the greed that replaced it spent the money.
- **His correction:** *"जो भी आपके पास आता है वह अपनी मेहनत का पैसा होता है"* — you traded for it, you earned it;
  never trade money you hold **with greed and fear**, trade it with an open mind and only **when a setup
  forms**.

### Plan your trade — but only where the market's own plan matches
- He takes *"Plan your trade and trade your plan"* and adds the missing half: **execute your plan only when
  the market's own plan is visible in the chart.** *"गलती क्या करते — हम अपना तो प्लान बना लेते हैं, लेकिन
  मार्केट का क्या प्लान चल रहा है, उसको हम कंसीडर नहीं करते."*
- Worked rule for a **continuously selling** market: the two possible plays are *buy the dip* or *sell the
  rise* — and **neither is a trade yet**. You act only when the market gives you a **flat or gap opening**
  that matches your plan; you sell while the market is offering **gap-down or continuation**; and if the
  market opens unchanged and refuses to cross the reference level, then for that day you may go straight to
  the selling side — *\"उस दिन के लिए आप डायरेक्टली मार्केट के अंदर सेलिंग में जाओगे\"* — unless a separate
  setup appears.
- **Speed is conditional on alignment:** *"अगर मार्केट हमारे अकॉर्डिंग चल रहा है तो मैं फास्ट वहां पर एक्सीक्यूट
  करूंगा; पोज़िशन अगर नहीं चल रही है तो धीरे-धीरे अपना प्लान बनाऊंगा, कंफर्म के साथ ट्रेड करूंगा."*

### Give the profit a month — and give the quantity a leash
- **Time-box the profit, not just the trade:** *"मैं ये चाहता हूं, इसको कम से कम एक महीने तक लेकर जाना है."*
  His reasoning is explicitly anti-fragility: a month cannot be loss after loss; a month is
  loss-profit-loss-profit — so after a month the trader's own **stage changes** from *loss-loss* to
  *profit-loss-profit-loss*, *"माइंडसेट चेंज होगा, थोड़ा सा आपके अंदर कॉन्फिडेंस आएगा"*, and a more careful
  process begins. **Profit must be given enough time to change the operator, not just the balance.**
- **The quantity trap:** *"क्वांटिटी को अचानक से मत बढ़ाइये."* 1 lot → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5, and *"एक दिन आएगा, 5 लॉट
  में लॉस हो जाएगा, वो तीन-चार लॉट का सीधा लॉस लेस आएगा"* — a single 5-lot loss erases the 3–4-lot gains.
  Manage quantity, do not raise it because a good few days paid for it.

### Capital size is not the blocker
- *"काफी लोगों को लगता है सर हमारे पास कैपिटल काफी कम है, हम क्या बड़ा पैसा बना पाएंगे?"* — his answer is
  **skill first**: *"अगर आपको ट्रेडिंग करना आता है, मैं गारंटी देता हूं आपको पैसे की कभी कमी नहीं होगी."* But
  *"पहले पैसा बनाना पड़ेगा"* — you must already be able to produce a trade before capital has anything to
  multiply.
- **Fear is a required input, greed is not:**
  - **Small capital** → keep a **backup**, so fear does not become panic. *"चाहे आपके पास कैपिटल छोटा हो, तो आप
    अपना एक बैकअप रखिए ताकि आपको डर न लगे."*
  - **Big capital** → *"बड़े पैसे वाले क्या होते हैं? उनके पास डर नहीं होता, वह पैसा लगाने से डरता नहीं."*
  - **Too much of either is the failure** — *"न लालच ज्यादा, न डर ज्यादा"*; keep a **neutral** stance, because
    *"आपने अपनी स्ट्रेंथ को ज्यादा समझ लिया तो फंस जाओगे, कम समझ लिया तो फंस जाओगे."*
- *"मार्केट पूरा माइंड का गेम है"* — no physical work is done here; only your own judgment is being traded.

### Keep permanently
1. **Big money starts as an accident and is kept by management.** The trade that runs past your plan is the
   source of capital; how you treat its proceeds is the whole difference.
2. **Never call your profit "the market's money."** *"जो भी आपके पास आता है वह अपनी मेहनत का पैसा होता है"* —
   the moment it is free money, fear switches off and greed takes the seat.
3. **Fear and greed share one seat**: where fear ends greed arrives, and where greed ends fear returns. Aim
   for the **neutral** middle — too much of either and you are caught.
4. **Give profit a minimum holding period — at least one month.** A month of profit-loss-profit-loss changes
   the operator's *stage*; a single good day changes nothing.
5. **Execute your plan only where the market's own plan is visible.** *Your* plan alone is half the
   condition; in a one-way market, act only when the opening matches (flat / gap / continuation), otherwise
   wait for a setup.
6. **Never raise quantity suddenly.** 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 lots and one 5-lot loss erases the 3–4-lot gains.
   Size is a survival variable, not an expression of confidence.
7. **Capital size is not the blocker — skill is.** *"पहले ट्रेडिंग करना सीखना पड़ेगा, तब जाकर पैसा आएगा."*
8. **If capital is small, hold a backup so that fear does not become panic; if capital is large, the
   advantage is that fear is absent** — but never confuse absence of fear with licence.
9. **Speed follows alignment:** fast when the position is running your way, slow with confirmation when it is
   not.
10. **Emotion can be controlled, never deleted.** Work at reducing it; do not wait to feel nothing.
