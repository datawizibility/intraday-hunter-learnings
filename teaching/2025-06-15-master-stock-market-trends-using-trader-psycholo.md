### Core idea
- **Trend is downstream of psychology.** Get the psychology right and the trend stops being something you chase: *"क्योंकि अगर हमने साइकोलॉजी को सही से समझ लिया तो ट्रेंड तो आपके पीछेछे चलना शुरू कर देगा।"*
- **The market's question is a seating question, not a chart question** — *"बयर कहां पे बैठा है, सेलर कहां पे बैठा है"* — and the aggregate of it is *"ये टोटल मार्केट का साइकोलॉजी होता है जिसके अकॉर्डिंग मार्केट काम करता है।"*
- **A plan is a seat-map, not a prediction.** At an all-time-high breakout on positive news nobody had sold, so no seller stop-losses sat above it; the up-move therefore had no fuel, and the market had either to fall or to come back down to *create* those stop-losses. Both branches licence intraday selling.
- **The seated side is read mechanically** — off a close printed exactly at a level, off the expiry writers, and off how much momentum the turning point had already delivered.
- **Small turning-point momentum = the big trader is still sitting.** If the trader who turned the market never got a big momentum, he has not booked profit — expect continuation in his direction.
- **The mirror case flips the plan to buying:** once the whole buy side has been flushed and no buyer stop-losses are left below, the market has nowhere to go but up — buy a small gap-down or a flat-to-gap-up open. And the law behind it: *"जितने कम उतना पैसा ज्यादा यह मार्केट का रूल होता है।"*

### Psychology is the seating map
| Question the market asks | What it decides |
|---|---|
| Where is the buyer sitting? | which stop-losses are still available above/below |
| Where is the seller sitting? | which side the market has to travel to, to reach them |
| How will each side trade? | continuation vs reversal |

- Everything follows from one read — *"बयर कहां पे बैठा है, सेलर कहां पे बैठा है"* — and he calls the aggregate of it the market's whole psychology: *"ये टोटल मार्केट का साइकोलॉजी होता है जिसके अकॉर्डिंग मार्केट काम करता है।"*
- Why it pays: *"क्योंकि अगर हमने साइकोलॉजी को सही से समझ लिया तो ट्रेंड तो आपके पीछेछे चलना शुरू कर देगा।"* He had already written the sell plan while the chart still looked positive — *"हम पहले ही अपना प्लान बनाना शुरू कर दिए थे।"* — while others were only then noticing that a negative trend had arrived.
- Your own side is not the market's side: retail buys the gap-up and the news, and the market traps them and comes down — *"गैप अप वगैरह को देखकर न्यूज़ वगैरह को देखकर वो बाय करते हैं और मार्केट उनको ट्रैप करता हुआ नीचे आ जाता है।"*
- The rotation under a turning point: the small traders keep buying, the large ones bank the move and slowly build the opposite position — *"जो रिटेल ट्रेडर होते हैं और जो छोटे ट्रेडर होते हैं वो यहां पे बाय करते रह जाते हैं।"* / *"जो बड़े-बड़े ट्रेडर होते हैं वो अपना बेनिफिट उठाते हैं"*
- Behind all of it sits the crowd rule: *"क्योंकि जहां पे भीड़ कम वहीं पैसा ज्यादा।"* — the crowded side is the paying side.

### The all-time-high sell: positive news, nobody sold
- The chart: *"मार्केट ने ऑल टाइम हाई को क्रॉस कर दिया था।"* on a big momentum candle, with the news also positive — *"तो पॉजिटिव न्यूज़ है। मार्केट ने ब्रेकआउट कर दिया।"*
- The inference he asks you to make: nobody sold into that breakout — *"कोई भी यहां पे सेल करके नहीं गया होगा"* — and if nobody sold, *"या तो मार्केट को गिरना पड़ेगा या फिर वह एसएल क्रिएट करने के लिए नीचे आना पड़ेगा।"*, so *"दोनों ही कंडीशन में इंट्राडे में सेलिंग किया जा सकता है।"*
- In the rule's own compressed form: *"यहां पे सेलर के एसएल नहीं है।"* ⇒ a little positive momentum can come, but it cannot hold — *"मार्केट को गिरना ही पड़ता है।"*
- The following day (a big gap-up that held above the all-time high) repeated the same read, because above an all-time high it is the buyers who carry the risk — *"तो बयर ने यहां पे रिस्क ज्यादा लिया हुआ है।"*
- He answers the obvious objection himself — sometimes such a market keeps running: on fresh negative news, or on a multi-day one-way momentum the trader talks himself into selling — *"ट्रेडर को ऐसा लगना शुरू हो जाता है कि चार से पांच दिन लगातार ऊपर जा रहा है।"*
- Expiry in Sensex gave a second, independent seat: *"ससेक्स में क्या था? उस दिन एक्सपायरी था।"*, with the put writers already paid — *"पुट राइटर तो ऑलरेडी पैसा बना चुके हैं"* — and the call writers still seated — *"अब यहां पे कॉल राइटर बैठे हैं। तो मार्केट और ऊपर नहीं जाने वाला।"* — so the market has no reason to go anywhere, it merely pins itself in the middle, and the next day's plan is selling.
- **The third seat, read off a close (Bank Nifty).** The read is not that buyers are absent and sellers are present — it is comparative: the market had taken support exactly at its own closing price — *"जो मार्केट ने क्लोज किया है एग्जैक्ट ही क्लोजिंग प्राइस का सपोर्ट लिया है।"* — *"इवन यहां पे क्या है? 56,500 का लेवल है।"* — so the buy side was the bigger position — *"लेकिन बयर ने ज्यादा पोर्शन बनाया होगा क्योंकि अपसाइड का मोमेंटम हुआ, रिट्रेसमेंट हुआ, सपोर्ट के आसपास मार्केट खड़ा है तो बायर यहां पे ज्यादा बैठे होंगे।"* Sellers had come too (there had been negative momentum); the point is only that the buyers held the larger position, so the next day's plan targets them.

### Small momentum ⇒ the big trader is still sitting
- After a turning point he asks who is sitting on the other side and how fast the market was turned — *"सेलर बैठा कौन है"* — and reads the answer as *"मार्केट को और गिराने वाला है"*
- The mechanism: the big trader who turned the market has not booked profit, because the momentum was never big — *"जो भी यहां पे बड़ा ट्रेडर बैठा होगा जिसने मार्केट को घुमाया है वह अभी भी अपना प्रॉफिट बुक करके नहीं गया होगा"* ⇐ *"क्योंकि मोमेंटम भी ज्यादा नहीं हुआ है तो इसने यहां पे प्रॉफिट भी बुकिंग नहीं किया हुआ"*
- His own execution on that read: the put built in the falling market took a loss in the retracement first — *"लेकिन रिट्रेसमेंट में मेरा लॉस हो गया था।"* — and he files it as *"कोई प्रॉब्लम वाली बात नहीं है।"* … *"हमें चाहिए कि हम डायरेक्शन के अकॉर्डिंग गलत ना हो।"* Once the operator started putting money in, the market went into continuous selling and the momentum was captured.
- Two sizing rules he attaches: intraday target = the size of your stop-loss — *"जितना एसएल होता है उतना ही आपका टारगेट होना चाहिए"* — and an intraday trade must be booked as an intraday trade — *"अगर आप इंट्राडे में ट्रेड करने के लिए आए हो तो इंट्राडे के अकॉर्डिंग प्रॉफिट बुक करना चाहिए।"*

### The flush and its mirror — no buyer SLs left below ⇒ the buy plan
- After the fall, the entire buy side is gone — *"मार्केट में जितने भी बायर थे उनको कंप्लीटली निकाल चुका है।"* — and that itself builds the next psychology: *"अब क्योंकि मार्केट इतना गिर चुका है तो यहां पे साइकोलॉजी क्या बनेगा? दूसरे लोग भी सेल करना शुरू कर देंगे।"*
- So the mirror of the all-time-high case: with nothing left below for the market to hunt, *"यहां पे बायर के एसएल नहीं है तो मार्केट को क्या करना पड़ेगा? ऊपर जाना पड़ेगा।"*
- Hence the plan, which he says is frightening to write into a falling market — *"छोटा गैप डाउन ओपन होता है, फ्लैट टू गैप अप ओपन होता है तो वहां पे हम बाइंग का प्लान बना सकते हैं।"* — *"मुझे बड़ा डर लगता है कि मार्केट गिर रहा है। यहां पे हम बाइंग का प्लान कैसे बना सकते हैं।"*
- The market then opened a bigger gap-down than the plan allowed, and he says plainly that such a chart is no longer useable (next section).

### Gap distance decides whether the chart is still usable
- The threshold he states is a distance, not a level — *"300 400 पॉइंट का ज्यादा गैप डाउन ओपन हो जाए"* — and the reason is who was already sitting: the seated sellers are deep in profit — *"जो ऑलरेडी सेल वगैरह करके बैठे होते हैं वो अच्छे प्रॉफिट में आ जाते हैं।"* — while the buyers are already trapped — *"जो बाय करके बैठे होते हैं वो ऑलरेडी ट्रैप हो जाते हैं।"*
- Verdict: the chart is finished — *"तो ये चार्ट हमारे कोई काम का रहता नहीं।"* — and *"फिर हमें नए चार्ट को ही फॉलो करना होता है।"*
- Discipline of the whole session, stated as he writes plans that scare him: *"लेकिन जो रूल है उनको हमें फॉलो करना ही होगा।"*

### Keep permanently
1. **Read the market as seating** — where is the buyer, where is the seller, how will each trade. Everything else (trend, plan, target) is downstream of that one read.
2. **A level crossed with nobody seated on the far side has no fuel.** If no seller sold into the breakout, then either the market falls or it comes down to *create* those stop-losses — and both branches licence selling in the direction of the fall.
3. **Positive news plus a clean breakout is a seat-map, not a buy signal.** Use it to find where the crowd has parked, then trade the market's reaction to it.
4. **Read the seated side off a close printed at a level** (or off a level that was defended): a close exactly at support leaves the buy side holding the larger position — so the next day's plan targets *them*.
5. **On expiry, follow the writers.** Once one writer camp has been paid, the camp still seated tells you the market will not travel that way — sell, or expect the pin.
6. **Momentum size identifies who is still in the market.** A turning point with no big momentum means the trader who turned it has not booked profit — expect continuation in his direction (and, per 2025-06-08, the losing side merely *holding* rather than being flushed).
7. **The absences are the plan.** Find the side where no stop-losses remain: no seller SLs above ⇒ the market cannot hold up; no buyer SLs below ⇒ the market has nowhere to go but up.
8. **A right direction can still trade as a loss.** A retracement inside a running trend is expected; a loss there is not a reason to abandon a correct direction, only a reason to keep the direction right.
9. **Intraday sizing: target = your stop-loss.** Do not sit through an intraday trade waiting for a positional target; book it as an intraday trade.
10. **Gap distance is a chart-validity test.** A 300–400 pt (or larger) gap against the seated side puts that side deep in profit and the other side in the trap — the old plan is void; follow a new chart.
11. **Less crowd, more money** — write the plan on the side with fewer people in it.
12. **Follow the rule even when it frightens you:** the plan at an all-time high, or a buy plan in a falling market, is exactly the moment the rule is being tested.
