### Core idea
- **One chart, two indicators, two jobs.** He builds a single setup out of **RSI** (the relative strength index — heard as *"आरएसआई आगरा सही होता है रिलेटिव्स फ्रेंड्स"* ⚠️) and **VWAP** (the price line — heard as *"आपको Web तो व्यक्ति आप इसमें क्लिक कर देना"* ⚠️), and divides the labour: **RSI decides *when*, VWAP decides *at what price***. RSI is read only to find the day's **first breakout**; once that breakout has happened RSI is finished and only the chart and the VWAP value are read.
- The session is deliberately **mechanical**. The settings are typed on screen, the entry line is the VWAP value **plus a buffer he adds himself**, and at the end he states the whole point — no price-action read is required, only **belief in the indicator and the system**.
- Three rules are laid down **before** the method: **do not trade the first three candles**; the earlier the entry, the better (**before 11:00 / 11:30**); and **exactly one trade a day** — which he says is what builds discipline.
- The arithmetic is fixed in rupees: **₹5 stop against a ₹10 target — 1:2**.
- Two dated charts are walked: **4 February** (the worked trade) and **3 February** (a breakout that arrived after **12:00** and was refused outright).

### The chart: RSI finds the moment, VWAP gives the price
- Setup as he performs it: open the indicators section, add **RSI**, add **VWAP**; both then sit on the chart. He changes the settings himself so the viewer can follow.
- **RSI settings:** the first field (period/length) is set to a value heard as **6**, with the default described as the normal figure (heard as **7414**, read as the default **14**). ⚠️ The second field's value is heard only as *"क्वालिटी"* and is **not recoverable**; two further digits in this passage (**6840**, **7414**) are indicator-setting values, unlabelled, and are **not reconstructed**. The remaining edits are cosmetic — he switches lines to **white** because he uses a black theme, and explicitly says the viewer does not need it.
- The read order is one line: *"फर्स्ट थ्री कंट्रोल होंगे जो मार्किट कि उसमें आपको ट्रेंड नहीं करना है"* ⚠️ — the ASR word *"कंट्रोल"* is **candle**. The first three candles are not tradeable.
- Where the moment comes from: *"पहले देखना होगा कि आरएसएस में पहली बार कहां पर एकजुट हो रहा है"* ⚠️ — ASR *"आरएसएस"* is **RSI**, *"एकजुट"* is **breakout**. He wants the **first** RSI breakout of the day and, specifically, **where** it happened.
- Then RSI is dropped: *"तो आपको आरएसी का कुछ लेना देना पिछले गाने को"* ⚠️ — the trailing words are ASR noise; the sense is that RSI is no longer needed — *"फिर आपको केवल चार्ट में देखना"*. From the breakout on, he reads only the chart and the VWAP value.
- If the RSI breakout falls inside the first three candles, the day is still not tradeable there: *"तो आपको फर्स्ट थ्री मंडल में ट्रेंड नहीं करना है चाहे कुछ भी हो जाए"* — whatever the sentiment, no entry inside those candles.

### A breakout must be visible — a touch is never a confirmation
- The breakout itself carries a quality gate. Four shapes are allowed: *"तो यह चार तरीके से आप इसमें ब्रेकफास्ट कंसीडर या ब्रेड टो कंसीडर कर सकते हैं"* ⚠️ — RSI crossing up from below, down from above, or leaving the middle in either direction.
- The print must be unmistakable: *"ऐसा बर्ताव करना चाहिए जो क्लियर नजर आए"*. A breakout he has to argue about is not taken, because confusion there becomes a problem later.
- A candle that merely *touches* the RSI line is not confirmation: *"दशरथ कराओ टच करने वाले को कंफर्म नहीं करेंगे"* ⚠️ (the leading words are ASR noise) — because at the moment of the touch nobody can know whether the breakout is real.
- The test he gives is visual: *"प्रॉपर आपको आंखों से नजर आए अंदाजा न लगाना पड़े"* — you must be able to **see** that the breakout happened, not estimate it.

### Entry on the VWAP side, with points you add yourself
- When to enter is answered by the VWAP line: *"पहली बात तो यह देखने की आरसी में कब और कहां पहली बार ब्रेकआउट ब्रेड है"* ⚠️, then *"विवेक की वैल्यू आपको नजर आएगी क्या नजर आ रही है"* — the VWAP value is read straight off the chart (ASR *"विवेक"* = VWAP).
- On the worked example the value he reads out is heard as **179 पॉइंट पांच चार**; he then does something he says is his own edit: *"तो इसमें आपको जस्ट कुछ पॉइंट्स ऐड कर देने अपनी तरफ से"* — **the entry line is VWAP plus a buffer of your own points**, so the order does not sit exactly on the average.
- The trigger is the side, not the touch: *"वेब के ऊपर प्राइस चला गया है फिर मैं ट्रैक को यहां पर इनिशियट करूंगा"* — once price is above the VWAP line the trade is initiated.
- On the **3 February** chart the first breakout was ignored because price had already run and did not come back: *"यहां पर देखिए आपको फर्स्ट कई घंटे को छोड़ देना ब्रेक और देखना है"* ⚠️ (ASR *"घंटे"* = **candles**), and again on the later chart *"तब आपको थ्री कैंडल छोड़ देना है उसके बाद आपको आराम से देखना है"* — skip the first candles, then read the print calmly.
- A stopped-out trade is simply left: *"मान लेते हैं आप स्टॉलों है वैसे भी हम वन बाय टू टारगेट स्क्रिप्ट लिखने को चिंता वाली बात है नहीं"* ⚠️ (ASR *"स्टॉलों"* = **stop-loss**) — with a 1:2 package already fixed, one stop-out is not a worry.

### The fixed arithmetic: a ₹5 stop against a ₹10 target
- Stated plainly on the example: *"कि आपके लिए मैं ₹5 कर रखा हूं ₹10 का टारगेट रखा हूं"* — ₹5 risked for ₹10. He then says the ratio is kept at **1:2** even when there is a loss to absorb: *"कुछ लौंग वगैरह भी हो मुझे तो तो मैं वन बाय टू यहां पर रिस्क रिवाज रखूंगा"* ⚠️ (ASR *"लौंग"* ≈ **loss**).
- What the fixed package buys, in his own words: *"वो और आपको कोई ज्यादा इसमें कोई प्राइस जैकसन रीड करने की आवश्यकता नहीं थी"* ⚠️ (ASR *"जैकसन"* = **action**) — no price-action read was needed, and nothing else on the market had to be watched.
- The stance behind it: *"केवल आपको अपने इंडिकेटर पर बिलीव होना चाहिए अपने सिस्टम पर बली होना चाहिए"* — belief in your own indicator and system, explicitly offered as a **substitute** for reading the market.
- And the check he leaves with the viewer: *"साथ ही साथ यह क्या करेंगे बैक टेस्ट भी कर लेंगे"*, followed by *"देखिए सिस्टम कैसा लगता है आपको"* — backtest it and judge the system for yourself.

### The clock: three candles, before 11:00–11:30, and a 12:00 hard stop
- The day's best window, spoken as a rule: *"वह इलेवन और 1130 से पहले जितना होगा उतना ही आपके प्रॉफिट होने के चांसेस ज्यादा रहेंगे"* — the earlier the entry, the better the chances.
- The trade cap is a flat **one**, whatever the setup: *"नो मैटर आपको केवल एक ही ट्रेड करनी है वह यही आप में डिसिप्लिन बनाएगा"* — and he calls that the discipline-builder.
- The hard cut-off: *"12:00 के बाद का जो प्रॉब्लम कंसीडर्ड नहीं करेंगे"* ⚠️ (ASR *"प्रॉब्लम"* = **breakout**) — a breakout arriving after 12:00 is not taken. On the 3 February chart exactly that happened, and the whole day was written off: *"अदरवाइज अगर यह 12 बजे से पहले-पहले होता तो हम क्या करते हैं आप इसे लिक्विड उठा सकते थे"* ⚠️ (ASR *"लिक्विड"* — the sense is *you could have taken it*) — before 12:00 the trade would have been available; after 12:00 it is not.
- Closing the last chart example, the stop survived: *"तो वहां पर हिट हुआ नहीं इंजॉय कीजिए आपको अपना प्रॉफिट"* — the trade was in profit, not stopped.

### Keep permanently
1. **Split the inputs by job, not by count.** One input decides **when** the setup exists; another decides **at what price** it is entered. Two tools answering the same question is redundancy; two tools answering different questions is a system.
2. **Skip the first three candles.** The opening print belongs to nobody — the readable structure starts after it, and an RSI breakout printed inside those candles is not tradeable even if the sentiment is strong.
3. **A breakout you cannot see at a glance is not a breakout.** A candle that only **touches** the line is never confirmation — if you have to argue with the chart about whether it broke, the answer is no.
4. **Once the trigger indicator has spoken, stop reading it.** After the first breakout, the indicator's job is finished; the chart and the entry line are the only things left to read.
5. **Set the entry line off the average, not on it.** Take the VWAP value, add your own buffer points, and enter only once price is on the correct side of that line — you are avoiding the exact print where everyone else orders.
6. **Fix the exit arithmetic before the entry and then leave it alone.** ₹5 against ₹10 (1:2) held even when it costs trades; a stopped-out trade is left, not chased.
7. **One trade a day, and take it early.** Whatever the setup, one trade, and the earlier it comes (best before 11:00–11:30) the better the odds; **nothing after 12:00** counts at all.
8. **Backtest the setup yourself before you trust it.** He says he will backtest it and tells the viewer to judge the system — the tool is not to be believed on his word alone.
