### Core idea
- One question, asked four ways at the top: at a level, will the market **break and continue**, or **break and come back** (a fake break)? The whole session is the *test* for that question.
- **Direction comes first.** He calls it the **रूट वैल्यू** — *"मैं कोशिश करूंगा कि कम से कम शब्दों में"* … *"रूट वैल्यू क्या होती है"* — the read of which way the market wants to work. Without it, no technique helps; with it, the technique decides the entry.
- ⭐ **The test is not how many times the level was tested.** That is the beginner's error and he names it outright: *"आप यह भूल जाइए कि उसने कितनी बार टेस्ट किया या नहीं किया"*. A logical method's success rate should **rise** as years of screen time accumulate; a test-counting method leaves you making the same guess after three years.
- ⭐ **The real test: was a POSITION built before the break?**
  - Position built, then the break → **continuation** (a real breakout): *"अगर मार्केट पोजीशन बनाकर अपसेट जा रहा है तो यह कंटिन्यू करने वाला है"*.
  - A move up with **no position built** → **fake breakout**: *"अगर पोजीशन बनाकर विदाउट पोषण बनाकर मार्केट ऊपर जा रहा है यह फेंक ब्रेकआउट"*. The mirror is stated the same way for support: *"ऐसी सिचुएशन में यह फेंक ब्रेक डाउन होने वाला है"*.
- ⭐ **Cross-check the break on the component, not on the index alone.** To know whether an index breakout will work, open a constituent: for Bank Nifty that is HDFC Bank / Kotak Bank / ICICI Bank — *"बैंक निफ्टी के कौन से कम पड़ते एचडीएफसी बैंक हो गया कोटक बैंक हो गया आईसीआई बैंक हो गया"* — and read what is happening there. If the component has already delivered the breakout, the index break works: *"एचडीएफसी बैंक का जो रेजिस्टेंस लेगा मार्किट वह काम करेगा"*.
- ⭐ **All three agreeing raises the odds.** When Bank Nifty, HDFC Bank and Nifty break together, the possibility of a genuine conversion / return is higher: *"जब तीनों में एक साथ होने लगा दो काम हो तो वहां पर कंवर्ट होने का या मार्किट में रिटर्न होने का पॉसिबिलिटी ज्यादा होता है"*.
- He trades **Bank Nifty and Nifty** (*"मैं बैंक निफ्टी और निफ्टी में काम करता हूं"*), but says the method is not index-bound: *"आप स्पोर्ट्स वगैरह के अंदर भी काम मार्केट के अंदर कर सकते हो"* ⚠️ (`स्पोर्ट्स` = stocks; ASR damage).

### The position test (how he separates continuation from a fake break)
- Setup the question plainly: at a resistance, ask whether a position was built below it before the push — *"जब मार्किट में यहां पर रजिस्टेंस बना हुआ है क्या इससे पहले से लिंग साइड कि यहां पर पोजीशन बनी है नहीं बनी"*.
- The short form of the rule: *"अगर डाइरैक्टली दे रहा होगा एक रिकॉर्ड होगा अगर बनाकर वह कंटिन्यू करेगा"* — a direct, position-less break is the record of a fake; a break after building holds the continuation.
- First-touch vs repeated-touch, as he frames the level itself: *"इसी तरह डिस्टेंस के लिए कि अगर मार्केट फर्स्ट टाइम किसी रजिस्टेंस को Touch करें तो"* — a first touch can give a reaction, a break that has been absorbed repeats.
- Order of work is explicit: *"डायरेक्शन को पहचानिए पहले आपको यह देखना है"*.
- ⚠️ The one thing he says cannot be shortcut: reading "position built / not built" off the chart is not learnable on day one — *"जब आप इसी तरीके से एक साल दो साल काम करोगे तो धीरे-धीरे आपको एक्सपीरियंस आ जाएगा"*. It is screen time, not a setting.
- Why he keeps the method logical rather than situational: a rule that only works "today" cannot compound — *"मार्केट के अंदर 90% काम करती हो"* is his standard for a rule worth keeping.

### The component read (index break vs constituent break)
- Concrete worked case in the clip: HDFC Bank is opened at the 09:16 mark, and the read is that HDFC Bank has **already given the breakout**, so Bank Nifty's resistance will be taken, not rejected — *"एचडीएफसी बैंक का जो रेजिस्टेंस लेगा मार्किट वह काम करेगा"*.
- The failure case is the mirror: when the **component** is left taking support at its own resistance while the index looks strong, the index support does not hold — *"यह सपोर्ट काम करेगा नहीं करेगा"* … *"बैंक निफ्टी बदनाम हो रहा है निफ्टी में अपना सुकून दे रहा है"* (a broken Bank Nifty drags the read down even while Nifty looks comfortable).
- The same cross-read runs the other way: if HDFC Bank has given the break and Nifty is still sitting at support, the resistance gets cleared and the lagging index follows.
- He repeats the instruction to check the pair rather than the single chart: *"आपको क्या के देखना है निफ्टी को देखना है"* and, for the mirror, *"बैंक मिट्टी में जाकर कि बैंक क्या करें"*.

### Why the entry can be skipped — the payoff of the test
- The break at 09:31 is read against the component, the fake is identified, and the entry is not taken; the benefit is stated as an avoided mistake: *"एक एंट्री गलत होने से बच गया"*.
- The alternative — taking the breakout because the level was tested many times — is the version that cannot improve with experience, which is why the test-count rule is discarded at the top of the session.

### Keep permanently
1. **Get direction (रूट वैल्यू) before any technique.** A tool without a direction is a guess; the direction is what lets a rule be written on the tools.
2. **Never judge a level by how many times it was tested.** Test-counting cannot improve with experience; a logical rule's success rate should rise as screen time accumulates.
3. **The break test is the position test.** Position built, then break → continuation. A break with no position built underneath → fake breakout (mirror for breakdowns).
4. **Never trade the index alone — confirm the break on a constituent.** Read the component (for Bank Nifty, HDFC Bank / Kotak Bank / ICICI Bank). If the component has already broken, the index break works; if the component is stuck taking support at its own resistance, the index break fails.
5. **All three agreeing is a probability, not a guarantee.** Bank Nifty + HDFC Bank + Nifty breaking together raises the odds of a genuine conversion; the third still has to be read, not assumed.
6. **A lagging index follows a leading one.** If one index has already broken and the other is still sitting at the level, the level gets cleared rather than rejected.
7. **Skipping a bad entry is a result.** The test's value is stated as the entry not taken — *"एक एंट्री गलत होने से बच गया"* — not as a bigger win.
8. **Reading position-built-or-not is earned, not configured.** It takes one to two years of the same frame before the read starts to appear; budget for that time instead of hunting a setting.
9. **Keep a rule only if it can work ~90% of the time.** A rule that only explains today's tape is not a rule; it is a story.
10. **The method is instrument-agnostic.** Indices are where he works, but the position/component/convergence test carries to stocks.
