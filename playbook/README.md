## Trap reading (weekly: 08-17→21)
- Breakout close WITHOUT follow-through momentum = trap → gap-up is a temptation to sell the trapped buyers.
- Recovery after a breakdown = breakout crowd trapped → follow market (buy).
- Double bottom after a fast down-move is often a trap to keep sellers out (continuation), not a reversal.

## Option-writer flushing sequence (new — 08-20)
- When option writers dominate (no momentum, sideways), market first flushes one side's writers (premiums spike, SLs hit).
- Whoever gets flushed first determines the next target: put writers flushed → market targets call writers → buy side works.

## No-trend / no-momentum days (new — 08-19)
- No trend + no momentum = hold trades don't work; only follow the market intraday. Don't force.
- Flat open + slightly negative structure → sellers may be sitting → be ready for sell; positive → buy.

## Size & risk adjustments (new — 08-17)
- Cut size when weekly-expiry / slow-momentum risk rises (e.g. 840 vs 1170 on Bank Nifty puts).
- Open trades pay better but punish undisciplined traders fast (5-10 min). Discipline or don't take the risk.
